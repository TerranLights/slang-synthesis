## Grammar points

### Eye-dialect contraction marking spoken/child register ("me'" for "med")
Lindgren spells Karlsson's recurring *jag är inte med* as *jag är inte me'* (p. 108, p. 129),
dropping the final -d in print specifically to flag casual spoken delivery — a deliberate
orthographic device, not a typo, and worth tracking as a marker this corpus uses consistently for
one character's voice rather than narration generally.

### Interjections functioning as complete conversational turns
Several entries above (*Hejsan hoppsan!*, *Kör till!*, *Jaså*, *Lugn, bara lugn*) stand alone as
entire lines of dialogue with no accompanying clause — typical of this book's dialogue-heavy style,
where a one- or two-word exclamation routinely carries a full conversational beat on its own.

### "världens bästa [agent noun]" as a productive self-praise frame
Not a single fixed idiom but a reusable syntactic slot (*världens bästa* + any newly invented
occupation/role noun) that recurs at least five times across the sampled pages, always in Karlsson's
own mouth, each time with a fresh noun. Worth flagging for the mechanics-analysis phase as a
genuinely productive comic construction rather than a one-off phrase.

### Reported-speech word order ("sa [NAME]" postposed)
Consistent with standard Swedish dialogue convention, the attribution tag regularly follows the
quoted clause with inverted subject-verb order dropped to plain *sa [Name]* (e.g. "sa Lillebror,"
"sa mamma," "sa Karlsson") rather than "Lillebror sa." This is standard, not slang, but is the
default narrative rhythm underlying nearly every dialogue exchange in the sample and is worth noting
as the baseline against which the register-marked items above stand out.

### Lexical blending as a hesitation marker ("Nja")
*Nja* (p. 129) is a genuine ja+nej portmanteau rather than a reduced form of either word alone —
worth distinguishing from ordinary hedging adverbs ("kanske," "typ") as a case of morphological
blending used for a specific pragmatic function (noncommittal hesitation).
