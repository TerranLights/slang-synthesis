**Morpheme breakdown (illustrative examples).**

> **Isama** = i- (instrumental/conveyance-trigger prefix) + sama ("join/accompany") — "to embody,
> incorporate (something into something else)." The i- trigger marks the thing being moved/applied
> as the grammatical focus, distinct from the actor-trigger forms seen throughout `004`.

> **Mákinikinita** = ma- (potentive prefix) + reduplicated root ki- + kita ("see") — "to be able to
> foresee." The reduplication here marks a future/incompletive-leaning potentive reading ("coming to
> be seen/perceived") rather than simple past ability.

> **Nang̃ang̃ailang̃an** = nang- (actor-trigger prefix allomorph of mang-, here appearing with full
> reduplication of the nasal-initial syllable) + ailang̃an ("need") — "needy, in need." Shows the
> same mang-/nang- AT prefix family already seen in `004` combining with reduplication to derive an
> adjective rather than a plain verb.

## Grammar/orthography points

No new orthography points beyond `004`'s write-up (same `ng̃` digraph and accent-mark conventions
throughout this part of the dictionary). One additional trigger/focus pattern not yet seen in
`004`'s sample is worth recording:

### Nominalized actor-focus relative clauses (`nag-`/`ang nag-...` constructions)

Several entries in this sample gloss an English agent noun ("learner," "donor") not with a
dedicated agentive-affix noun but with a full relative-clause phrase built on the `nag-` (completed/
habitual actor-trigger) verb form: **ang nag-aaral** ("the one who studies" = learner), **ang
nagkakaloob, ang nagbibigay** ("the one who gives" = donor, in `004`). This is a distinct strategy
from the `taga-` agentive-noun pattern recorded in `004` (tagapang̃asiwà, tagapaglaan) — both
strategies coexist in the source for deriving "one who does X" nouns, and which one a given entry
uses appears to be lexically determined (headword-by-headword) rather than predictable from the
verb's trigger class alone. Worth tracking further once a dedicated grammar reference is extracted,
to see whether the two patterns carry a genuine register/productivity distinction or are simply
free variants.
