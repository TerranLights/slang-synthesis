## Grammar points

*(No new grammar rules are introduced in this appendix beyond what the main lesson sequence,
`007`–`007h`, and the idiom/expression appendices, `007i`–`007j`, already document — this is a pure
reference glossary. A handful of entries above extend already-documented productive patterns with new
worked examples — the **taga-** agentive pattern, the **wala-+-noun** compound-negation pattern, and the
**ni-** irregular-allomorph object-trigger pattern — cross-referenced in each relevant row's Notes
column above rather than re-explained here.)*

**A genuinely new finding worth flagging explicitly**: this glossary pass surfaced **three
structurally distinct intensification/figurative strategies converging on similar meanings** that
the main lesson sequence's own grammar points didn't have occasion to contrast directly: (1) full
lexical reduplication (documented extensively across Lessons 1/11/22/34/44); (2) idiom-template
reuse of a single body-part frame ("broken head" yielding both **sirâ-úlo** "crazy" and **basag-ulo**
"fight," depending on the specific co-occurring verb); and (3) the **ubod ng [quality-noun]**
"essence/core of X" intensive-construction template. This three-way variety of intensification
strategies — morphological reduplication, idiom-template reuse, and a dedicated "essence of" syntactic
frame — is itself a useful finding for the project's later Phase 3 mechanics-analysis work on how this
language marks degree/intensity.
