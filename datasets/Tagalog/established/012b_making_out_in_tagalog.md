## Grammar points

### Reduplication as an intensity marker (recurring pattern across this chunk)
Multiple entries in this chunk show full-word reduplication used productively to intensify meaning:
*durog na durog* ("totally crushed," i.e. "wiped out/stoned," from *durog* "crushed"), *yamot-na-yamot*
("bored to death," from *yamot* "annoyed"), echoing *totoong-totoo*/*talagang-talaga* from the
Introduction/Ch.1 chunk. This looks like a genuinely productive intensification strategy across
registers (not confined to slang), worth treating as a named mechanism in the mechanics-analysis
phase (pp.50, 51).

### Curse-register intensity ladder (Lintik family)
*Lintik!* (bare), *Lintik talaga!* (intensified with *talaga* "really/truly"), and *Tinamaan ka ng
magaling!* (an elaborated ironic variant) all draw on the same "struck by lightning" curse image at
escalating levels of force — a within-family intensity ladder distinct from the *Anak ka ng...*
family's ladder, which instead varies by *which relative* is invoked (pp.54–55).

### ari as a gender-neutral anatomical placeholder
*ari* ("thing/organ") is used as a non-gendered base term for genitalia, disambiguated by an
appended *ng lalaki* ("of a male") or *ng babae* ("of a female") when the gender needs to be explicit
— first seen in the insult *Maliit ang ari mo!* (p.55) and developed further in Chapter 12's intimate
vocabulary (see `012c_making_out_in_tagalog.md`).

### Translation-register inflation (a pattern to watch across the whole book)
At least one entry in this chunk (*Napakasama ang lasa*, "the taste is very bad") is given a markedly
more vulgar English gloss ("It tastes like shit") than the Tagalog itself actually carries, and
*Landi* (lit. "flirt/flirtatious") is glossed as the vulgar "Bitch!" despite being a comparatively mild
native disapproval term. Both are tagged in the Vocabulary table per the book's own framing of
intensity/force, but the underlying literal meaning is noted so this doesn't get mistaken for a
literal one-to-one taboo mapping in the mechanics-analysis phase.
