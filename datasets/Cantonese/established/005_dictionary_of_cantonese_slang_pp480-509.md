# A Dictionary of Cantonese Slang (Hutton & Bolton) — PDF pp.480-509

**Source.** *A Dictionary of Cantonese Slang*, Christopher Hutton and Kingsley Bolton.
Source file: `09.A Dictionary of Cantonese Slang.pdf` (509 PDF pages total, image-only scan,
no text layer). This shard covers PDF pages 480-509, rendered at 150 DPI and read with vision
(no OCR/transcript available). Every page was checked for handwritten marginalia first; none
was found — all content below is printed text only.

**What this range actually contains.** This is the tail end of the book, and it is almost
entirely back matter, not dictionary content:

- **PDF p.480 (printed p.463)** — the final page of the dictionary body proper: the tail of
  the 冤 (yūn) entry family plus three new headwords (丸 yún, 擁 yúng, 湧 yúng) with their
  slang sub-entries. **This is the only page in the assigned range with extractable
  dictionary entries**, tabled below.
- **PDF pp.481-498 (printed pp.464-481)** — a Chinese-character **radical/stroke-count INDEX**
  mapping headword characters to their Yale romanization only (no glosses, no definitions,
  no example sentences). Not extractable as vocabulary data.
- **PDF pp.499-508 (printed pp.482-491)** — the **REFERENCES** section, an alphabetical
  bibliography of academic/lexicographic sources (English and Chinese-language works on
  Cantonese, triad argot, slang dictionaries of other languages, etc.). No slang terms.
- **PDF p.509 (printed p.492, the final page of the book)** — **"Abbreviation for Sources,"**
  a key mapping the parenthetical source-citation codes used throughout the dictionary body
  (e.g. `snch`, `fmyy`, seen in the p.463 entries below) to the magazines/comics/newspapers
  they were drawn from. Also not a vocabulary table, so not tabled, but confirms the
  dictionary's citations are drawn from HK tabloid press, comics, and entertainment weeklies.

Given the above, this shard's table is short by design — it faithfully reflects that pp.481-509
hold no further headwords, rather than under-extracting available content.
