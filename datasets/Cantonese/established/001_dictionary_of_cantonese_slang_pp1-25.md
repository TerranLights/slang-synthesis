**Source:** `/media/kuroskalacs/Home-Theater/w-lang/Cantonese/09.A Dictionary of Cantonese Slang.pdf`
(Christopher Hutton & Kingsley Bolton, *A Dictionary of Cantonese Slang: The Language of Hong Kong
Movies, Street Gangs and City Life*). This PDF has no text layer (image-only scan, 509 pages total);
all content below was read via vision on 150dpi page renders of PDF pages 1-25.

PDF pp.1-25 cover: front cover (p.1); Foreword by Ip Pau-Fuk, retired HK Police Chief Inspector
(p.2, book p.vii); Preface (pp.3-7, book pp.x-xiv) describing the dictionary's aim, methodology,
sourcing (informants, HK comics/newspapers/films depicting triad street gangs, "OS" = official-source
lists), and its explicit stance that obscene/vulgar/triad items are included faithfully, not
sanitized; further front matter (pp.8-14, 16, 18 — Acknowledgements, continued Guide to Entries,
abbreviations/sources lists — skimmed, no extractable vocabulary); Guide to Entries excerpts
(pp.15,17, book pp.xxi-xxiii) explaining the Yale romanization/IPA correspondence and entry-format
conventions (romanization — Chinese characters, incl. invented/"chorphan" characters for words with
no standard character — optional literal gloss in brackets — English gloss(es), semicolon-separated
for distinct senses — citation codes for source/page). Actual dictionary entries begin on PDF p.19
(book p.1, headword **āa**) and run through PDF p.25 (book p.7, headword **báa pei**), covering the
full **A** section into the start of **B**. One illustrative example cited in the Preface itself
(**néih fèih jó**, used to argue that a plain-sounding insult can out-wound a string of swear-words)
is also recorded below, flagged as a Preface citation rather than a body entry.

The dictionary explicitly documents Hong Kong Cantonese triad/street/film slang, including obscene
items; taboo vocabulary is recorded faithfully per the project's standing convention — not sanitized.
Geographic Scope is filled as "Hong Kong" uniformly below because the dictionary's own title and
Preface state this as the whole work's explicit scope; Attested Era/Region are left "—" unless an
individual entry states one itself.
