front-of-alphabet skew. Selection favors: (a) items not already captured in `established/022`
through `022l`'s unit vocabulary; (b) idiomatic/non-compositional entries (fixed expressions,

glossary itself adds). Purely basic, already-well-attested items (numbers, colors, common nouns
