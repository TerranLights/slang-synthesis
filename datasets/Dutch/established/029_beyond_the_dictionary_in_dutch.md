# Dutch — Established Vocabulary: "Beyond the Dictionary in Dutch" (Donaldson), Part 1

**Source:** Donaldson, B.C., *Beyond the Dictionary in Dutch: A Guide to Correct Word Usage for the
English-Speaking Student* (Coutinho, Muiderberg, 1990). English-headword usage-discrimination
dictionary (~500 English lemmas covering ~2500 Dutch words) — this file covers the start of the
alphabetical body, printed pages 1–16 (PDF pages 8–16 of `32 Beyond the Dictionary in Dutch A Guide
to Correct Word Usage.pdf`), entries "about" through "around."

**This is a HIGH-PRIORITY, register-relevant source.** Unlike a general grammar reference, every
entry in this book exists specifically to discriminate near-synonyms by register/formality/
connotation — so nearly every row below carries a genuine usage distinction in its Notes column
rather than a generic gloss.
