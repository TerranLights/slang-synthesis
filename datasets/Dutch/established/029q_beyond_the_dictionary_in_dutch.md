## Grammar points

*(No standalone grammar sections. Grammar-adjacent notes embedded in this range — the *zich
herinneren*/*onthouden* "recall" vs. "retain/keep in mind" distinction (a substantial difficulty

object) vs. reflexive *zich herinneren* (to remember, likewise no *aan*) contrast under "to
