## Grammar points

### 1. Brief-conventies — salutation/closing register (Les 24, printed p. 68)

Dutch letters place the sender's city and the date at the top (`Houten, 1-12-2006`, comma between
city and date). The salutation is register-conditioned: **Beste** or **Lieve** for family/friends/
acquaintances (informal-to-warm register) vs. **Geachte** for business/formal letters. Closings are
similarly register-marked: *een vriendelijke groet* or *een hartelijke groet* ("kind/warm regards"),
followed by name, address, and signature. This three-way salutation split (*Lieve* > *Beste* >
*Geachte*, roughly warm-informal → neutral-informal → formal) is a small but concrete register scale
worth keeping in mind for any in-universe letter/message register work.

### 2. Idioom — full harvest, Lessons 21–28

- **Les 21** (Verkleinwoorden, printed p. 62): *Hebt u een minuutje voor mij?* — not a fixed idiom
  but a formulaic diminutive-bearing request phrase ("Do you have a minute for me?"), deliberately
  chosen by the source to model the lesson's own grammar point (the diminutive *-je* marking
  brevity/casualness of the request, not literal smallness).
- **Les 22** (Voorzetsels (2), printed p. 64): *Op tijd komen* — "to arrive on time"; a fixed
  collocation rather than a figurative idiom, again tied to the lesson's own preposition-of-time
  content.
- **Les 23** (Telwoorden, printed p. 66): *Tijd is geld* — "time is money" (direct cognate idiom).
- **Les 24** (Maanden en data; brieven, printed p. 68): *Laten we er (nu maar) een punt achter
  zetten!* — literally "let's put a period behind it (now)," idiomatically "let's call it a day /
  let's wrap this up" — a punctuation-mark metaphor for closure.
- **Les 25** (Rangtelwoorden; breuken, printed p. 72): *Op je tellen passen* — literally "to watch
  your counting," idiomatically "to watch your step / be careful" (a numeral-themed idiom tied to
  the lesson's own topic).
- **Les 26** (Trappen van vergelijking, printed p. 74): *Beter laat dan nooit!* — "better late than
  never" (already captured in `033`'s own Idioom sample; not re-tabulated here to avoid a duplicate
  row).
- **Les 27** (Bijvoeglijke naamwoorden (2), printed p. 76): *Een gouden handdruk* — "a golden
  handshake" (a severance/exit payment; direct cognate idiom, also tied to the lesson's own
  material-adjective content, since *gouden* "golden" is itself one of the lesson's worked stof-
  adjective examples).
- **Les 28** (Onregelmatige werkwoorden, printed p. 78): *Daar kun je naar fluiten!* — literally
  "you can whistle for that!", idiomatically "you can forget about ever getting that / that's gone
  for good."
