### 1. Bijwoorden (Lessons 75–78) — written/formal-register adverb inventory

The source organizes ~50 adverbs across four lessons by semantic function (time, place, frequency,
degree/quantity, connective/logical-relation, modality/epistemic stance, expectation, estimation,
restriction, and a final miscellaneous-modality set), consistently flagging which items are
literary/formal-register alternatives to a simpler everyday word. Selected structurally interesting
patterns: **doubled-modal-adverb combinations carry compositional nuance** not reducible to either
word alone — *nog maar* ("only ... [so far]," implying an expectation of more: *ik heb nog maar één
kind*, "I have only one child [so far, but more are expected]") vs. plain *maar* ("only," with no
such implication); **al/nog/pas form a coherent three-way expectation-marking system** around a
single quantity/time point — *al* marks "earlier/more than expected" (*zij is al zeventig!*, "she's
already seventy!" — surprise at how much/soon), *nog* marks "not yet at the expected point" (*zij is
nog geen vier jaar*, "she's not yet four" — expectation not yet met), *pas* marks "only just/no
earlier than" (*zij is pas drie jaar*, "she's only three" — expectation of more time ahead) — this
is a genuinely systematic Dutch temporal-expectation adverb paradigm worth flagging for any
in-universe temporal-modality mechanics. The full vocabulary is tabulated below.

### Idioom — full harvest, Lessons 71–78 (Deel 2 complete)

- **Les 71** (Verschillende woordsoorten met dezelfde stam, printed p. 178): *Een zucht van
  verlichting slaken* — "to let out a sigh of relief" (direct cognate idiom).
- **Les 72** (Het gebruik van het lidwoord, printed p. 180): *Bergen kunnen verzetten* — "to be able
  to move mountains" (direct cognate idiom).
- **Les 73** (Modale werkwoorden, printed p. 182): *Het mag geen naam hebben* — literally "it may not
  have a name," idiomatically "it's nothing worth mentioning / it's negligible" (an understated
  dismissal of something's significance).
- **Les 74** (Het gebruik van 'zou' en 'wou', printed p. 184): *Dat zou je wel willen!* — "You wish!"
  / "As if [that would happen]!" — a dismissive retort to an unrealistic hope or boast.
- **Les 75** (Bijwoorden (1), printed p. 186): *Het volkomen met elkaar eens zijn* — "to be in
  complete agreement" (a fixed collocation built from this lesson's own degree-adverb *volkomen*,
  rather than a figurative idiom).
- **Les 76** (Bijwoorden (2), printed p. 188): *Dat komt van pas!* — "that comes in handy!"
- **Les 77** (Bijwoorden (3), printed p. 190): *Laat me maar!* — "Just leave it to me!" / "Never
  mind, I've got it" (built from this lesson's own near-meaningless discourse-softening *maar*).
- **Les 78** (Bijwoorden (4), printed p. 192): *En wat dan nog?* — "So what?" / "And what of it?" — a
  dismissive rhetorical-question idiom, closing out the book's Idioom feature.
