# Dutch — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Linguaphone *Cursus Nederlands* — Introduction & Lessons 1–10

**Source:** The Linguaphone Institute, *Linguaphone Cursus Nederlands* (Dutch-medium audio-course
companion; © 1957, 12th revised ed. 1979, printed in Great Britain). Seven Dutch/Flemish
academic speakers/consultants credited (Dr A. M. Barkey Wolf-Berkelbach v.d. Sprenkel; Prof. Dr
N. A. Donkersloot; Prof. Dr F. C. Driessen; Dr A. Huysinga; Prof. Dr G. G. Kloeke; Prof. Dr P.
Minderaa; Prof. Dr Th. Weevers — several holding chairs in Dutch Language & Literature at Leiden,
Amsterdam, and London). This extraction covers the "Woordenlijst" (per-lesson vocabulary
booklet)'s Introduction ("Laten we Nederlands spreken", Eerste/Tweede Deel) and Lessons 1–10, PDF
pp. 87–91 (printed booklet pp. 10–21 of the Woordenlijst).

**Source structure note (important for citing page numbers below):** this PDF binds together
**three separately-paginated booklets** that shipped as one physical course: (1) "Tekst van de
Lessen" (dialogue texts only, no glosses) — PDF pp. 1–84; (2) "Woordenlijst" (per-lesson
vocabulary with English glosses, then two full alphabetical Dutch–English/English–Dutch
dictionaries) — PDF pp. 85–137, **this file's source**; (3) "Schriftelijke Oefeningen" (written
exercises) — PDF pp. 138–183. All page citations in this file are the Woordenlijst booklet's own
printed page numbers (not PDF page numbers) unless stated otherwise.

**Coverage note.** Every distinct vocabulary item glossed in the Woordenlijst's Introduction and
Lessons 1–10 is extracted below (siblings `024b`–`024e` continue through Lesson 50, the phonology
appendix, and grammar points drawn from the Oefeningen booklet). Per the source's own "Notes on
the Vocabularies" (p. 10–11): **words are glossed only at first occurrence in the course** — the
per-lesson lists are already cumulative-deduplicated across all 50 lessons, so extracting every
lesson's list (rather than sampling) genuinely captures the full ~50-lesson vocabulary with no
repeats, and is the intended master wordlist (the source's own two alphabetical A–Z dictionaries
at the end merely recompile the same words and are treated as redundant — see the closing file
`024e`'s note). The "Tekst van de Lessen" dialogue-text booklet and "Schriftelijke Oefeningen"
exercise booklet are largely example-sentence drill material built from this same vocabulary
(skipped per the coverage rule's drill-exercise exclusion), **except** for the exercise booklet's
implicit grammar patterns (paradigm-drill structure reveals real grammar points — captured in
`024e`) and the dialogue-text booklet's "Aanvullende Leesoefeningen" supplement, which includes a
genuinely new short proverbs/quotations collection (captured in `024e`).

**Vision-reading note.** This source has no text layer at all (confirmed via `pdftotext`, empty
output) — every entry below was vision-read from rendered page images (120 DPI). **Confirmed
page-spread-scan issue**: each PDF page is a scanned two-printed-page spread (PDF page *N* =
printed pages `2N-2` and `2N-1`), verified empirically against multiple printed page numbers
before committing to any range (not assumed from a fixed-offset formula). **No handwritten
marginalia was found on any page read for this file** — all content is clean typeset print;
flagged per the spec's vision-reading guard as a explicit check, not a silent assumption.

**Non-redundant-supplement note.** This source is not a systematic grammar reference like the
already-extracted `001`–`023` files — it's a graded dialogue-course vocabulary list with almost no
explanatory grammar prose. Overlap with prior extractions is therefore mostly at the lexical level
(expected: "book", "table", "father" etc. recur in every beginner course) rather than at the
grammar-point level, so it is extracted as an independent vocabulary source (source citations/page
numbers are given per entry) rather than filtered against prior chapters. Real grammar-point
content unique to this source (phonology/IPA appendix, and the exercise booklet's paradigm
patterns) is flagged explicitly in `024e`.
