## Grammar points

### Appendix: irregular verb principal parts

The appendix gives, for each of the book's ~75 most commonly used irregular verbs: the infinitive,
the imperfect (singular and plural, since many irregular verbs distinguish these — see
`established/022e`'s Unit 10 imperfect-formation note), the past participle used with the perfect
tense (per `established/022c`'s Unit 8 perfect-tense note), and the English gloss. Verbs that form
their perfect tense with **zijn** rather than **hebben** have their past participle prefixed with
"is" in the table, exactly as the source formats it.

Note on the perfect-with-zijn subset (verbs of motion/change-of-state, cross-referenced against
`established/022c`'s Unit 8 grammar point on hebben vs. zijn): **beginnen, bevallen, blijven, gaan,
komen, lopen, rijden, sterven, vallen, varen, vergeten, vliegen, worden, zijn, zwemmen** all use
**is** + past participle rather than **heeft/hebben**.
