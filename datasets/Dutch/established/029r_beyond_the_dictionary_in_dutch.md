## Grammar points

### Verbs of motion: *zijn* vs. *hebben* in the perfect (towards vs. within a place)

Noted under "to run": *hollen*, *hardlopen*, *lopen*, *rennen*, and *rijden* all belong to the
category of motion verbs that take either *zijn* or *hebben* in the perfect tense depending on
whether the motion described is directional (**towards** a place — takes *zijn*) or non-directional
(motion **within** a place — takes *hebben*). This is the same general principle governing verbs
like *lopen*/*rijden*/*zwemmen* elsewhere in Dutch grammar, restated here specifically for the "to
run" synonym set.

*(Other grammar-adjacent notes embedded in this range — the *verantwoord* (justifiable action) vs.
*verantwoordelijk* (responsible person) distinction under "responsible," the *rechts* attributive-
form irregularity (*rechterhand*, *rechterkant*) vs. its regular *-e* inflection with political
"right" under "(to be) right," and the *laan* (avenue, not "lane") false-friend warning under "the
road, street, lane, path, driveway etc." — are captured inline in their rows' Notes above.)*
