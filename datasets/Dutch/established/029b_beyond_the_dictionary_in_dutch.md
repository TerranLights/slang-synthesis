## Grammar points

*(As in Part 1, this source is a lexical usage-discrimination dictionary with no standalone grammar
sections. Genuinely grammar-adjacent rules embedded in individual entries — the Dutch
"positional-verb" system replacing "to be" (*staan/zitten/liggen/hangen/lopen/blijven/wezen/worden*,
under "to be"), the *voor* vs. *voordat* vs. *eer(dat)* preposition/conjunction distinction under
"before," and the *vragen om/aan/naar* government pattern under "to ask" — are captured inline in the
relevant rows' Notes above, since each is scoped to a single headword's synonym set.)*
