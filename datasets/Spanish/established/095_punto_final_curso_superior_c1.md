# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Punto Final: Curso Superior ELE, Nivel C1 (Nivel Superior) (representative-sample extraction)

**Source:** *Punto Final: Curso Superior de Español como Lengua Extranjera, Nivel C1 (Nivel Superior)*
(student edition "Qua"/"Muy" imprint, 145-page scan, Adobe Acrobat 6.0 Image Conversion Plug-in, 2006
scan timestamp; **no text layer at all** — `pdftotext` returns empty across sampled pages, confirmed
pure page-image scan, 61MB large file). Extracted via vision-reading of `pdftoppm`-rendered page
images (120 dpi).

**CONFIRMED PAGE-OFFSET FINDING:** the PDF's internal page numbering and the book's own printed page
footers are offset by a constant +3 (printed_page = pdf_page + 3) — the first 3 PDF pages are cover/
title/copyright matter not carrying printed page numbers. Cross-checked against the book's own TOC
(which lists "Con chispa" at printed p.13, found exactly at PDF page 10) and held consistently across
all samples taken. Noted here so any future dispatch extracting more of this book doesn't have to
re-derive it.

**Structure:** the book is organized into themed units (confirmed: Unit 1 "La mujer," Unit 2 "La
publicidad," ~5-6 units total across 145pp at ~25-27pp/unit), each following an identical internal
template: "Hablemos de..." (magazine-style reading + comprehension), "Tertulia" (structured debate
activity with an opinion/agreement-disagreement phrase bank), "¡Para no pegar ojo!" (argumentative
writing task), "Aprieta los codos" (grammar point, recurring section header — literally "elbow-
grease," a Spanish idiom for "work hard/buckle down," used ironically as the grammar-drill section's
running title throughout the book), "Con chispa" (idiom/wordplay activity page — **the single highest-
value content type found in this book**), "El ayer de..." (historical background reading), "¡Arriba el
telón!" (audiovisual-fragment listening activity, e.g. an Almodóvar film clip), "Palabras escritas
por..." (literary excerpt by a named author, e.g. Isabel Allende, Julio Cortázar), and a DELE Superior
exam-prep writing prompt.

**Coverage approach:** given the book's heavy grammar-drill content (perífrasis verbales, futuro/
condicional, preposiciones) duplicates material already extensively covered in 006, 014-019, 060-068,
070-077, representative sampling targeted the two highest-yield, lowest-redundancy content types:
the "Con chispa" idiom pages (genuinely new colloquial-idiom content, not covered elsewhere in this
corpus) and the "Tertulia" opinion/debate phrase banks. Unit 1 ("La mujer," PDF pp.1-27/printed 4-30)
was read in full; Unit 2's ("La publicidad") "Con chispa" page was targeted but not successfully
located at its expected offset (TOC lists it at printed p.38; pages at PDF 34-38 were checked and the
page was not found at this offset — likely a page-count discrepancy from an intervening half-page or
insert not accounted for in the simple +3 offset within unit 2; not pursued further given the wave's
sampling budget). A Julio Cortázar literary excerpt and advertising-history timeline were also sampled
in Unit 1/2 but yielded little extractable vocabulary (narrative prose, not glossed).

**Non-redundant-supplement note:** grammar points (gerundio/participio perífrasis, futuro/
condicional contrast) are NOT re-extracted — already well covered elsewhere in this corpus. Only
idioms, debate/opinion phrase banks, proverbs, and register-marked physical-description vocabulary are
included below.
