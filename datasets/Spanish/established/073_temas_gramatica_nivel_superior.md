# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Temas de Gramática. Nivel Superior, con Ejercicios Prácticos

**Source:** Concha Moreno, *Temas de Gramática. Nivel Superior. Con Ejercicios Prácticos*
(Sociedad General Española de Librería, scanned PDF, 227 pp.). A Spanish-medium advanced
"problem-points" grammar explicitly pitched (per its own "Presentación," p. 5, read in full) at
teachers answering student doubts, advanced/superior-level students, autodidacts, and official-exam
candidates — 21 themed "Temas" (topics), each a deep dive into one specific area of confusion
(verb+preposition meaning changes, ser/estar, the passive and how to avoid it, constructions with
se, verbos de cambio, subjunctive triggers, relative/temporal/concessive/causal/consecutive/
conditional clause types, ya/todavía, expressions of desire and doubt), plus a full answer key. No
extractable text layer (`pdftotext` empty on every sampled page) — confirmed vision-required per
the Wave 3 triage survey.

**Vision-reading approach:** read 11 of 227 pages (~5%). Read the presentation (p. 5) and the full
"Contenido" index (p. 4, listing all 21 temas) to map the book's scope, then selected the three
temas that scored highest for non-redundancy against this corpus's existing coverage (checks
below): Tema 1 (*La ortografía. Acentos*, pp. 6–7, read as a supplement to this dispatch's own
`071_gramatica_de_uso_a1_b2.md` accentuation coverage), Tema 6 (*Verbos con preposición. Cambios de
significado*, pp. 65–66), and Tema 8 (*La pasiva. Recursos para evitarla*, pp. 88, 90). The other
18 temas (pasados, ya/todavía, prepositions, basic verbs+preposition, ser/estar, construcciones con
se, verbos de cambio, all 10 subjunctive/clause-type temas, condicionales, estilo indirecto,
deseo/duda) were **not** vision-read — the index-level topic match against `006`, `070` (this same
dispatch's B2 book, which already covers ser/estar/parecer, causales, consecutivas, temporales,
relativos, otros nexos condicionales, and verbos de entendimiento/percepción in comparable depth),
and the broader established/ corpus made clear they would be heavily duplicative.

**Non-redundant-supplement note:** grepped established/ before selecting — "ya...todavía" already
has 5+ prior hits (011p, 013d, 014, 018, 020), so Tema 3 was skipped despite being a named topic.
"evitar la pasiva" / passive-avoidance strategy has only 1 prior hit (`021_collins_easy_learning_spanish_grammar.md`),
and a search for "verbos con preposición" + "cambios de significado" (verbs whose core meaning
shifts depending on which preposition follows, e.g. *acabar con* ≠ *acabar de* ≠ *acabar en* ≠
*acabar por*) returned **zero** prior hits anywhere in established/ — this is the strongest find in
this entire 5-book dispatch. Tema 1's specific sub-rules (plural-formation accent shifts like
carácter→caracteres, sobreesdrújulas, compound-word and -mente-adverb accent retention) go beyond
what `071` captured (which covered diphthong/hiato rules and the basic diacritic set but not plural
or compounding behavior), so it was kept as a genuine supplement rather than a duplicate.
