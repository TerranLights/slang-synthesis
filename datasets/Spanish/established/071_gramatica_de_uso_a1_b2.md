# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Gramática de uso del español. Teoría y práctica (A1-B2)

**Source:** Luis Aragonés & Ramón Palencia, *Gramática de uso del español. Teoría y práctica, con
solucionario* (Ediciones SM, 2007, scanned PDF, 291 pp., 127 MB). A well-known Spanish-medium
A1-B2 reference grammar — 126 micro-units, each a fixed two-page spread (theory page + practice
page), covering essentially the entire elementary-to-upper-intermediate grammatical system, plus a
short "Otras cuestiones" appendix (accentuation, capitalization, voseo) and a final analytical
index/answer key. No extractable text layer (`pdftotext` empty on every sampled page) — confirmed
vision-required per the Wave 3 triage survey.

**Vision-reading approach:** read 13 of 291 pages (~4%) — the narrowest sample of the five books in
this wave, because this book's 126-unit scope (masculine/feminine agreement, articles,
demonstratives, possessives, every verb-tense paradigm, imperative, subjunctive, conditionals,
infinitive/gerund/participle constructions, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, clause types) is
almost entirely redundant with material already captured many times over elsewhere in this
corpus — by `005`/`006`/`008`(+b/c) (Practice Makes Perfect series), `069` and `070` (the other two
workbooks from this same dispatch), the sibling `065`-`068` "Uso de la Gramática Española" series
(a *different* book by a different author/publisher covering nearly the same A1-B2 ground in
Spanish-medium form), and a dozen reference grammars (014, 017-022, 024, 029). Read the full index
(pp. 7-9, listing all 126 units) to confirm this, then targeted only the handful of units flagged
as genuinely non-redundant by the checks below: Unit 109 (*yo sí, yo no, yo también, yo tampoco* —
expressions of agreement/disagreement, p. 226), Unit 123 (*reglas de acentuación 1*, p. 256),
Unit 124 (*reglas de acentuación 2* — vowel-group/diphthong stress rules, p. 258), Unit 125
(*uso de mayúsculas*, p. 260), and Unit 126 (*conjugación verbal con vos* — a dedicated one-page
full-paradigm voseo appendix, p. 262). The other 121 units were not vision-read.

**Non-redundant-supplement note:** grepped established/ for "reglas de acentuación" /
"palabra aguda/llana/esdrújula" (only 2 prior hits: `020_gramatica_didactica_del_espanol.md`,
`029_using_spanish_contemporary_usage.md` — both English/academic-register treatments, not this
book's A1-level rule-of-thumb presentation), "uso de mayúsculas"/"mayúscula" (only 1 prior hit:
`029`), and "yo también...yo tampoco" agreement expressions (only `056_perfect_phrases_travel_mexico.md`
and sibling `065_uso_gramatica_espanola_elemental.md` as close hits — neither collects the
sí/no/también/tampoco paradigm as a single four-way system the way this unit does). Voseo itself is
documented in many established/ files already (grepped 18+ hits), including this dispatch's own
`069`, but none of them is a **dedicated, single-page, full-paradigm conjugation appendix**
covering present indicative (regular + irregular) and imperative (regular, irregular, and
reflexive/pronominal) side-by-side with tú — this unit's systematic completeness is the
non-redundant value, not the existence of voseo coverage per se.
