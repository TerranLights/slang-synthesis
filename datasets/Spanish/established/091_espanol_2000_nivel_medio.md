# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: *Español 2000: Nivel Medio* (sampled, OCR-recovered)

**Source:** Nieves García Fernández and Jesús Sánchez Lobato, *Español 2000: Nivel Medio*
(Sociedad General Española de Librería, S.A., Alcobendas, Madrid). Scanned PDF, 238 pages (per
`pdfinfo`), large file (67MB). A 21-lesson intermediate course, each lesson built around a dialogue
("Documentos") plus a tight verb-tense/mood focus (imperfecto/indefinido, subjuntivo in all its
tenses, condicional, voz pasiva, formas impersonales, formas no personales) and a per-lesson
"Expresiones," word-formation ("Prefijación"/"Sufijación"/"Raíces prefijas y sufijas"), or spelling
point. An appendix covers the IPA, Cyrillic/Arabic alphabets, and irregular verbs.

**Important scan-completeness caveat:** the book's own table of contents (OCR'd from the front
matter) lists a final "GLOSARIO" appendix starting at printed p.246, but this PDF scan is only
238 pages long and physically ends mid-way through the "VERBOS IRREGULARES" appendix (last
rendered page shows PROBAR/QUERER/REÍR/VALER/VENIR conjugations) — **the glossary section is not
present in this scan at all.** This is a source-completeness gap, not an extraction gap: there was
nothing to extract from a section the available copy does not contain.

**Text layer:** every page is a full-page 200dpi grayscale JPEG scan (confirmed via `pdfimages -list`)
with a badly-misrecognized embedded OCR text layer (non-embedded "Helvetica"/WinAnsi substitute
font with no ToUnicode map) — `pdftotext` output is **not usable directly**: word-spacing is lost
throughout and individual letters are frequently misread (e.g. "¿"→"è", "¡"→"i", "f"/"p" dropped
or substituted depending on surrounding glyphs). This is the same underlying scan-quality problem
already documented for `034_espanol_comercial.md` in this corpus, and the same fix was applied
here: target pages were identified by keyword-grepping the bad embedded text layer (which, despite
being unusable as a transcription source, still contains enough intact substrings to locate
sections), then those specific pages were re-rendered to 200dpi grayscale images with `pdftoppm`
and re-OCR'd with Tesseract's Spanish (`spa`) trained-data model (downloaded fresh this session,
since the environment ships only `eng`/`osd` by default). **OCR quality assessment:** every
re-OCR'd page came out clean and fully legible — correct accents, correct word boundaries, correct
letter recognition — a strong improvement over the embedded text layer, consistent with the
`034` precedent. Transcription Confidence is accordingly set to n/a and Vision Reading Confidence
to verified throughout this file, per this corpus's convention for OCR-recovered scanned sources.

**Redundancy check against existing Spanish grammar-practice corpus** (`065`-`073` "Uso de
Gramática"/"Gramática de Uso"/"Prácticas" family, `074` subjuntivo, `075`-`078` verb books): this
course's lesson-by-lesson verb-tense/mood drilling (imperfecto/indefinido contrast, subjunctive
tense sequence, passive/impersonal forms, non-personal verb forms) is **the same ground already
well covered** by that cluster and is deliberately NOT re-extracted here (skipped per the
non-redundant-supplement convention, along with all fill-in-the-blank drill exercises per the
standard coverage rule). What follows is a sample of this book's genuinely distinctive content:
its word-formation root/affix inventories (several of which go beyond what's catalogued elsewhere
in this corpus), its "Americanismos en la lengua española" indigenous-loanword inventory by source
language, its "Expresiones con echar" idiom cluster, and its Hispanicized-spelling treatment of
English/French loanwords (filme, cóctel, eslogan, güisqui, etc.) — none of which appear to be
duplicated anywhere else yet in this corpus. Two narrowly standard grammar points (por/para,
porqué-family homophone spelling) were deliberately skipped as near-certain duplicates of material
already in the 065-073 cluster.

**Entry count:** 33 vocabulary/word-formation/idiom rows, sampled from roughly a dozen pages out
of 238 (lessons 7, 9, 13, 15, 17/appendix, 18, 20, 21) — not an exhaustive pass.
