## What this book is

A mystery novella ("Primera edición: 1987," EDELSA Grupo Didascalia, Colección "Para que leas," dirigida
por Lourdes Miquel y Neus Sans — the same editorial duo who authored the "Lola Lago, detective" series
sampled elsewhere in this wave, established/115 and established/118, confirming a direct institutional/
authorial link between this reader and those two). Protagonist Pepe Rey is a private detective in
Madrid investigating the murder of an actress, Natalia, poisoned via a traditional Nochevieja
(New Year's Eve) "lucky grape" ritual. Setting: **Madrid, Spain**, around Christmas/New Year, no
specific year stated in the narrative itself (publication 1987). Register: everyday conversational
Madrid Spanish with light noir-detective-story trappings, substantial insult/slang vocabulary in
dialogue between the detective and his secretary Susi.

## Pedagogical apparatus found

**15 numbered cultural footnotes** ("Notas," collected at the back, same structural pattern as the
"Lola Lago" series and *La Celestina*) explaining Spanish Christmas/New Year's customs, Madrid
geography, and literary allusions — explicitly glossing the cultural backbone the plot's central murder
method depends on (the "doce uvas de la suerte" New Year's grape tradition). Notably, **this edition
also carries a parallel French-language translation of the same 15 notes** ("Notes," immediately
following the Spanish set) — indicating the book was produced for (at least) a French-speaking
Spanish-learner market as well as a general one; only the Spanish-language notes are extracted below
per this corpus's scope.
