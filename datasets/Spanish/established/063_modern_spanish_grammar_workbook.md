## Non-redundant-supplement scope decision — confirmed near-total redundancy, kept deliberately brief

This is the pure-exercise companion workbook to *Modern Spanish Grammar*, 2nd edition — the exact
book 017 already extracted in depth, covering Part A (Structures, Ch.19-28) closely and Part B
(Functions, Ch.29-73) "close to comprehensively." This workbook's own structure is an exact 1:1 match
to that same chapter numbering (confirmed via its 8 self-evaluation checklists, which list every
grammar point and function by the identical unit numbers 017 already cites): pronunciation/spelling/
gender/plurals/articles/adjectives/numerals/pronouns (Units 1-10, matching 017's "skipped as
redundant" Part A Ch.1-15 decision), relative/interrogative/indefinite/negative pronouns and full
verb-tense formation (Units 11-17), subjunctive by trigger-category and periphrastic verb forms
(Units 18-20, matching 017's Ch.19-20 extraction), modal verbs/ser-estar/reflexives/passive/
prepositions/por-para/word order (Units 21-28, matching 017's Ch.21-28), and the entire Functions
catalog — greetings, phone calls, letters, apologizing, interrupting, asking questions, negating,
reporting, and on through all the categories 017's "PART B" section already extracts "close to
comprehensively" (Units 29 onward).

**A full-text scan for dialect/register/slang markers** (`latin america`, `peninsular`, `coloquial`,
`vulgar`, `argot`, `jerga`, `slang`, `dialect`, `register`, `regional`, plus spot-checks for common
colloquial items like `tío`, `guay`, `chaval`, `mogollón`, `flipar`, `colega`, `molar`, `chulo`,
`rollo`) turned up **no systematic dialectal annotation and no colloquial/slang vocabulary** beyond
a handful of "Latin American" scenario-setting details in exercise prompts (a Latin American film
star being interviewed, a Latin American business trip) that carry no actual lexical content, and
"colega" used strictly in its neutral professional "colleague" sense throughout (never the
colloquial "buddy/mate" sense), consistent with this being a fairly formal-register business/
travel-situation-oriented Functions section.

**Conclusion: this workbook adds no grammar point, no vocabulary item, and no dialectal/register
note beyond what 017 already captured from the same authors' companion grammar.** Per this
project's "honest partial/minimal coverage over forced exhaustiveness" convention, the vocabulary
