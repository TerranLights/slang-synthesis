## Grammar points

### Present perfect: six irregular past participles catalogued in one lesson

Bonus Lesson E systematically catalogs the present perfect of six of Spanish's most common irregular
participles, all previously untouched by Bonus Lessons B-D's regular-participle drills: decir→dicho
("le he dicho"), escribir→escrito ("hemos escrito"), volver→vuelto ("han vuelto"), poner→puesto ("han
puesto"), abrir→abierto ("ha abierto"), hacer→hecho ("le hemos hecho") — plus a review use of
ver→visto. This is a directly reusable single-lesson reference for Spanish's irregular participle set.
(Bonus Lesson E, throughout.)

### Pluperfect/past perfect: "había/habías/habíamos/habían + past participle"

Bonus Lesson F (despite its own stated title referencing the imperfect) is entirely built around the
pluperfect — a past-before-a-past construction: "ya había cenado antes de ir al teatro" (I had already
eaten dinner before going to the theater), "había comprado los boletos hace tres semanas" (he had
bought the tickets three weeks ago), "no habíamos empleado a estos actores antes" (we had not employed
these actors before). The lesson's closing transformation drill explicitly contrasts a simple past
negative statement ("no cené anoche en casa") against the correct pluperfect response describing an
earlier prior action ("yo ya había cenado en la oficina") — reinforcing the pluperfect's core function
of sequencing two past events relative to each other, distinct from the simple preterite. (Bonus
Lesson F, throughout, esp. the closing drill pp. 8-9.)
