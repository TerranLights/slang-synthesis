# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Prisma Progresa (Nivel B1) (representative-sample extraction)

**Source:** *Prisma Progresa: Método de Español para Extranjeros, Nivel B1* (Editorial Edinumen,
alumno/student edition), 158-page scan (`ScanToPDF` creator, 2008 scan timestamp; **no text layer at
all** — `pdftotext` returns empty across sampled pages, confirmed pure page-image scan). Extracted
entirely via vision-reading of `pdftoppm`-rendered page images (120 dpi). One PDF page = one printed
page throughout (no spread-scan offset, unlike some other vision-required books in this wave).

**Coverage approach:** 158 pages / 12 teaching units + 2 review units is far too much for wall-to-wall
vision-reading in this wave, and — per dispatch guidance — this book's B1 grammar content (subjunctive,
imperative, pasados, condicional, discourse connectors) overlaps heavily with the extensive grammar
corpus already established in this language folder (065-077, 060-064, 014-019, etc.). Representative
sampling was used instead: the book's own table of contents (PDF pp.2-8) was read in full to map all 14
units' topics and "Contenidos culturales" callouts, then three units were vision-read in their entirety
as highest-value targets: **Unit 1** (El Rastro / regateo / cortesía — genuinely new cultural-register
content, no grammar overlap), **Unit 3** (body-part and gym vocabulary, imperative formation drills —
vocabulary kept, grammar drills skipped as redundant), and **Unit 7** (ecology vocabulary, opinion/
discourse-connector phrase bank — vocabulary and connectors kept, drills skipped). The TOC explicitly
flagged two dialect-contrast cultural boxes — Unit 3's "Español de América: uso del imperativo" and
Unit 7's "Contraste de expresiones: español de España y español de América" — as unit content; both
unit page-ranges were vision-read in full (pp.29-40 and pp.77-88 respectively) but **neither box was
found printed in the student (alumno) edition** — most likely these are audio-only listening-exercise
content in this book's format (cassette/CD icons appear throughout without accompanying transcripts),
not printed text, so nothing was fabricated for them. This is noted rather than silently dropped.

**Non-redundant-supplement note:** Grammar points re-drilled in the sampled units (imperative forms,
preterite/imperfect contrast, future, conditional, subjunctive of doubt) were NOT re-extracted — they
are already covered in depth across 005-019, 060-068, 070-077. Only genuinely new vocabulary, cultural
notes, idioms, and discourse-connector inventories are included below.

**Marginalia:** none found in the sampled pages (clean scan, no library stamps or handwriting observed).
