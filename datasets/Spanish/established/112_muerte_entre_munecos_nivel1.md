# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Reference: Muerte entre muñecos (Lecturas de Español, Nivel Intermedio I)

**Source:** *Muerte entre muñecos* (Julio Ruiz Melero, Edinumen, "Lecturas de Español" series,
Nivel Intermedio I, 34 pp., vision-required PDF — confirmed: `pdftotext` extraction is essentially
empty, ~1 char/page). Read in full via vision (34/34 pages).

**Series/apparatus:** third sibling book in this wave from the same Edinumen "Lecturas de Español"
collection as `110_amnesia_nivel1.md` and `111_carnaval_nivel1.md` (different author again — Julio
Ruiz Melero — confirming the series rotates authors per title/level). Same left-margin gloss-box
format, but this Nivel Intermedio entry is noticeably **denser** than the two Elemental-level
readers: nearly every page carries 2–4 glossed items, mixing single vocabulary words, idioms, and
explicit **cultural-custom notes** (e.g. on Spanish greeting customs, siesta, late dinner hours,
end-of-course gift-giving). All ~85 margin-gloss terms found across the narrative were extracted
below. The book also uses a formal "con Explotación Didáctica" subtitle on its cover, consistent
with the series, though — as with the other two siblings — the post-narrative exercise apparatus
(not reached in this extraction; the narrative runs the entire book to p. 36) was not the target.

**Plot/setting:** hardboiled detective pastiche set in **Barcelona**, narrated by **Maite Rovira**,
a struggling, underemployed female private investigator who inherited the agency from her
ex-husband (specific real Barcelona locations are used throughout: the Ensanche, Villa Olímpica,
Vía Laietana, the Diagonal, Sarrià, the Collserola mountains). A client, Marga Ramos, hires her to
confirm her husband Ricardo Fernández's suspected affair. The investigation spirals into a murder
(a woman, later identified — with an internal inconsistency in the source text itself, alternately
named "Carla Wagner" and "Clara Wagner" — found dead in an Villa Olímpica apartment) and a
diamond-smuggling ring run through a toy company (Ludocesma) that hides contraband diamonds inside
promotional dolls/action figures tied to a fictional blockbuster film, "Los Conquistadores de
Marte." The twist ending (a staple of the genre): the client herself, Marga Ramos, orchestrated the
entire scheme and the murder, using her own detective as an unwitting alibi-witness, and is shot
dead in the story's final confrontation.

**Register/content note:** this is the most hardboiled-genre-marked reader in the wave — heavy use
of noir idiom (fiambre "corpse," cabeza de turco "scapegoat," tapadera "front/cover," corte el
rollo "cut the crap") alongside the series' usual cultural-custom glosses. Register is mixed:
first-person noir-detective narration (colloquial, wisecracking) interleaved with dialogue.
