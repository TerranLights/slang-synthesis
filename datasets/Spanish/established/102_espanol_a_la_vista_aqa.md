## Scope note and institutional framing (confirmed distinctive, per dispatch hypothesis)

This is confirmed to be a **UK GCSE exam-preparation course**, not a general beginner course: "Welcome
to Español a la vista para AQA... The four módulos take you systematically through the four topic
areas of your GCSE examination" (Introduction). AQA is a UK secondary-school exam board. Distinctive
structural features not seen elsewhere in this corpus's beginner-course set:
- **▲/▼ tiering markers** on individual tasks for "higher level" vs. "foundation level" — a direct
  reflection of the UK GCSE's two-tier exam structure (this concept has no equivalent in any other
  book in this corpus, which are not written against a specific national exam syllabus).
- **Mañas** sections: dedicated study-skills/exam-technique tips (dictionary skills, preparing an oral
  exam, preparing coursework, "how to cope in the final exam").
- **Ponte a prueba**: timed practice tests at the end of each módulo, explicitly covering the same
  four skills (listening/speaking/reading/writing) the GCSE exam tests.
- Topic/Scope column in the TOC is written to **match GCSE specification topic headings directly**
  (e.g. "Part-time jobs... work experience, applying for jobs, interviews, answering the phone";
  "Education issues: comparison between countries, equal opportunities... bullying"; "Choices and
  responsibilities: fashions, influence of advertising, legal ages, smoking, drugs, alcohol, social
  issues") — this exam-topic-driven vocabulary selection, rather than general-purpose conversational
  scope, is the book's core distinguishing feature per the dispatch hypothesis, and is confirmed here.

Four módulos: 1 "El mundo mío" (self/family/home/daily routine/school), 2 "De vacaciones y de viaje"
(travel/tourism/accommodation/services), 3 "Vivir y trabajar" (home life/health/jobs/leisure/shopping),
4 "Nosotros los jóvenes" (relationships/environment/education/careers/social issues) — ending in
Grammar reference, verb tables, grammar practice, English-Spanish and Spanish-English vocabulary
lists, maps, and a grammar index.
