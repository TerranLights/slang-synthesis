# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Gramática. Nivel B2 (Anaya ELE)

**Source:** *Gramática. Nivel B2* (Anaya ELE series, scanned PDF, 260 pp.), a Spanish-medium
upper-intermediate (B2, per the Instituto Cervantes Plan Curricular) grammar-theory-and-practice
workbook, 29 units of 8 pages each ("¡Fíjese!" contextualizing dialogue → "Ya sabe"/"Así es"
explanation box → "Además" extension box → exercises → "Mis conclusiones"), plus a final
self-test and answer key. No extractable text layer (`pdftotext` empty on every sampled page) —
confirmed vision-required per the Wave 3 triage survey.

**Vision-reading approach:** read 20 of 260 pages (~8%) rather than exhaustively. Read the full
index (pp. 1, 3–4, listing all 29 units and their page ranges) to map the book's structure, then
read the "Ya sabe"/"Así es"/"Además" explanation pages (2 pages each) of 8 units, chosen
specifically because their topics are **syntactic/connector-based** rather than paradigm-based and
had the least overlap with this corpus's existing grammar coverage (see redundancy note below):
Unit 7 (el pronombre se), Unit 16 (ser/estar/parecer), Unit 19 (construcciones comparativas),
Unit 20 (oraciones causales), Unit 21 (oraciones consecutivas), Unit 22 (oraciones temporales),
Unit 23 (relativos en oraciones explicativas y especificativas), Unit 25 (otros nexos
condicionales), and Unit 27 (verbos de entendimiento y percepción con indicativo y subjuntivo).
The remaining ~20 units (el sustantivo, el adjetivo, los artículos, los demostrativos, los
posesivos, los indefinidos, los adverbios, every indicative/subjunctive tense paradigm, formas no
personales y perífrasis, verbos de cambio, voz pasiva y media, oraciones condicionales con si,
concesivas, estilo indirecto, las preposiciones) were **not** vision-read: per the redundancy check
below, this corpus already has deep, multi-source coverage of all of them. Exercise pages
(5–6 of each unit's 8) were not transcribed — confirmed by spot-check to recombine vocabulary
already captured from the same unit's exposition pages, consistent with this project's coverage
rule for drill material.

**Non-redundant-supplement note:** checked against this corpus's existing grammar references —
`006_practice_makes_perfect_advanced_spanish_grammar.md` (object pronouns, determiners,
commands/modals, impersonal/passive, relative pronouns, reported speech, prepositions, idioms),
`008_practice_makes_perfect_spanish_verb_tenses.md` + `008b`/`008c` (full tense system incl.
subjunctive tenses, concessives, temporal clauses), `014_practice_makes_perfect_complete_spanish_grammar.md`,
`017_modern_spanish_grammar_practical_guide.md`, `018_big_red_book_of_spanish_grammar.md`,
`019_spanish_essential_grammar.md`, `020_gramatica_didactica_del_espanol.md`,
`021_collins_easy_learning_spanish_grammar.md`, `022_websters_spanish_grammar_handbook.md`,
`024_introduccion_linguistica_hispanica.md`, and `029_using_spanish_contemporary_usage.md` (all of
which already document voz pasiva, verbos de cambio, pretérito pluscuamperfecto de subjuntivo,
oraciones concesivas/aunque, and estilo indirecto in comparable or greater depth), plus the
already-landed `069_gramatica_practica_nivel_elemental.md` (basic comparatives/superlatives,
ser-estar contrast at the A1-B1 level) and `074_practica_tu_espanol_subjuntivo.md` (subjunctive
triggers). A targeted grep for "oraciones causales"/"oraciones consecutivas" and "perception verbs
+ indicative/subjunctive" returned **no** prior hits anywhere in established/ — those two topics,
plus this book's unusually complete connector tables for temporal and conditional clauses, are the
clearest non-redundant value this book adds, and were prioritized accordingly. Ser/estar/parecer
and el pronombre se were also included because this book's specific framings (ser/estar pairs with
meaning change like listo, the pronoun-ordering hierarchy, se + verbs-of-change) were not found
duplicated elsewhere during spot checks.
