## Coverage notes

This is a short, compact tourist-phrasebook companion to a BBC audio course (per credits: "Audio producer... Sound engineering... Presenters..."), organized into 8 situational chapters: Bare Necessities, Getting Around, Somewhere to Stay, Buying Things, Café Life, Eating Out, Entertainment, Emergencies. At only 31 book pages it is fully tractable for complete extraction, which was done below (minus the book's "check out" comprehension-quiz dialogues and "as if you were there" role-play drills, which recombine vocabulary already captured in the main phrase lists rather than introducing new terms).

**Dialect-specificity flag (per dispatch instructions):** despite the generic title "Latin American Spanish," the book's actual content is overwhelmingly **Mexico-specific**, not pan-Latin-American. Every place name used in its example dialogues is Mexican (Cancún, Ciudad de México/"el DF", Chichén Itzá, Palenque, Taxco, Guanajuato, Acapulco, Bellas Artes) with the sole exception of one Machu Picchu (Peru) reference in an Entertainment-chapter dialogue. Currency throughout is pesos. The vocabulary choices consistently track Mexican Spanish specifically (not a generic Latin American standard) — see the dedicated regional-vocabulary table below. Attested Region is therefore marked "Mexico" rather than "Latin America" for the large majority of entries, with the book's own self-description ("Latin American Spanish") noted as a mismatch worth flagging for this project's region-tagging conventions.
