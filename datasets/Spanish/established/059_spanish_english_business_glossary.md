## Redundancy finding — this is the exact same glossary already fully extracted in `035b`-`035i`

This dispatch's instructions flagged this title for a redundancy check against the four business
Spanish titles already extracted in Wave S1 (`034_espanol_comercial.md`, `035_manual_of_business_spanish.md`
+ its glossary scratch files `035b`-`035i`, `036_spanish_business_correspondence.md`,
`037_spanish_business_situations.md`). That check found **total duplication, not partial overlap**:

1. **Same authors, same publisher series.** This standalone *Spanish/English Business Glossary*
   (Routledge, 1997) is by the identical author pair — Michael Gorman and María-Luisa Henson — as
   *Manual of Business Spanish: A Comprehensive Language Guide* (Routledge, 2006), whose own
   "Business Glossary" section (printed pp.250-417) was extracted in full in `035` as scratch files
   `035b`-`035i` (~4,927 headword entries, Spanish-English and English-Spanish halves). This
   standalone book's own front matter (p.1) lists itself in Routledge's "Business Correspondence /
   Business Situations / Manual of Business [Language] / [Language]/English Business Glossary"
   series alongside the other three Spanish titles in this corpus, confirming it is the same
   publisher program, same authors, and (as verified below) the same underlying glossary dataset
   that was later folded into the 2006 omnibus.

2. **Word-for-word identical content, verified at multiple independent points in the alphabet:**
   - **Opening entries** (both start at "abandonar"): this PDF's text —
     *"abandonar vb abandon vb, quit vb / abandono nm neglect n, withdrawal n, desertion n /
     abastecedor, -ora nm,f supplier n / abastecer vb supply vb..."* — is identical, word for word,
     to `035b`'s table rows for abandonar/abandono/abastecedor/abastecer (differing only in the
     established/ table's own structural reformatting of part-of-speech tags into a column).
   - **Mid-alphabet spot-check ("management"):** this PDF's English-Spanish half (line ~6826 of the
     raw extracted text) reads *"management n dirección general nf, administración de empresas nf,
     gerencia nf, gestión (LAm)... management buy-out adquisición de una empresa por sus propios
     directives nf management by objectives dirección por objetivos nf management consultant..."* —
     identical to `035h`'s "management" row.
   - **Closing entries** (both end at "zoning"): this PDF's final lines — *"...zero n cero nm...
     zip code (US) n código postal nm... zone 1. n zona nf, polígono nm... zoning n zonificación nf,
     division por zonas nf"* — are identical to `035i`'s final rows (zenith/zero/zip code/zone/
     zoning).
   - **Front-matter "Key to glossary"** (grammatical abbreviations: abbr/adj/adv/conj/det/n/nf/
     nfpl/nm/nmpl/pp/pref/prep/vb; symbols `*` slang, `(US)`, `(GB)`, `(LAm)`) is the identical key
     `035b`'s own extraction note already describes as sourced from "PDF p.257" of the 2006 omnibus
     — i.e., the omnibus's glossary section (extracted in `035b`-`035i`) reproduced this standalone
     1997 book's own front matter verbatim when it incorporated the glossary.
3. **Headword-count consistency check:** `035b`-`035i` together contain 4,953 table rows (minus
   8 header rows across the 8 files) ≈ 4,945 headwords, matching `035`'s own stated "~4,927
   headword entries" for the omnibus glossary section — consistent with this standalone glossary
   (182 pp., a comparable Spanish-English + English-Spanish split) being the same dataset at
   effectively the same scale, not a subset or superset.

**Conclusion: this PDF is not a distinct source for this project's purposes — it is the original
1997 standalone publication of the exact same business glossary that `035`'s 2006 omnibus
incorporated and that this corpus already extracted in full (`035b`-`035i`).** Per the
non-redundant-supplement convention, it is **not re-extracted**; this file exists only to record the
redundancy finding and the verification method, so a future worker does not re-attempt the same
~4,900-entry extraction. No new vocabulary rows are added — see `035b_manual_of_business_spanish_glossary_spanish_english_1.md`
through `035i_manual_of_business_spanish_glossary_english_spanish_4.md` for the complete content.
