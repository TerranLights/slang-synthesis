**Coverage-approach decision.** This is a pure A1/A2-level topic-vocabulary workbook — the great majority of its 60 units (greetings, numbers, days, body parts, basic food, basic clothing, the house, basic verbs, animals) duplicate ground this corpus already covers in much greater depth (Madrigal 003-004, Easy Spanish Step-by-Step 013, Practice Makes Perfect series 005-009d, Learning Spanish Like Crazy 010-012, and now 083's own 20-chapter thematic sweep). Per this project's non-redundant-supplement convention, extraction below concentrates on the units with genuine non-redundant value: **the grammar-adjacent vocabulary units** that teach structural relationships rather than flat noun lists — antonym pairs (Contrarios 1&2), the classic Spanish gender-exception trap words (Masculino o femenino), verb+preposition collocations (Verbos con preposiciones), and the semantic-categorization exercise (Cada oveja con su pareja). These are exactly the kind of "how the words relate to each other" content not well captured by flat glossary-style sources elsewhere in this corpus.

## Graded-series note (full comparison written up in 085)

This book's structure — 60 single-page, purely topical/foundational units with **no** dedicated idiom, proverb, false-friend, or colloquial-register unit anywhere in its 60-unit table of contents — stands in sharp contrast to companion volume 085 (Intermedio-Avanzado), whose own 60-unit TOC adds explicit units titled "Refranes" (proverbs), "Expresiones equivalentes," "Expresiones coloquiales," "Parecidos, pero diferentes" (near-synonym/false-friend discrimination), and "Reacciones" (idiomatic reaction phrases) — none of which have any counterpart in this elementary volume. See 085's closing summary for the full register-progression write-up, including the handful of topics (Animales, La televisión, El coche, Cada oveja con su pareja) that appear in both books and allow a direct side-by-side complexity comparison.
