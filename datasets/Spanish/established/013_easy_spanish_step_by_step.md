## Grammar points

No new grammar points captured — Chapters 1-3 (noun gender/definite-indefinite articles/adjective
agreement; ser vs. estar with subject pronouns incl. vosotros/ustedes regional-use note; hay;
interrogative words; days/months/seasons) duplicate material already fully captured in
`005_practice_makes_perfect_basic_spanish_grammar.md` and other established files. See the scope
note above for what was checked before this determination.

One incidental cross-reference worth flagging for the mechanics-analysis phase: this book's
subject-pronoun table explicitly states **vosotros** is "used only in Spain" and that Latin
Americans use **ustedes** for the plural of both **tú** and **Ud.** — consistent with, and a second
independent confirmation of, the voseo/vosotros regional split already flagged as a flagship finding
in `002_colloquial_spanish_latin_america_2.md`.
