# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: *Lecciones de la Conversación en Español* (sampled, vision-read)

**Source:** А.В. Коляева (A.V. Kolyaeva), *Lecciones de la Conversación en Español* (Russian
title: *Уроки разговора на испанском языке*), published St. Petersburg, KARO, 2005. ISBN
5-89815-530-9. 205 PDF pages (208 printed pages). **A Russian-authored, Russian-medium thematic
Spanish course aimed at secondary-school students (the book's own Russian preface specifies 6th-7th
grade pupils at specialized language schools, "школьники 6-7 класса"**, i.e. roughly ages
12-13) — a notably lower actual level than the "Nivel Avanzado"/intermediate-advanced folder
placement implies; its 12 thematic units (sports, medicine, Spanish festivals, the post office,
Spain's geography/cities, St. Petersburg [described in Spanish, for the Russian students' own
city], Latin America, the discovery of the Americas, pre-Columbian civilizations, bullfighting,
Spanish poets [García Lorca], shopping) mix authentic Spanish texts/dialogues with Russian-medium
exercises, translation drills, and glossaries, plus embedded songs/poems/proverbs/riddles the
preface says are meant as classroom warm-ups.

**Text layer / vision-reading note:** confirmed via `pdfimages -list` to be a bitonal (1-bit)
JBIG2 scan at 400dpi; the embedded OCR text layer is unusable (`pdftotext` output on sampled pages
came back as scrambled, in some cases apparently reversed-order, symbol noise — this source was
already correctly flagged vision-required in the original triage, and that classification is
confirmed correct). Target pages were rendered to 200dpi grayscale images with `pdftoppm` and
OCR'd with Tesseract's Spanish (`spa`) model (the same tool/model already used for `034`, `091`,
and `092` in this corpus). OCR quality on the Spanish-language text/dialogue portions was clean
and fully legible. Transcription Confidence is n/a and Vision Reading Confidence is verified for
every row below, per this corpus's OCR-recovery convention.

**Redundancy check:** one item from this sample, *turrón* (Christmas nougat), is already
individually catalogued in `038_speak_the_culture_spain.md` (p.276) and is therefore NOT
re-entered here. The book's holiday-festival content otherwise does not appear to duplicate
anything else yet in this corpus — in particular, its explicit comparison of Spanish festivals
with named Latin American counterparts (Bolivia's Alasita, Argentina's Fiesta de la Tradición)
is a distinctive angle not found in the existing phrasebook/culture-guide cluster (`038`-`041`).
The book's sports/medicine/geography vocabulary-list units were NOT sampled in depth, since a
quick check suggests they are largely generic topical word-lists of the kind already well covered
by the visual/student dictionaries (`049`-`052`) already in this corpus; this extraction instead
focuses on the two areas found to have genuinely distinctive content: the idiom/collocation
glosses embedded in the sports-unit dialogues, and the Fiestas de España unit's holiday-specific
vocabulary and cross-regional festival comparisons.

**Entry count:** 19 vocabulary/idiom/cultural rows, sampled from Unidad I ("Deportes") and Unidad
III ("Fiestas de España") only, out of 12 units/205 pages. Not an exhaustive pass.
