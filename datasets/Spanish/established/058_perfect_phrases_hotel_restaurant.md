# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Phrases: Perfect Phrases in Spanish for the Hotel and Restaurant Industries (full book)

**Source:** Jean Yates, *Perfect Phrases in Spanish for the Hotel and Restaurant Industries: 500+
Essential Words and Phrases for Communicating with Spanish-Speakers* (McGraw-Hill, 2008), 193 pp.
Employer-facing phrasebook for hospitality managers communicating with Spanish-speaking hotel/
restaurant staff. Seven chapters (1. Spanish Basics; 2. Establishing Policies — hotel entry/exit,
employee roles, dress codes, safety; 3. General Instructions — task sequencing, locations; 4.
Specific Tasks for Guest Room Attendants — room cleaning, bed-making, bathroom, guest interaction;
5. Specific Tasks for the Laundry Unit — washing/drying/pressing machines, stacking; 6. Specific
Instructions for Kitchen and Food Service Staff — food prep, cooking, baking, plating, dishwashing,
garbage, room service, banquet setup/service/cleanup; 7. Specific Tasks for the Indoor Maintenance
Team — public-area cleaning, repairs, pest control) plus a numbers appendix and bidirectional
English-Spanish/Spanish-English glossary.
