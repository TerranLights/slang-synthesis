# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Gramática práctica del español. Nivel elemental

**Source:** *Gramática práctica del español. Nivel elemental* (SM, scanned PDF, 115 pp.), a
Spanish-medium graded grammar-practice workbook organized as 50 two-page "Unidad" spreads
(A1–B1 range) plus five "Autoevaluación" self-test sections, each spread pairing a short grammar
explanation ("teoría") box with drill exercises. No extractable text layer (`pdftotext` returns
empty on every sampled page) — confirmed vision-required per the Wave 3 triage survey.

**Vision-reading approach:** read 27 of 115 pages (~23%) rather than exhaustively. Read the full
table of contents (pp. 3–5, listing all 50 units with their grammar points) to map the book's
structure, then sampled: Unit 1 (alphabet, p. 8) as a structural spot-check, and the explanatory
grammar box of every unit from Unit 33 through Unit 50 (pp. 78–117) — the book's upper-intermediate
half (imperative, obligation periphrases, future, probability/subjunctive, desire, conditional,
comparatives, superlatives, impersonal se, exclamatives, interjections, indefinites, and the full
past-tense system). Units 1–32 (articles, ser/estar, basic present-tense conjugation, possessives,
demonstratives, prepositions of place, gustar-type verbs) were **not** vision-read: the TOC made
clear this is pure elementary-level material already covered in depth by this corpus's existing
`005_practice_makes_perfect_basic_spanish_grammar.md` (50 parallel lessons covering the same
ground) and `003_madrigals_magic_key_part1.md`. A handful of pure-exercise pages (Autoevaluación 4
and 5, pp. 94, 112–117) were also sampled and confirmed to be drill-recombination of
already-captured material with no new vocabulary, so they were not transcribed.

**Non-redundant-supplement note:** checked against `005_practice_makes_perfect_basic_spanish_grammar.md`,
`006_practice_makes_perfect_advanced_spanish_grammar.md`, `008_practice_makes_perfect_spanish_verb_tenses.md`
(+ 008b/008c), and `003_madrigals_magic_key_part1.md`/`004_madrigals_magic_key_part2.md` before writing.
Core conjugation paradigms (futuro, condicional, subjuntivo, pretérito perfecto/indefinido/imperfecto)
substantially overlap with 006/008 and are captured here only where this book adds a genuinely new
angle. What is distinctive and new to this corpus:
- **Voseo forms given systematically alongside tú/usted in every paradigm table** (hablá/comé/escribí
  imperatives, hablás/comés present, etc.) — no prior Spanish established/ file documents the vos
  paradigm this consistently across imperative, present, future, and conditional. Flagged as a
  register/dialect finding.
- The **ojalá (que) / así + subjuntivo** distinction (así + subjunctive = negative wish/threat, not a
  plain wish) — not found elsewhere in this corpus.
- The **quizás/posiblemente/probablemente/sin duda/tal vez (+ indicative OR subjunctive) vs. a lo mejor
  (+ indicative ONLY, never subjunctive)** contrast — a specific usage trap not spelled out elsewhere.
- The **full interjection inventory** organized by communicative function (surprise, calling attention,
  pain, disinterest, encouragement, asking for help, tiredness) — a flagship vocabulary find for this
  book; no prior established/ file has interjections organized this way.
- The **impersonal/general-subject system** (uno, tú genérico, la gente, todo el mundo, dicen que, se
  impersonal) collected as one paradigm.
- Ordinal numbers 1st–10th with the primer/tercer apocope rule.
