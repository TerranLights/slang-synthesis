## Grammar points

### The gustar-verb family extended: encantar, fascinar, interesar, molestar, caer bien/mal

Lessons 49-50 round out the "gustar-type" verb class begun in Lección 48, all sharing the same
inverted-subject structure (grammatical subject = the thing/person causing the reaction; the
experiencer appears as an indirect-object clitic): encantar (to love/be delighted by), fascinar (to
fascinate), interesar (to interest), molestar (to bother), and the phrasal caer bien/caer mal (to
like/dislike a *person*, explicitly distinguished by the instructor from romantic "gustar"). The word
order for all of these is shown as flexible — "le fascina la primavera" or "la primavera le fascina,"
"me cae bien Pedro" or "Pedro me cae bien" — with the instructor stating this explicitly for both
fascinar and caer bien. All five are drilled across present, imperfect ("me encantaba," "les
molestaba"), and conditional ("me encantaría") to show the pattern holds across tenses, not just the
present. (Lección 49, throughout; Lección 50, throughout.)
