# Spanish — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: *Prisma Comienza: Nivel A1*

**Source:** *Prisma Comienza: Método de Español para Extranjeros, Nivel A1* (Editorial Edinumen,
alumno/student edition). 152-page PDF, confirmed **no text layer at all** (`pdftotext` returns
empty across sampled pages) — vision-required classification from the triage is correct.
Companion A1-level volume to *Prisma Progresa: Nivel B1*, already extracted in Wave 4 as
`094_prisma_progresa_b1.md` — same Prisma series, same publisher (Edinumen), same
four-column-per-unit table-of-contents format (Contenidos funcionales / gramaticales / léxicos /
culturales), confirmed via the índice de contenidos pages.

**Companion-volume relationship to 094:** same pedagogical apparatus as 094 (PRISMA del alumno /
PRISMA del profesor split, 12 teaching units + 2 review units ≈ 80 class hours), but this A1 volume
starts from true zero — alphabet, ser/tener/trabajar/llamarse in the present, numbers 0-101 — where
094's B1 volume assumed a working intermediate base. **As with 094, this book's grammar
progression (present-tense regulars/irregulars, reflexives, comparatives, gustar-type verbs,
negation, the gerund, ir a + infinitive) is thoroughly redundant** with this corpus's existing
beginner-grammar cluster and was not re-extracted. What is genuinely distinctive — and, being a
different book covering different units from 094's own non-redundant B1 sample, not duplicated by
094 — is this book's dense run of "Contenidos culturales" items: several units carry explicit
stereotype/counter-stereotype content (Unit 5's "estereotipos sobre España y los españoles," Unit
10's "Rompiendo tópicos: no todos somos toreros ni flamencas" and its regional-stereotype reading
"Así somos"), gesture vocabulary, and country-specific festival/gastronomy notes (Mexico, Guatemala).

**Coverage approach:** full índice de contenidos read (all 12 units + 2 revision units mapped).
Representative sampling of three units' culture content: Unit 5 ("Los horarios, costumbres y
estereotipos sobre España y los españoles"), Unit 6 ("Gastronomía española," "El ocio de la
juventud española"), and Unit 10 ("Estereotipos de los habitantes de las comunidades autónomas,"
"Romper tópicos sobre España," agreement/disagreement phrase bank). Two flagged culture-box topics
from the table of contents — Unit 5's "Literatura: Juan José Millás" and "El lenguaje no verbal" —
were searched for but **not found printed in the pages vision-read for this sample**; per this
corpus's established convention (cf. 094's identical finding for two of its own TOC-flagged boxes),
this is noted rather than silently dropped or fabricated — these may be PRISMA del profesor
(teacher's-edition) content, or may simply fall outside the specific page ranges sampled here.
