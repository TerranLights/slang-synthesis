## What this book is

**Third title in this wave from the same "Lola Lago, detective" series** as *Poderoso caballero*
(established/115) — same authors (Lourdes Miquel / Neus Sans), same Difusión imprint, same diary-entry
format and recurring cast (detective Lola Lago and her partners Paco and Miguel, secretary Margarita,
errand-boy Feliciano). This installment is marked **"Nivel 1"** (easier reading level than *Poderoso
caballero*, which carries no such marking and reads as more advanced), consistent with shorter
sentences and simpler vocabulary observed in the sampled text.

**Plot/setting:** Madrid-based detective agency investigates whether the paintings of a supposedly
famous surrealist painter, "Urpiano," are forgeries — raising the possibility that "Urpiano" never
existed as a real person at all. The investigation moves from Madrid to **Figueres, Cadaqués, Port
Lligat, and Rosas** on Catalonia's Costa Brava — all real towns associated with Salvador Dalí, who
appears as a constant point of reference/comparison throughout (the Dalí Museum in Figueres, Dalí's
former home at Port Lligat). Diary dates run Oct. 15-24 (year unstated in sampled text, but the 1978
reference points plus a later euro/peseta note at footnote 36 suggest pre-2002 setting, consistent with
*Poderoso caballero*'s early-1990s era for the same series).
