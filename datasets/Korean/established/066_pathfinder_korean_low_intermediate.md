**Morpheme breakdown (sample agglutinative forms encountered).**

> **가셨습니까?** = 가- (go, verb stem) + -시- (subject-honorific) + -었- (past) + -습니까 (formal
> interrogative ending) — "Did you go?" (honorific). L13 telephone/직장 register context;
> illustrates the honorific-tense-mood stacking typical of formal registers throughout this book.

> **드리겠습니까?** = 드리- (honorific "give," suppletive for 주다) + -겠- (future/intention) +
> -습니까 (formal interrogative) — "Shall I give [it] to you?" L13's 도움-offering pattern
> (-어/아 드릴까요?).

> **먹으면서** = 먹- (eat, stem) + -으면서 (simultaneous-action connective, "while ~ing") — L15's
> target grammar point; epenthetic 으 inserted because the stem ends in a consonant.

## Grammar points

Confirmed against the book's own back-matter grammar index (문법, p.196) — 15 lessons, roughly
two to three grammar points each plus one or two functional-expression sets. Listed by lesson.
Points whose territory substantially overlaps already-documented basic Korean grammar
(established/001-029) are paraphrased briefly; points with distinctive explanation or example
material are given fuller treatment.

### Lesson 1 (첫 출근 / First Day at Work)
- **-(이)라고 하다** (reported/quotative speech — "X is called/said to be Y").
- **-에 대해서** ("about/regarding X").
- **-(으)러 가다/오다** (purpose connective with motion verbs — "go/come in order to").
- **-(으)ㄴ지 ... 이/가 되다/지나다** (elapsed-time construction — "it has been [time] since...").

### Lesson 2 (일상 생활 / Daily Life)
- Basic daily-routine description patterns (overlaps prior coverage; not deeply re-derived here).

### Lesson 3 (주말 / Weekend)
- 귀가 가렵다/아프다/얇다 idiom family (see Vocabulary/idiom table below).

### Lesson 4 (계획 / Plans)
- **-고 있다 / -고 있었다** (present/past progressive).
- **-겠습니다** (first-person intention/formal future).
- **아마 -(으)ㄹ 겁니다 / 아마 -었/았을 겁니다** (probabilistic future/past — "it's probably the
  case that...").

### Lesson 5 (은행과 생활 / Banking and Life)
- **-(으)ㄹ줄 알다/모르다** (know/don't know how to do X).
- **-는 동안** ("while/during the time that...").
- Sequential-action **-고** used in step-by-step instruction contexts (ATM procedure dialogue).

### Lesson 6 (요리 / Cooking)
- **Classifier/counter words**: 병(bottle), 통(container/head of cabbage), 봉지(bag), 송이(bunch/
  cluster, e.g. of flowers or bananas), 상자(box), 킬로그램(kilogram), 판(tray/flat, e.g. of eggs).
- **-(으)로 만들다** ("to make [something] out of/with X").
- **-고 나서** ("after doing X").
- **Relativizer set**: -는 N (present), -(으)ㄴ N (past), -(으)ㄹ N (future) attaching to a following
  noun — the book treats these as a paradigm triple rather than teaching each in isolation, which
  is a clean presentation worth noting for the mechanics-analysis phase.

### Lesson 7 (건강 / Health)
- **N에 좋다/나쁘다** ("good/bad for N").
- **-도록 하세요** (soft imperative/recommendation — "please make sure to...").
- Frequency adverbs: 자주(often)/거의(rarely, lit. "almost [not]").
- **-(으)ㄹ 때** ("when/at the time that...").
- 손이 크다/모자라다, 손을 잡다 idiom family.

### Lesson 8 (집 / House)
- **-지만** (contrastive "but/although").
- **그런데** (discourse topic-shift/contrast connective, taught explicitly as a distinct
  conjunctive adverb rather than folded into -지만).
- **-(으)ㄴ/는데** (background-setting connective).
- **-(으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ 것 같다** (seems/appears to be the case).
- 전세/월세 housing-system vocabulary (see Vocabulary table).

### Lesson 9 (만남 / Meeting/Encounter)
- **-기로 하다** ("to decide/agree to do X").
- 바람을 맞다/쐬다/피우다 idiom family.

### Lesson 10 (인상 / Impressions)
- **-이/가 아니라** ("it's not X, but rather...").
- **-게 생기다** appearance-description pattern (see Vocabulary table).

### Lesson 11 (경험 / Experience)
- **-었/았을 때** (past-time "when").
- **-어/아 보다 / -어/아 보세요** (experiential "try doing X" / "please try doing X").
- **-(으)ㄴ 적이 있다/없다** (experiential "have/haven't ever done X").
- 초반/중반/후반, 초순/중순/하순 period-division vocabulary.
- 눈감아 주다, 눈이 높다, 눈이 캄캄하다 idiom family.

### Lesson 12 (여행 / Travel)
- **-(으)로 해서** ("by way of/via X," route-specifying).
- **N 중** ("in the middle of/currently doing N" — see the status-board vocabulary set).
- **-(으)려면** ("if one intends to/wants to...").
- **-자마자** ("as soon as...").

### Lesson 13 (도움 / Help/Assistance)
- **-(으)시겠습니까?** (formal honorific "will you...?").
- **-어/아 드릴까요?** ("shall I do X for you?" — benefactive offer).
- **N 때문에 / 왜냐하면 -기 때문이다** (cause/reason, both the particle and the clause-final form
  taught together).
- **-거든요** (informative/explanatory sentence-final particle, "you see, it's that...").
- 코앞에 닥치다, 코에 붙이다, 콧대가 높다 idiom family.

### Lesson 14 (학교 생활 / School Life)
- **-어/아도 괜찮다** ("it's okay even if...").
- **-(으)면 안 되다** ("must not/it's not okay if...").
- **-었었** (double-past/discontinuative past — marks a past state explicitly no longer true,
  distinguished from simple -었 past). Example context: reminiscing about an ended friendship/
  period, explicitly contrasted with simple past in the book's own explanation.
- **-던** (retrospective modifier — recalls a past habitual/ongoing state one personally
  witnessed). Drilled heavily in both the Student Book practice and the (skipped) Workbook.

### Lesson 15 (초대 / Invitation)
- **-(으)면서** (simultaneous-action connective, "while doing X and Y at once").
- **-(으)ㄴ/는 걸 보니까 -(으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ 모양이다** (inferential evidential — "judging by the
  fact that X, it seems/must be that Y"). Taught as a matched pair (evidence clause + inference
  clause) rather than -는 모양이다 alone.
- **반말, 낮춤말 (banmal / low-speech register)** — an explicit, dedicated lesson section on
  converting formal (-습니다/-어요) speech into casual 반말 for close friends/children, with
  worked before/after dialogue pairs. This is a direct, hands-on register-conversion drill distinct
  from the honorific-system theory already covered in established/031 (*Korean Honorifics and
  Politeness*) — it's pedagogical practice material for the formal→casual direction specifically.
- 바가지를 긁다/쓰다/씌우다 idiom family.
