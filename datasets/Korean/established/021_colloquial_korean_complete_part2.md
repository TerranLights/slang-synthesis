**Culture point (paraphrased, not quoted):** Korean currency is the won (₩); coins in
denominations 1/5/10/50/100/500 and bills in 1,000/5,000/10,000. Unlike American/European
practice, Koreans do not commonly use personal checks — cash is the default for daily
transactions, which the source frames as the reason cash-handling vocabulary (짜리, 현금을 찾다)
is front-loaded in this unit.

**Culture point (paraphrased):** foreign travelers can seek care at general hospitals such as
Severance Hospital or Asian Medical Center (English-speaking staff); emergency visits may involve
hours of waiting; a 24-hour emergency ambulance service (Asia Emergency Assistance) exists for
foreigners; Korean pharmacists are legally permitted to prescribe medicine directly based on a
patient's described symptoms, without a doctor's visit, for minor ailments.

**Grammar point — noun suffix -ㄹ(을) 정도로 ("to the extent that..."):** attaches to a noun,
expressing the degree to which a first clause's verb affects the subject. Morpheme example: 죽을
정도로 머리가 아파요 "I have a headache to the extent that I could die" (죽- "die" + -을 [future
modifier] + 정도 "degree" + -로 [instrumental/manner particle]).
