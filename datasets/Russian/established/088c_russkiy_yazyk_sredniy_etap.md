## Grammar points

### Полная и краткая форма страдательных причастий (Full and short forms of passive participles) — pp. 44–45

Standard formation (прочитанный → прочитан/-а/-о/-ы, построенный → построен/-а/-о/-ы, открытый →
открыт/-а/-о/-ы) and the full-form-as-attributive vs. short-form-as-predicate distinction. Already
well covered elsewhere in this corpus (`002/003`, `006/007`, `012/013`); no new exception or
paradigm found here. Skipped per non-redundant-supplement scoping.

### Reading-text content (paraphrased)

- **"Певец России"** (pp. 41–43) — biographical piece on singer-songwriter Игорь Тальков
  (1956–1991), assassinated; text discusses his life, his archive/self-education, and his songs'
  civic/protest themes. Supplies the death-euphemism vocabulary cluster above.
- Lyric excerpt from a Tal'kov song dedicated to Виктор Цой (Viktor Tsoi, another musician who died
  young) is quoted briefly (4 lines, illustrative only, per copyright discipline) — poetic treatment
  of death as departure/messenger imagery, echoing the "уйти из жизни" euphemism.
- **"Наши песни"** (pp. 51–52), plus two folk/popular song texts ("Динь-динь-динь", "Русское поле",
  pp. 53–54) — reading texts about Russian song culture; incidental vocabulary is common-core and
  not separately tabulated beyond the pre-text box above.
