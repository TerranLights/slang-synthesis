## Grammar points

### ВЕСЬ-ЦЕЛЫЙ-ЦЕЛЬНЫЙ: pronoun/adjective boundary, no comment block

Like the earlier ЕДИНСТВЕННЫЙ-ЕДИНЫЙ-... group, this triple mixes a true pronoun (ВЕСЬ, a
determiner declining like a pronoun with irregular forms — весь/вся/всё/все) with two ordinary
adjectives (ЦЕЛЫЙ, ЦЕЛЬНЫЙ) and receives no opening `Комментарий` — the discrimination must again be
reconstructed from the drill exercises' worked minimal pairs (e.g. целая бутылка молока [an
unopened, full bottle — ЦЕЛЫЙ's "undiminished quantity" sense] vs. цельное молоко [whole/
unprocessed milk — ЦЕЛЬНЫЙ's unrelated "unprocessed/undivided substance" sense], a genuine false-
friend trap for learners since цельный and целый are historically cognate but have diverged sharply
in collocational range).

### НАСТОЯЩИЙ-ДЕЙСТВИТЕЛЬНЫЙ-ПОДЛИННЫЙ-ИСТИННЫЙ: a four-way "genuine/real" cluster with a clean
register axis

This is one of the cleanest register-differentiated clusters in the book: all four share the core
"actually X, not a fake/pretense" meaning, but НАСТОЯЩИЙ is the register-neutral/colloquial default
while ДЕЙСТВИТЕЛЬНЫЙ, ПОДЛИННЫЙ, and ИСТИННЫЙ lean bookish/formal — and the book's comment further
subdivides which pairs converge on which specific sub-sense (ДЕЙСТВИТЕЛЬНЫЙ+ПОДЛИННЫЙ on
"actually existed"; НАСТОЯЩИЙ+ПОДЛИННЫЙ+ИСТИННЫЙ on "the ideal example of its kind"), with
ДЕЙСТВИТЕЛЬНЫЙ carrying sole rights to describing actual states-of-affairs/life-facts. НАСТОЯЩИЙ's
sense 5 (tagged `неодобр.`, "a real idiot/fool") is a further register-marked polysemy split within
one headword, continuing the pattern already seen with ГОРДЫЙ, ЧЕЛОВЕЧИЙ, КРОВНЫЙ, and ВЫСОКИЙ/НИЗКИЙ
in earlier shards.

### НАСТОЯЩИЙ-НАТУРАЛЬНЫЙ-ЕСТЕСТВЕННЫЙ: entry opens, continues into next shard

This new group's opening comment establishes НАТУРАЛЬНЫЙ as carrying a terminological/labeling
register even where it's a plain synonym of НАСТОЯЩИЙ — a useful further data point on Russian's
neutral-vs-technical-register split for material/foodstuff adjectives (paralleling the earlier
-ичн-/-ическ- suffix contrast from `066c`/`066d`, though here it's lexical rather than
morphological). ЕСТЕСТВЕННЫЙ's own entry and discrimination are not yet reached in this page range.
