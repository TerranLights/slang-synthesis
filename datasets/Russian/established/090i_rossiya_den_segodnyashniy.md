## Grammar points

### Recurring "весомый/весомость" (weightiness) metaphor family

This chapter's «весомость социального положения» directly echoes Chapter 1's «весомый» аргумент — both use the physical-weight adjective весомый/весомость metaphorically for social/argumentative importance. Two independent attestations across different chapters/authors confirm this is a stable, lexicalized metaphor in Russian journalistic register, not a one-off coinage — a good candidate for a productive "weight = importance" metaphor mapping in the eventual conlang.

### The "как бы" hedge particle

Illustrated here modifying a noun ("как бы семья," "a sort-of/as-if family") rather than a verb or clause, showing this discourse particle's broad syntactic reach in colloquial Russian — it can hedge nouns, adjectives, and whole clauses alike. Worth tracking as one of the most productive colloquial hedging devices in the corpus so far (cf. "мол," Chapter 2, a different but related discourse-particle category — reported speech rather than hedging).
