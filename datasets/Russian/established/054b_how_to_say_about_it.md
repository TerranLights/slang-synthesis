## Grammar points

### Layered affirmation strategies (§1, pp. 21–29)

The book treats "yes" as a graded space rather than a single word. The simplest strategy is bare
predicate-repetition ("Вы любите музыку?" — "Люблю."), which the source frames as the default,
register-neutral option (p. 22–23). Degree-words (очень, достаточно, вполне) answer questions about
intensity/sufficiency/match-to-requirement rather than mere yes/no, and the book works through a
three-way contrast (большой? — большой / очень / достаточно) to show each answers a subtly different
implicit question (size itself, degree of size, or size-relative-to-purpose; pp. 24–26). A distinct
cluster of fixed phrases (о чём речь, разумеется, конечно, а как же, ещё бы, ещё бы не + inf, а как
тут было не + inf, то есть) all affirm by asserting *self-evidence* — that the fact could not possibly
be otherwise — differing mainly in how emphatic/rhetorical they are (pp. 26–29).

### Layered negation strategies (§2, pp. 30–68)

The negation inventory is organized by how strong/blunt the disagreement is, and — repeatedly — by
formality: several clusters are explicitly flagged by the source as informal-register-only, unsuitable
for an official conversation (Скажешь тоже!/Здравствуйте! p. 37; the "resentful counter-question" group
Почему?/С чего ты взял? p. 39; the ironic-dismissal group Ещё чего!/Делать мне больше нечего! p. 45;
the rude-refusal group Не суй нос не в своё дело! p. 68 carries an explicit in-text warning against
official use). Отнюдь sits at the opposite end, flagged as literary/educated register (p. 32). A
separate strategy negates not by denying the fact but by denying its *preconditions*
("нечего/некого/не в чем" + infinitive, "причин/повода + inf нет" — p. 33): "мне не в чем сомневаться"
= there is nothing here that would give me grounds to doubt.

### Hedged and inferential answers (§4–5, pp. 53–63)

Two distinct hedge families are distinguished. One (как вам сказать, пожалуй, кажется, вроде бы)
marks the speaker as still searching for the right formulation, or unsure of a half-remembered fact
(pp. 55–58). The other (скорее всего, надо думать, должно быть, видимо, вероятно, наверное vs. вряд
ли, едва ли, не думаю) marks a confident *inference* about whether an as-yet-unconfirmed event will or
won't occur — this is not memory-uncertainty but probabilistic judgment (p. 55, framed table).
Separately, видите ли / знаете / тут вот какое дело mark not uncertainty but *interpersonal* stance
while explaining: seeking mutual understanding, confiding, or registering the speaker's own mild
confusion respectively (p. 61).

### Evasive answers and outright refusal (§6–7, pp. 64–70)

An evasive answer keeps the grammatical form of a real answer but empties it of the requested content,
replacing a specific noun with an indefinite "один/одна/одно + N" phrase (так, с одним своим знакомым;
да так, в одном киоске) — the source notes the particle это, when present in the original question,
marks the fact asked about as either directly observed or unusually salient to the asker (p. 65–66). A
genuine refusal to answer, by contrast, is organized into four escalating registers: (1) downplaying
relevance/importance (Неважно, А не всё ли равно?); (2) deflecting to whose business it is (Это не
наше дело, Нас это не касается); (3) the same deflection applied to a third party rather than the
speaker; (4) a direct, informal-only rebuke of the asker themselves (А тебе-то какое дело?!, Не суй
нос не в своё дело!) — the source explicitly warns the fourth register out of official contexts (p.
68).
