## Grammar points

### ЗЛОЙ/ЗЛОБНЫЙ/ЗЛОСТНЫЙ: a three-way intensity/deliberateness discrimination

A genuinely useful three-way split for later slang-mechanics comparison: ЗЛОЙ is the broad,
register-neutral "ill-natured/mean" term; ЗЛОБНЫЙ intensifies it and adds a gloating/malicious-
pleasure component; ЗЛОСТНЫЙ shifts the axis entirely from emotional disposition to *deliberateness
of a harmful act* (often in legal/administrative register — "злостный неплательщик" = "willful
defaulter"). This is a clean three-tier model (breadth/neutral → intensity/emotional → intent/legal)
that could usefully inform how the conlang-synthesis phase might model a Russian-style near-synonym
cluster around a "bad/harmful" concept.

### ДЛИННЫЙ/ДЛИТЕЛЬНЫЙ/ДОЛГИЙ/ПРОДОЛЖИТЕЛЬНЫЙ: register note

Per this entry's own comment, this four-way "long/lengthy" cluster splits along a register axis
rather than a purely semantic one: ДЛИТЕЛЬНЫЙ leans formal/scientific (paired with deverbal nouns and
institutional contexts — отпуск, период, эксплуатация), ДЛИННЫЙ and ДОЛГИЙ lean colloquial (with
ДОЛГИЙ specifically carrying emotional coloring the neutral ДЛИННЫЙ lacks), and ПРОДОЛЖИТЕЛЬНЫЙ
occupies a formal/journalistic register niche of its own (продолжительные аплодисменты is a
newspaper-report cliché). This is one of the richest register-differentiated near-synonym clusters
in the book so far and is directly relevant to slang-mechanics register mapping.
