## Grammar points

**Particle taxonomy by function** (the section's organizing structure): утвердительные
(affirmative: да, так, конечно), отрицательные (negative: не, ни, вовсе не), вопросительные
(interrogative: ли, разве, неужели), усилительные (emphatic/intensifying: же, ведь, даже, и, ни),
восклицательные (exclamatory: что за, как), указательные (demonstrative: вот, вон), and
определительно-уточняющие (specifying/clarifying: именно, как раз, точно). This taxonomy is a
genuinely useful organizing frame not previously captured from a Russian source in this project
(prior particle coverage was organized by orthography or by individual particle, not by this
semantic-function grid).

**Colloquial-register particles, explicitly flagged by the source.** мол and дескать are named
directly as characteristic of разговорная речь (colloquial speech): both function as **quotative/
reported-speech markers**, roughly "he/she said, allegedly, supposedly" — used to distance the
speaker from a quoted claim (Он говорит, мол, я тут ни при чём "He says he's not to blame,
apparently" — inserting mol signals the speaker isn't vouching for the claim). -ка softens an
imperative into a more casual, self-directed or friendly suggestion (сходи-ка "why don't you go,"
дай-ка "let me just..."). эк is a colloquial exclamatory particle expressing surprise or mild
reproach at an intensity ("look how..." / "well I never..."). These three (мол, дескать, -ка, plus
эк) are exactly the kind of register-marked discourse particle this project's slang-mechanics
analysis phase needs — quotative distancing and imperative-softening are productive mechanisms that
could map directly onto invented in-universe slang.

**именно vs. как раз, semantic near-synonyms in the specifying/clarifying class.** Both single out
an exact match ("precisely, exactly"), but как раз leans toward coincidence/fit ("just right, as it
happens") while именно leans toward emphatic identification ("that one specifically, none other").

**разве vs. неужели, both interrogative-doubt particles.** разве questions an assumption more
neutrally/informationally; неужели expresses stronger surprise or incredulity at the same
proposition — a genuine intensity gradient within the same functional slot.

**-то, -нибудь, кое- indefinite-marking particles** (cross-referenced from `077`'s Section 3
neopredelённые-mestoimeniya chapter, which explicitly defers this discussion to Section 6): -то
marks a referent unknown to the *speaker* (кто-то "someone [I don't know who]"); -нибудь marks a
referent unspecified/arbitrary from either party's perspective, often future/hypothetical
(кто-нибудь "anyone/someone-or-other"); кое- marks a referent known to the *speaker* but
deliberately withheld or left vague for the listener (кое-кто "a certain someone [I could name but
won't]"). This three-way epistemic split (unknown-to-speaker / unspecified-to-both / known-but-
withheld) is a precise semantic distinction not previously this explicit in prior extractions and
is directly relevant to slang/register mechanics (кое- in particular carries an insinuating,
knowing tone useful for coded/slang speech).
