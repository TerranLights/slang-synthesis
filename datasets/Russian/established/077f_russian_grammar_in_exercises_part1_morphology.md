### Grammar points

**Defective 1st-person-singular paradigm, closed list.** A small set of verbs cannot form an
ordinary 1st person singular present/future form at all: убеждать/убедить, победить, стонать/
застонать, ослабевать, вредить/навредить, and related pairs — speakers must periphrase (я смогу
убедить, я сумею победить) rather than *убежу/побежу. A compact, citable closed list not gathered
in this form from prior Russian sources.

**лазить as the colloquial variant of лазать.** Both mean "to climb around, clamber" but лазить
(лажу, лазишь, лазит) is explicitly marked as the colloquial-register variant, and — unusually —
has **no imperative form** at all (a genuine paradigm gap, not just dispreference).

**-ся postfix, four-way functional split** (brief context, already covered in more depth in
`058`): reflexive proper (действие направлено на себя: одеваться), reciprocal (действие
направлено друг на друга: встречаться), passive/mediopassive (дверь открывается), and a residual
class where -ся is lexicalized with no compositional reflexive meaning (смеяться, надеяться,
бояться — these never occur without -ся). -ся attaches after a consonant (including й); -сь after
a vowel. Verbs with -ся cannot take a direct object in the accusative/genitive without a
preposition — a governing constraint worth flagging for any invented-slang verb-derivation
mechanism that reuses -ся.

**Prefix + motion-verb directionality, brief summary** (already comprehensively covered in `028`
and `057`; kept only as cross-reference): в-/во- = entry (movement into an enclosed space);
вы- = exit; при- = arrival at a goal (does not combine with брести/бродить, "wander" verbs, since
those lack a defined endpoint); у- = departure with an implication of long/indefinite absence;
с-(со-) = a round trip or "vanish/relocate" sense, and does not combine with в-/за-.

## Grammar-Tables appendix (pp.401-424) — spot-check note, not transcribed

The back matter includes a systematic "Грамматические таблицы" (Grammar Tables) reference: a
preposition-by-preposition table giving each preposition's case government, semantic function(s),
and an example, covering roughly 30 prepositions (без, благодаря, в, вдоль, внутри, вместо, вокруг,
вопреки, для, до, за, из, из-за, из-под, к, кроме, между, мимо, на, над, and others through the end
of the alphabet). Spot-checked pp.401-405; confirmed this is a compact, systematic restatement of
preposition/case-government territory already established across `002/003`, `006/007`, and `065`
(the dedicated case-semantics reference) — not transcribed in full, per this project's copyright
discipline against bulk-reproducing a reference table. No further sampling of the remaining ~19
pages was done; if a future pass wants exhaustive preposition-table coverage, pp.405-424 remain
unread.
