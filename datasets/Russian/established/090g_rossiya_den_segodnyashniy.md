## Chapter 7 — «Забудьте шесть соток!» ("Forget the Six Hundred Square Meters!")

Printed pp. 50–53 (PDF pp. 53–55). Essay on the Soviet-era "dacha"/allotment-garden (шесть соток) phenomenon, its Khrushchev-era social-control origins, the Brazilian loanword «фазенда», and its post-Soviet decline versus "New Russian" (новорусский) excess. Source note: adapted from K. Krylov, *Огонёк*, No. 24, 2004. One of the richest chapters so far for Soviet-era institutional vocabulary and one confirmed slang loanword (**кайф**).

## Grammar points

### Metalinguistic self-commentary as a source-internal register signal

This chapter is unusual among those extracted so far for containing the source *text's own* explicit linguistic commentary — identifying "фазенда" as (per the original author) Russian's only Portuguese loanword, and opening the whole chapter by calling "дача" untranslatable. This kind of author-supplied metalinguistic flag is worth watching for across the rest of the book: it's a stronger, more explicit signal of register/loanword status than this project's own extractor judgment, and should be weighted accordingly in the mechanics-analysis phase.
