## Grammar points

### Eliciting and attributing an opinion, seeking agreement (В §§1–3, pp. 71–80)

The book distinguishes three related question-families that are easy to conflate: (1) asking a
neutral opinion (Как вы думаете...?, Как по-вашему...? — the speaker has no stated view of their own
yet, p. 71–74); (2) attributing an already-formed opinion to a specific person, first or third, using
a parallel set of по-моему/по его мнению-type markers whose word-order flexibility the source
specifically illustrates — the marker can sit sentence-initially, sentence-medially, or
sentence-finally without changing the meaning (p. 74); and (3) a *tag-question* strategy (Правда?,
Не так ли?, Верно?) that seeks the interlocutor's agreement with an opinion the speaker has *already*
voiced, functioning as a bid to find "a like-minded person" rather than to gather new information (p.
75). A further distinct pair contrasts a genuinely neutral question ("Как ты считаешь, мы сможем
съездить в Петербург?") against a leading one built on negation ("Ты не считаешь, что нам обязательно
надо съездить в Петербург?") — the latter already embeds the speaker's own position and asks only for
confirmation (pp. 78–79); this negated-question-as-confirmation-seeking pattern mirrors the
hope/apprehension use of negated questions already documented in §2 of Раздел I.А (see 054).

### Asking about and expressing an impression (Г §§1–2, pp. 81–90)

A first family of impression-questions (Как вам наш город?, Как тебе понравился...?, Как ему
спектакль?) simply asks what impression a referent made; a second, distinct, family (Ну и как вам
там?, И как ей тут?) instead asks how a person is *faring* within an ongoing situation or place — the
book is explicit that these are not interchangeable (pp. 82–84). Responses to impression-questions
form a genuine intensity scale running from "extremely good" (Нет слов!, Более чем!, О! Это Х!) through
graded-positive (Не то слово!, хороший), neutral/unremarkable (Х как Х, ничего особенного), mild-
negative (так себе, не слишком), down to sarcastic-extremely-bad (Тоже мне Х!, Одно название, что Х!,
С позволения сказать Х!) — the book's own framed table on p. 87 explicitly labels this last cluster
"= очень плохая" (p. 86–87). The scale recurs with the same graded structure when asking specifically
about tastes/preferences in §3 (p. 92–94): Ещё как! (extreme positive) down through hedged partial
liking (Не то чтобы люблю, но... / Любить не люблю, но...) to Терпеть не могу (extreme negative), with
Скажешь тоже!/Да ты что?! marking indignant rejection of the very premise that the speaker could like
the thing in question.
