## Grammar points

**Adverb taxonomy by function.** образа действия (manner), места (place), времени (time), причины
(cause), цели (purpose), меры и степени (measure/degree), plus a predicative subclass. Standard
categorization, already established elsewhere, kept only as organizing context for the finds below.

**Colloquial-register adverb marking, explicit in the source.** The source itself explicitly flags
a set of adverbs as belonging to разговорный стиль (colloquial style): легонько (gently, diminutive
of легко), тихонько (quietly, diminutive of тихо), частенько (quite often, diminutive of часто).

**Productive reduplication as an intensifying derivational device.** A genuinely productive
morphological pattern for intensification, directly relevant to slang/register mechanics: adjective/
adverb root doubled, the first copy in the instrumental-like -ым/-им intensifier form, the second
bare — белым-бело (dazzlingly white), чёрным-черно (pitch black), давным-давно (a very long time
ago), полным-полно (absolutely packed/full), еле-еле (very barely, simple reduplication without the
instrumental element), крепко-накрепко (firmly and securely, with a на- prefix on the second
element), строго-настрого (very strictly, same на- pattern), перво-наперво (first and foremost,
same pattern). This -ым/-им + bare-root and на- + root reduplication device recurs across at least
three morphologically distinct sub-patterns and is worth flagging as a mechanism in its own right
for the mechanics-analysis phase.

**Instrumental-case simile/figurative construction ("shaped/behaving like X").** A distinct
grammatical device: the standard of comparison appears in the instrumental case rather than as a
как-phrase, to describe a body part's shape or a manner of moving — хвост картошкой (a curled,
potato-shaped tail), хвост колесом (a tail curled like a wheel), нос крючком (a hooked nose, lit.
"nose by-hook"), борода клинышком (a small wedge-shaped beard), борода лопатой (a broad, spade-
shaped beard), ноги циркулем (bow-legged, lit. "legs like a compass"), грудь колесом (chest puffed
out, lit. "chest like a wheel"), слёзы ручьём (tears streaming, lit. "tears like a stream"). The
same instrumental-standard construction extends to manner-of-motion verb collocations: хлынуть
стрелой (rush like an arrow), вылететь пулей (shoot out like a bullet), упасть камнем (drop like a
stone), жить отшельником (live like a hermit), летать соколом (fly like a falcon), смотреть волком
(glower, lit. "look like a wolf"), свернуться калачиком (curl up [in bed], lit. "curl up like a
kalach loaf" — a very common everyday idiom for curling into a ball), выть волком (howl like a
wolf, i.e., be in a wretched state).

**уже vs. ещё, aspectual-discourse distinction.** уже marks that a state/action has begun or a
threshold has been crossed ("already"); ещё marks that a state persists or a threshold has not yet
been reached ("still" / "not yet," combined with не). The source gives a diagram: for a given event
with a start and end point, ещё не X = before the start; X (bare) = ongoing; уже X = the event has
started; уже не X = after the end. A special combinatorial rule: with time-quantity expressions,
ещё means "as long ago as/still as far back as" while уже means "as soon as/already by" (Я узнал об
этом ещё во вторник "I found out about it as far back as Tuesday" vs. Я узнал об этом уже во
вторник "I found out about it already by Tuesday"). ничуть is explicitly footnoted by the source as
a colloquial-only synonym of нисколько ("not at all," used only in negation).

**тоже vs. также, subject-scope distinction.** тоже marks that the *same* action/state recurs
across *different* subjects (Мы пригласили гостей, они тоже пригласили гостей — a different party
also invited guests); также, in the default case, marks repetition of the action by the *same*
subject, roughly equivalent to "kроме того" (in addition to that). Under subject-fronting/inversion,
this distinction can neutralize and both become usable as near-synonyms; when они combine with а, и,
но, также forms compound conjunctions (а также, и также, но также). то же (demonstrative pronoun то
+ particle же) is written as two words and is a distinct, unrelated form from the single-word adverb
тоже — the source explicitly calls out this spelling confusion as a common error, paired with то
самое as its own emphatic-demonstrative construction.

**Negative adverbs, не- vs. ни- prefix distinction.** Standard rule, already established elsewhere:
не- (некогда, негде) marks the *absence of possibility*, ни- (никогда, нигде) marks *emphatic total
negation*, usually co-occurring with a separate не on the verb. Kept only as brief context for the
idiom set below, not re-derived in full.

## Idioms (fixed collocations built on negative/measure adverbs)

есть поедом (nag/eat someone alive, of a person), валом валить (pour in en masse/in droves), ходить
ходуном (shake/rock violently) — the source explicitly flags all three as belonging to colloquial
style. яблоку негде упасть (packed to the rafters, lit. "nowhere for an apple to fall"), никуда не
годится (won't do at all, is useless).
