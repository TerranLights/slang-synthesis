## Grammar points

### Ability/possibility register cluster (в состоянии vs. способен vs. можно/нельзя)

The lesson's central grammar point is a three-way possibility/ability system, presented in a
framed register box (p. 27) and reinforced by an explicit "Обратите внимание" side-note (p. 28):
- **`кто в состоянии + inf.`** — used for *material/circumstantial* capability (having the means,
  money, time, external conditions to do something) — e.g. `Он не в состоянии содержать семью`
  (does he have the material means to support a family).
- **`кто способен + inf.`** — used for *inherent* capability (a personal/intellectual/physical
  ability or aptitude) — e.g. `он способен на все ради детей` (he is capable, by character, of
  anything for his children).
- **`можно/нельзя + inf.`** — impersonal possibility/permission, with its own aspectual split
  (p. 32 side note): **нельзя + infinitive СВ** = physically/practically impossible (`нельзя
  позвонить` — there's no phone available); **нельзя + infinitive НСВ** = socially forbidden/not
  permitted by norms (`нельзя звонить` — not allowed here, e.g. staff-only phone).

### Generalized-second-person impossibility construction

`(чего) не + 2nd-person-singular-СВ / не + infinitive` — used to express impossibility in a
generalized/gnomic sense addressed to no one in particular (p. 36), explicitly glossed as the
"generalized-personal 2nd person singular" (parallel to the register note on p. 36's earlier
"Обратите внимание на грамматическую форму 2 л. ед.ч. в обобщенно-личном значении"). This is the
grammatical form underlying most of the proverbs captured above (`не войдешь`, `не укусишь`, `не
прикажешь`, `не заткнешь`, `не скинешь`) — a systematic proverb-formation pattern worth flagging
for `analysis/` (generalized 2sg. perfective non-past as the default register for Russian folk
proverbs about universal human limitation).

### Causal/consequence construction cluster

`(что) дает возможность/позволяет + inf.`; `(что) делает возможным + что`; `(что) ведет/приводит
к чему` — a register-neutral construction set for stating that one thing enables or causes
another, drilled via a social-policy debate context (contraception's societal effects), p. 27,
38.
