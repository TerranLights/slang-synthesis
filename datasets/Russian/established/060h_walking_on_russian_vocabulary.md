## Grammar points

### Idiom answer key gives natural full-sentence context (pp.195-224 generally)

Where the main Идиомы sub-section (captured in 060f) gives only bare idiom headwords with no
worked example, this appendix's answer key resolves each into a natural full sentence (e.g. "Он
дал слово приехать к нам в гости" for дать слово, "Она себя чувствует в новой компании как рыба в
воде" for как рыба в воде). This confirms the intended pedagogical design: the bare-headword lists
in the main chapter are meant to be actively worked by the student *before* checking this appendix,
not a deficiency in the main-chapter material itself.

### Appendix organization mirrors chapter order but is not separately titled per sub-section

The key runs continuously through exercise numbers that reset per original sub-section (e.g.
"Упражнение 1" recurs at the start of each new sub-section's key block), with no printed sub-section
headers of its own — sub-section boundaries had to be inferred from vocabulary content matching a
specific 060d-060g file's sub-section (confirmed: Синонимы key starts p.195/PDF 197; a
higher-exercise-number block spanning paronyms/collocation/prefixed-verbs runs through the
190s-200s; Просторечная лексика's own key closes the book at p.224/PDF 226 with items matching
060g's Ex.13-16 headwords).
