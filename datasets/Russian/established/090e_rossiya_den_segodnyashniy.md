## Grammar points

### Grade-number-to-person-noun suffixation

двоечник ("D/F-student") from двойка ("a two," the lowest grade) exemplifies a productive Russian pattern: a numeral-noun for a school grade + `-ник` suffix yields a person-noun. The parallel form отличник ("top student," from отлично "excellent") is referenced in the source's own footnote explaining the five-point grading scale (1 lowest – 5 highest, footnote p. 37) and confirms this is a systematic, not one-off, derivational slot — a plausible template for coining in-universe academic/institutional slang later.
