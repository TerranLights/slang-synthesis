## Grammar points

### Lesson 7 (§C): The preposition на + accusative; long- vs. short-form adjectives

The на-vs.-в accusative-of-destination noun list and long/short adjective morphology are
**largely redundant** with prior full treatments in this corpus. Worth flagging as a clean,
consolidated list not always gathered in one place elsewhere: the specific set of nouns that take
на rather than в for "going to" (вокзал, завод, рынок, курорт, конференция, опера, концерт, съезд,
лекция, улица, площадь, урок, почта, факультет, работа, остров, собрание, выставка, свадьба,
вечер) — genuinely useful as a closed reference list rather than a rule, since the choice is
lexically idiosyncratic. Also kept: the short-form-adjective-obligatory contexts (adjective +
qualifying phrase; subject = это/всё/что) as a clean three-rule summary.

### Lesson 8 (§C): Dimensions/distance; the verbs звать/позвать and называть(ся)/назвать(ся)

**Largely redundant** with prior treatments, except one genuinely useful discrimination: звать is
the general "to call" (by name, or to call out to someone), while назвать is specifically preferred
for "to call someone/something a specific name/label" — and называться carries an idiomatic
"as they say"/"so to speak" discourse-marker sense (Они были, что называется, недоделаны — "They
were, so to speak, unfinished") not always captured alongside the naming-verb paradigm elsewhere in
this corpus.

### Lesson 9 (§C): Subjunctive mood; conditional "if" (если) clauses; ли vs. если; являться/явиться

**Largely redundant subjunctive/если machinery** (бы + past tense for hypotheticals; чтобы after
хотеть/просить/настаивать/требовать to report a request onto a third party) with prior full
treatments. **Genuinely useful discrimination kept:** ли ("whether or not") vs. если ("if"), a
classic learner confusion point since English "if" covers both senses — Russian requires ли
whenever "whether or not" could substitute in the English translation, and ли's position shifts to
immediately follow whichever clause-element is being questioned, which can change the entailed
meaning (Я не знаю, господина Брауна ли отвезли в гостиницу vs. Я не знаю, в гостиницу ли отвезли
господина Брауна — same words, different scope of the "whether" question). Also kept:
являться/явиться/появиться (see Vocabulary above) — a formal-register copula plus a
directionality-contrastive perfective pair (явиться "to report/arrive in an official capacity,"
answering Куда?, vs. появиться "to show up," answering Где?) not tabulated together this clearly
elsewhere in the corpus.
