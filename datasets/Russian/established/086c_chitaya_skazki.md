## Grammar points

### Prefixed-verb inventories, not paraphrasable rules (pp. 132-177)

Unlike Section I, Section II supplies no explanatory grammar box for any of its 16 subsections — each
is simply a "Задание" line naming the prefixed aspectual-pair forms of one base root (or a small
cluster of related roots) that the cloze exercise drills, paired with one or two fairy tales chosen
apparently for narrative fit rather than to illustrate a stated rule. The one genuine morphological
finding worth flagging for later mechanics analysis is **-полн-** (p.137): the source's own chapter
title calls it a "связанная основа" (bound stem) — a root meaning "full" that never surfaces as a
free/bare verb, only ever appears already prefixed (выполнять, заполнять, исполнять, наполнять,
пополнять). This is a clean textbook example of a bound root for this project's morphological-typology
notes, distinct from the productive-but-free roots (смотреть, жить, думать, бить, работать, etc.)
everywhere else in this section, whose prefixation is instead purely combinatorial (any of several
directional/aspectual prefixes attaching to a root that also stands alone). See the Vocabulary table
above for the full per-root prefixed-form inventory — this is the section's real extractable content.
