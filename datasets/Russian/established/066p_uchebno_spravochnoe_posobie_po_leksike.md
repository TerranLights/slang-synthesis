## Grammar points

### Four near-synonym verb groups sharing a preposition-directionality contrast

Two pairs in this shard follow the same "where-to vs. where-from" directional-preposition contrast
pattern: ОПУСКАТЬ/ОПУСТИТЬ (specifies destination, на что) vs. СПУСКАТЬ/СПУСТИТЬ (specifies origin,
откуда), mirrored exactly by their reflexive counterparts ОПУСКАТЬСЯ/ОПУСТИТЬСЯ vs.
СПУСКАТЬСЯ/СПУСТИТЬСЯ. This directional-preposition-as-discriminator pattern is a clean, reusable
template for Russian's prefixed-motion-verb system (worth cross-referencing against `028_russkie_
glagoly_dvizheniya_s_pristavkami.md` and `057_russian_verbs_of_motion.md`, already extracted per the
checklist, for a fuller motion-verb-prefix picture in a future non-redundant-supplement pass).

### Verbs sharing one imperfective but splitting into two distinct perfectives

ГОТОВИТЬ/ПРИГОТОВИТЬ vs. ГОТОВИТЬ/ПОДГОТОВИТЬ (and their reflexive counterparts
ГОТОВИТЬСЯ/ПРИГОТОВИТЬСЯ vs. ГОТОВИТЬСЯ/ПОДГОТОВИТЬСЯ) are a structurally distinct case from every
other verb pair extracted so far: rather than two independent aspectual pairs, these are one shared
imperfective (готовить/готовиться) whose telic outcome is disambiguated entirely by which perfective
prefix (при- vs. под-) it pairs with — "ready for immediate use" vs. "ready for a further task." This
is a genuinely useful data point on how Russian's perfectivizing prefixes can encode a semantic
contrast that the shared imperfective verb itself doesn't disambiguate, directly relevant to later
slang-mechanics work on prefix productivity (also flagged by the checklist's fusional/verbal-aspect
typology note).

### РАССМАТРИВАТЬ's polysemy split across two separate near-synonym entries

РАССМАТРИВАТЬ is explicitly cross-referenced by the book to a second, unrelated near-synonym
entry later in the book (p.271, "рассматривать — обсуждать — разбирать") rather than having that
second sense folded into this entry — a rare case in this book of a single headword's polysemy being
split across two entirely separate discrimination groups rather than numbered within one entry. This
should be checked and reconciled once a shard covering p.271 is reached, to avoid treating the two
senses as belonging to unrelated headwords.
