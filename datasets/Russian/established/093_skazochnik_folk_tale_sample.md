## Structural note: what this series actually is

**Сказочник is *not* a native-Russian folk-tale collection** — it is the Russian-language edition of
an international children's partwork magazine (the same "Storyteller"-type format Marshall Cavendish/
GE Fabbri ran in multiple countries/languages), translated/adapted into Russian. Each 26-28-page
issue carries **continuous pagination across the whole 20-issue run** (issue 1 = pp. 1-8ish for its
lead story; issue 19 runs roughly pp. 379-402; issue 39 runs roughly pp. 799-820+), confirming this
was designed to be collected into bound volumes, not read as 20 independent issues. Each issue
contains:

- A cover story (usually a classic Western European fairy tale) plus 2-4 shorter secondary stories.
- Four "coloring pages" (СТРАНИЧКИ-РАСКРАСКИ) per issue — pure activity content, not text.
- A recurring couplet/jingle on the inside cover ("Наш добрый Сказочник-затейник / Всегда на выдумки
  горазд, / Когда перелистнём страничку, / Сигнал-звоночек он нам даст!") — a mascot rhyme, present
  identically in both issues 01 and 19 checked, evidently a fixed running feature of the whole series
  rather than story-specific content.
- A composer credit (Michael Jeffrey) and named illustrators/voice actors per issue, confirming this
  was produced as an audio-narrated partwork (matching the parent folder's "+ Audio" name even though
  no audio survives in this archive copy).

**Genre mix found across the 3 sampled issues** (confirming this is a deliberately international,
not Russian-specific, "world fairy tales" anthology — the series' own subtitle, "Коллекция всемирно
