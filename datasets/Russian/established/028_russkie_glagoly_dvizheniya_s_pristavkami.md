# Russian — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Prefixed Verbs of Motion (full book)

**Source:** Гордиенко Н. Ф. (Gordienko, N. F.), *Русские глаголы движения с приставками* ["Russian
Verbs of Motion with Prefixes"] (Учебное пособие / teaching manual), Уральский государственный
университет им. А.М. Горького (Ural State University), Екатеринбург, 2008. Full book, pages 1–162
(cover through Приложение 1's closing prefix-meaning summary table).

**Coverage note:** This is a Russian-medium teaching manual (not previously extracted — distinct
from the English-medium `Vol 1 of 3/52.Russian Verbs of Motion.pdf`, a separate, later dispatch).
The whole book covers all 16 verbal prefixes it treats (в-, вы-, вз-, до-, за-, из-, на-, о-/об-,
от-, под-, пере-, при-, у-, про-, раз-, с-) across its "Часть 2" chapters (Тема 1–16), plus the
short unprefixed "Часть 1" (base motion-verb pairs) and the book's own closing summary table
(Приложение 1, "Основные значения глагольных приставок") — extracted below as the master
Grammar-Points reference since it independently corroborates/consolidates every meaning the book
develops chapter-by-chapter. Every distinct verb-pair (SV–NSV aspectual pair) presented in each
theme's meaning tables is captured in the Vocabulary table below; the many repeated drill-exercise
sentences, fill-in-the-blank exercises, dictation excerpts (from Paustovsky, Pushkin, Lermontov,
Bulgakov, Tolstoy, Chekhov, Bunin, Yesenin, Brodsky, folk tales, and anecdotes/jokes used as
illustrative reading passages) and the book's two large drill sections (Повторительно-контрольные
упражнения and Тесты «Проверь себя», pp. 135–154) are **not** separately tabulated — they recycle
vocabulary already captured from the theme sections and introduce no new lexical items, per the
coverage rule's "skip repeated drills" instruction. The closing 55-row summary table (pp. 155–161)
is paraphrased into the Grammar points section rather than reproduced verbatim.

**PDF extraction note:** This source has a clean, genuine text layer (`pdftotext -layout` output
verified against the full 162 pages) with no font-substitution cipher and no garbled/CID-mangled
Cyrillic — unlike some other sources in this corpus, no decode was needed. Vision Reading
Confidence is `n/a` throughout for that reason. No handwritten marginalia was found in this copy.

**Morphological typology note:** Russian is fusional (see `../00_Extraction_Checklist.md`), but
this book's entire subject matter is a genuinely compositional layer on top of that: every entry is
literally `prefix + motion/base-verb root + (imperfectivizing suffix)`, and the same prefix
recurs with a small, closed set of meanings across dozens of otherwise-unrelated verb roots. A
representative morpheme breakdown is given under one illustrative entry per prefix (16 total)
rather than under all ~230 entries, per the morphological-typology guide's "use it when an atomic
gloss would hide real compositional structure, don't force it on every entry" instruction — here
the *pattern itself* is what matters, and it is uniform enough that showing it once per prefix
communicates it without needing to repeat it 230 times.

## Grammar points

### The 16 prefixes and their meanings — consolidated from the book's own closing summary table

The book's own Приложение 1 (pp. 155–161) closes with a 55-row table titled "Основные значения
глагольных приставок" ("Main meanings of verbal prefixes") that itself consolidates every
grammar point developed chapter-by-chapter. Rather than reproduce that table's 55 rows verbatim,
the meanings are paraphrased below, organized by prefix, cross-referenced to the Vocabulary
section above where the concrete verb pairs live. (The book counts 55 distinct *senses* across 16
prefixes because several prefixes are genuinely polysemous — e.g. за- alone carries 8 distinct
senses across Тема 5's eight numbered subsections.)

1. **в- (во-)** — three senses: movement inward (войти в дом); movement upward onto something
   (влезть на дерево); and, with reflexive -ся on verbs of perception/thought, "deepening into" an
   action (вдуматься в слова).
2. **вы-** — movement from inside to outside (выйти из комнаты); and, on verbs of speech/rest with
   -ся, exhaustiveness/full satisfaction of an action (выспаться, высказаться).
3. **вз- (взо-, вс-)** — movement upward, both literal (взлететь, всплыть) and, for взойти/
   восходить specifically, the astronomical "rise" sense and the botanical "sprout" sense.
4. **до-** — action carried to completion or to a definite boundary/limit (дойти до парка, дожить
   до 90 лет); with reflexive -ся, achieving a result despite difficulty (дозвониться,
   догадаться).
5. **за-** — the most polysemous prefix in the book, with eight distinct numbered senses: (I)
   movement behind an object or into a recess; (II) movement upward/into onto an object combined
   with sense I's verbs; (III) en-route/incidental action ("dropping by" on the way somewhere);
   (IV) covering an entire surface or filling a space; (V) excessive/self-detrimental absorption
   in an action (with -ся, on verbs of perception, thought, speech); (VI) onset/inception of an
   action (запеть, закричать); (VII) enclosure/fastening (закрыть, застегнуть); (VIII) several
   fixed idioms (заводить собаку, засыпать вопросами, зайти в тупик).
6. **из- (изо-, ис-)** — three senses: covering everywhere/exhaustively (изъездить всю Россию,
   synonymous in this sense with об-); movement outward/removal (извлечь, изгнать, издать,
   избрать, исключить); and predicative completeness/exhaustion of an object through repeated
   action (износить, избить, изорвать, истратить).
7. **на-** — action directed onto a surface, often collision or layering (наехать, наклеить,
   намазать); with reflexive -ся on verbs of consumption/perception/speech, "to one's fill/full
   satisfaction" (наесться, насмотреться, наговориться) — parallel in structure to вы-'s
   exhaustiveness sense but distinct in that на- + -ся marks satisfaction rather than completion.
8. **о- (об-, обо-)** — four senses: movement around or past an object (обойти, объехать);
   covering many objects or an entire object (обойти все музеи); thorough/all-sided treatment
   (обдумать, обсудить, осмотреть, описать, обыскать); and, attached to adjective/noun roots
   rather than verb roots, "become X" (осчастливить, обогатиться, осиротеть, овдоветь, ослепнуть,
   оглохнуть) — this last sense is a genuinely different derivational process from the first
   three (denominal/deadjectival rather than deverbal).
9. **от- (ото-)** — movement away, usually a short distance (отойти от окна); and separation of a
   part from a whole (оторвать, откусить, отрезать). The book explicitly notes от- and под- as an
   antonym-forming pair on shared roots (отойти ↔ подойти).
10. **под- (подо-)** — four senses: approach (подойти, подъехать), including the idiomatic
    "suit/be fitting" extension of подходить; movement under something (подплыть под мост,
    подписать — literally "write under"); movement from below upward (поднять, подпрыгнуть); and
    covert/undetected action (подслушать, подсмотреть, подделать, подкинуть).
11. **пере-** — six senses, the most senses after за-: action across/through an obstacle
    (перейти через улицу); relocation from one place to another (переехать, переставить,
    передать); redoing an action or doing it differently (переписать, переделать, переодеться);
    dividing an object into parts (перерезать, перепилить); excess beyond a norm (переоценить,
    пересолить, переутомиться); and, with reflexive -ся, reciprocal action between two or more
    parties (переписываться, переглянуться).
12. **при-** — four senses: approach/arrival, action to a goal (прийти, приехать, привезти);
    attachment or bringing into proximity/contact (приставить, приклеить, прикоснуться, пришить);
    incomplete or brief action (присесть, приоткрыть, привстать); and, with reflexive -ся on verbs
    of perception, deepening attentively into an action (присмотреться, прислушаться) — this last
    sense is a near-minimal-pair contrast with за-'s "excessive/absorbed" -ся sense on the same
    root (смотреться): при- implies careful attentiveness, за- implies losing oneself.
13. **у-** — four senses: removal/departure (уйти, увезти, удалить); covering an entire surface
    (увешать, усеять, усыпать); persisting or succeeding despite obstacles (уберечь, устоять,
    уцелеть, уговорить); and, on adjective/noun roots, causing a state to come about (улучшить,
    укрепить, усыновить) — structurally the same denominal/deadjectival strategy noted for о-
    above, applied with a different (mostly positive/ameliorative) set of source roots.
14. **про-** — four senses: movement past an object (проехать мимо цирка); movement through or
    penetrating a space (пройти через парк, проникнуть); erroneous or inadvertently harmful action
    (проехать свою остановку, простудиться, проговориться); and bounded duration, where the verb
    takes a quantity/duration expression rather than a destination (пройти 10 километров, прожить
    2 года) — this last sense is grammatically distinct from the other three in that it doesn't
    combine with a prepositional-phrase goal at all.
15. **раз- (разо-, рас-)** — six senses: movement from one place into different directions/
    dispersal (разъехаться по домам); division into parts (разбить, разрезать); movement/
    scattering onto a whole surface or in different directions (разбросать, рассыпать);
    annulling/reversing the result of a prior action (развязать, разлюбить, раздумать) — often
    paired with an antonym in за-, с-, or another prefix (развязать ↔ завязать, разлюбить ↔
    полюбить); with rас- specifically, thoroughness/full attention (рассмотреть, расспросить);
    and disjunction/separation of entities previously joined (разъединить, развестись).
16. **с- (со-)** — five senses: a single round trip, "there and back" (сходить в театр); movement
    from above downward (съехать с горы, сойти с лестницы); with reflexive -ся, convergence from
    different directions to one point (сойтись, съехаться, собраться); removal of something from
    a surface (стереть, смыть, сорвать); and combination/joining of separate things into one
    (сложить, склеить, соединить).

### Aspect and the unidirectional/multidirectional motion-verb system (Часть 1)

Russian motion verbs split into 14 base pairs, one member unidirectional/determinate (идти,
ехать, бежать...) and one multidirectional/indeterminate (ходить, ездить, бегать...); both
members of an unprefixed pair are imperfective. Prefixation is what introduces the perfective/
imperfective opposition: prefixing the *unidirectional* member yields a perfective verb (идти →
войти), while prefixing the *multidirectional* member yields the corresponding imperfective
partner of that same prefixed pair (ходить → входить). This is the structural backbone the entire
book is built on top of, restated identically in the preface's own "условные сокращения" section
and implicitly in every subsequent theme's SV/НСВ column pairing (p. 4–5).

### The "prefix + aspectual suffix" imperfectivizing layer

Beyond the base perfective/imperfective opposition inherited from Часть 1, most prefixed
perfectives derive a *further*, separately-suffixed imperfective partner via suffixes -ыва-/-ива-
(войти→входить uses root suppletion instead, but e.g. доделать→доделывать, переписать→
переписывать, разбить→разбивать use the productive suffix strategy). The book flags this suffix
explicitly in a footnote under Тема 4 (p. 30): "Глаголы НСВ образуются с помощью суффиксов -ива-,
-ыва-" ("imperfective verbs are formed by means of the suffixes -ива-, -ыва-"). This is the
productive, still-active half of the aspectual-pair system, contrasted with the closed, historical
suppletive alternations (-ход-/-й-, etc.) inherited from Часть 1's base verb list.

### Register note: разговорное слово (colloquial) tags

The book's own abbreviation key (p. 4) defines "разг." = разговорное слово (colloquial word), and
this tag is applied sparingly but explicitly to specific lexical items rather than to whole
prefixes: угнать (машину) "to steal a car" (Тема 13), достучаться (Тема 4), нахохотаться (Тема 7),
зачитаться (Тема 5), сбегать "to run and fetch" (Тема 16). These are flagged `colloquial` in the
Usage Tier column above rather than defaulted to `core`, since this is exactly the kind of
source-marked register annotation the extraction spec requires never be skipped. All other
vocabulary in the book is presented as unmarked standard/core register.

### Idioms and figurative extensions (обобщённо, cross-cutting all 16 themes)

Every theme (except Часть 1 itself) closes with its own numbered subsection titled "Некоторые
случаи употребления глаголов с приставкой [X] в переносном значении и во фразеологизмах" (some
cases of figurative/idiomatic use). These are captured inline in the Vocabulary table above as
"(idiom)"-tagged entries under their respective prefix's section, rather than pulled into a
separate table, since each idiom is a genuine extension of that specific prefix's core meaning
(e.g. до- "reach a boundary" → доходить до читателя "to reach/be understood by the reader";
про- "erroneous action" → провалиться на экзамене "to fail an exam").
