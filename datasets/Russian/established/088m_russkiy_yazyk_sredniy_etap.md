## Grammar points

No new non-redundant grammar content — direct/indirect speech transformation rules are standard and
already covered in depth elsewhere in this corpus.

### Reading-text content (paraphrased)

- **"Москве 850 лет"** (pp. 188–191) — on Moscow's 850th-anniversary celebration and the rebuilding
  of the Cathedral of Christ the Savior (destroyed under Stalin, rebuilt in the 1990s); supplies the
  poetic-epithet and commemorative vocabulary above — a notably epithet-rich text.
- **"Великая княгиня Елизавета Фёдоровна"** (pp. 202–203) — biographical piece on Elizaveta
  Fyodorovna Романова (sister of the last Empress, widowed by an assassination, founder of a
  charitable religious order, later murdered by Bolsheviks and eventually canonized as a saint).
  Supplies an unusually rich cluster of historical-religious register vocabulary, including the
  formal canonization idiom above — genuinely one of the richest register-specific finds in the
  book.
