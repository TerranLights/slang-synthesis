## Grammar points

### Lesson 4 (§C): Perfective/imperfective aspect; Reflexive verbs

**Aspect pairs and prefixation patterns are largely redundant** with prior full aspect-system
treatments in this corpus. Two points kept as genuinely useful consolidations: (1) a clean list of
common perfectivizing prefixes (при-, вы-, про-, по-, на-) alongside the "drop -ва-" and
irregular-suppletion patterns (говорить→сказать, брать→взять) in one place; (2) the reflexive-verb
discussion's three distinct functions worth distinguishing — true reflexive (готовиться, "to get
oneself ready"), passive-forming reflexive with instrumental agent (Книги возвращаются моими
студентами — "the books are being returned by my students"), and a closed class of
"emotion/attitude" verbs that are reflexive with no separable non-reflexive counterpart of the same
meaning (нравиться "to appeal to," бояться "to fear," дождаться "to wait for" — note that the
grammatical subject of нравiться is the appealing object, and the person is in the dative: "что/кто
нравится кому").

### Lesson 5 (§C): Imperative mood; Conditional particle бы; Adverbs; также vs. тоже

**Imperative formation rules are largely redundant** with prior sources. Kept as genuinely useful:
the full function inventory of бы (general wish; hypothetical если бы...бы conditional; the
"question-word + бы + ни + past tense" free-choice/concessive construction — куда бы он ни ездил,
"wherever he travels"; and a politeness-softening use in indirect suggestions — "Вам бы сходить к
врачу," "Perhaps you'd better see a doctor") — the concessive construction and the politeness use
are not always spelled out together this clearly elsewhere in the corpus. Also kept: the
также-vs.-тоже discrimination (тоже for previously-mentioned/old information, также for
newly-introduced information) — a genuine near-synonym pair prior sources sometimes gloss as
simple synonyms.

### Lesson 6 (§C): Aspect in the future tense; Indefinite adverbs/pronouns (-то vs. -нибудь vs. -либо)

Future-tense aspect contrast is **largely redundant**. The **-то/-нибудь/-либо indefinite-marker
system is worth a fuller note** since its distributional rule (-нибудь in questions, imperatives,
future-tense, and conditional/subjunctive contexts; -то in past/present-tense positive statements
unless a repeated action is meant; -либо as an even-more-indefinite variant, "at all," typically
in negative/hypothetical contexts) is a genuinely systematic three-way indefiniteness distinction
that maps roughly onto (but is not identical to) the English some-/any-/at-all- distinction, and is
a clean, fully-worked paradigm for any future indefinite-pronoun design work in the derived conlang.
