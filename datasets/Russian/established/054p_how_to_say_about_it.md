## Grammar points

### Также as a dual-function synonym: тоже-like vs. и-like uses (§2.1, pp. 245–247)

The book's opening move: establishing that также is not a single unified conjunction but a
register-marked item that can substitute for *either* of the two conjunctions covered in `054o`,
depending on context — synonymous with тоже when marking non-new/aligned information, or synonymous
with и (equivalently, with the phrase кроме того, "in addition to that") when introducing genuinely
new information. A drilling exercise (p. 246, library-hours/reading-room descriptions) asks learners
to sort a set of также-containing sentences into these two functional categories before the
positional rules are introduced.

### Positional rules distinguishing также's two senses (§2.2, pp. 247–250)

This part supplies the systematic payoff of the тоже/и distinction from `054o`, now applied to
также's two uses:

- **Also-sense (synonymous with тоже)**: также occupies the **same position** as тоже would — directly
  before whichever part of the sentence is being marked as matching the preceding sentence: "В
  субботу он тоже открыт. / В субботу он также открыт."
- **Additionally-sense (synonymous with и/кроме того)**: here также's characteristic position is
  **before the predicate** (or before the second component of a compound predicate), rather than
  before the specific new word the way и would sit — "её также можно получить" / "они будут также
  печататься." The book explicitly notes (p. 247) that in this sense, только также — not и — is
  possible directly before a predicate in this slot: "В этой позиции возможен только союз также."

A further register-specific restriction closes the section (p. 248): **in its additionally/кроме того
sense, также cannot open a sentence** — sentence-initial position in that sense is reserved for и
("И завтра ожидается обильный снег," never *"Также завтра..."). A side-by-side comparison table (p.
248, weather forecasts and a Tchaikovsky-competition example) demonstrates this restriction directly,
marking the sentence-initial также slot with an asterisked "not used in this position" note — the
same discipline the book has used elsewhere (e.g. Раздел II §4's syntactic position notes) for
flagging a hard positional constraint rather than a soft stylistic preference. A closing exercise set
(pp. 249–250) drills the full three-conjunction system (тоже/и/также) across varied factual content
(academic scheduling, expedition planning), followed by open-ended description prompts (p. 250) asking
learners to independently describe pairs of similar people/places/objects using the conjunctions
studied so far.

This closes subsection **А. Указание на сходство событий...** of Раздел VI in its entirety (pp.
233–250). Subsection **Б. Присоединение к сообщению дополнительной информации о том факте, о котором
только что шла речь** begins at printed p. 251, introducing a distinct construction pair — **и
ещё**/**а ещё** — used not to mark a *second, similar* event but to *add further detail to the same
already-mentioned situation* (the book's own worked contrast, pp. 251–252: "Мне говорили" and "Я
слышал" both refer back to the same underlying situation — "я знаю" — even though they name different
grammatical subjects). This subsection also draws a register-neutral generalization worth flagging in
advance for `054q`: sentences built with а ещё/и ещё mark the newly-added information as **more
significant** than what preceded it (p. 252's "Обратите внимание!" box) — covered starting in `054q`.
