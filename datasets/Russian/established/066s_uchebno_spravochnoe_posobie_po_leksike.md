## Grammar points

### СООБЩАТЬ/ОБЪЯВЛЯТЬ/ЗАЯВЛЯТЬ/ИЗВЕЩАТЬ/ИНФОРМИРОВАТЬ: register cline completed

Completes the five-way "inform/announce" cluster opened at the end of 066r. The full register cline
is now: СООБЩАТЬ (neutral) → ОБЪЯВЛЯТЬ (official, facts/events generally) → ЗАЯВЛЯТЬ (official, the
speaker's own position/stance specifically) → ИЗВЕЩАТЬ (official, the notifier's own forthcoming
action specifically) → ИНФОРМИРОВАТЬ (bookish, emphasizes officialness of content). ЗАЯВИТЬ carries
a fixed-idiom extension (`заявить в милицию`, "to report to police") worth flagging for later
register/idiom analysis work, as an example of a near-synonym verb developing its own frozen
collocation distinct from its general definition.

### РАССМАТРИВАТЬ/ОБСУЖДАТЬ/РАЗБИРАТЬ: an "examine officially" cluster differentiated by outcome-orientation

A three-way cluster sharing "to engage with something in detail" but differentiated by what happens
next: РАССМАТРИВАТЬ/РАССМОТРЕТЬ is oriented toward reaching a decision or evaluation (explicitly
`(офиц.)`-marked, and notably allows either an individual or a collective/institutional subject);
ОБСУЖДАТЬ/ОБСУДИТЬ is oriented toward the *process* of multiple parties voicing differing opinions,
with no single decision-maker implied; РАЗБИРАТЬ/РАЗОБРАТЬ is oriented toward one party's own
detailed analytical opinion. Useful as a second data point (alongside the ВОЗГЛАВЛЯТЬ-cluster from
066r) for how this source consistently marks institutional/official register with `(офиц.)` rather
than leaving it to be inferred from collocations alone.

### ЗАТРАГИВАТЬ/КАСАТЬСЯ/УПОМИНАТЬ: a "mention briefly" cluster, two members marked as polysemous homonym-adjacent

Both ЗАТРАГИВАТЬ/ЗАТРОНУТЬ and КАСАТЬСЯ/КОСНУТЬСЯ are flagged with `*` in the source (their entries
in this shared sense are asterisked, and КАСАТЬСЯ additionally marked `(перен.)`), signaling each verb
has a more literal/primary sense outside this near-synonym group's scope (likely "to affect/concern"
for ЗАТРАГИВАТЬ and "to touch physically" for the literal sense underlying КАСАТЬСЯ/КОСНУТЬСЯ). Only
the shared "mention only briefly" sense is discriminated here; УПОМИНАТЬ/УПОМЯНУТЬ is presented
without such a flag, suggesting it may be closer to monosemous within this source's scope, or at
least that its "mention/name without further comment" sense is treated as primary.

### УЧИТЬСЯ/ЗАНИМАТЬСЯ and ИЗУЧАТЬ/УЧИТЬ/ОБУЧАТЬ: two adjacent but formally distinct "learning verb" clusters

Two related but formally separate clusters cover the semantic space of learning/teaching. The first
(УЧИТЬСЯ, УЧИТЬСЯ/ВЫУЧИТЬСЯ, УЧИТЬСЯ/НАУЧИТЬСЯ, ЗАНИМАТЬСЯ/ЗАНЯТЬСЯ) is presented with no
comparative comment, only individual definitions — ЗАНИМАТЬСЯ/ЗАНЯТЬСЯ notably carries five distinct
numbered senses with different aspect restrictions per sense (senses 1, 2, 4 `только нсв`; sense 3
`только св`), making it one of the more grammatically complex single entries encountered in this
book so far. The second cluster (ИЗУЧАТЬ/ИЗУЧИТЬ-УЧИТЬ/ВЫУЧИТЬ-УЧИТЬ/НАУЧИТЬ-ОБУЧАТЬ/ОБУЧИТЬ) *does*
carry a comparative comment along a "depth of understanding vs. rote memorization vs. transmission to
others vs. transmission to many by a system" axis, with the source's own illustrative minimal-pair
example (school pupils "учат" physics vs. university students "изучают" physics) — a clean,
reusable template for the depth-of-understanding register distinction, comparable to the earlier
obligation-gradient and register-cline templates already flagged in 066r.

### ОВЛАДЕВАТЬ/УСВАИВАТЬ/ОСВАИВАТЬ: a "mastery" cluster split along parts-vs-whole and skill-vs-comprehension axes

A three-way cluster (shared meaning: "someone makes an effort to understand how something works and
use it practically") split two ways: ОВЛАДЕВАТЬ/ОВЛАДЕТЬ for practical-skill acquisition generally;
ОСВАИВАТЬ/ОСВОИТЬ specifically for understanding a *holistic* phenomenon's operating principles;
УСВАИВАТЬ/УСВОИТЬ specifically for understanding and remembering *parts/elements* of a whole under
study. Both ОВЛАДЕВАТЬ and УСВАИВАТЬ/ОСВАИВАТЬ carry `*` flags suggesting further unrelated senses
(likely "to seize/take possession of" for ОВЛАДЕВАТЬ) outside this comparison's scope.

### Глаголы с суффиксами -е-//-и-: a highly productive inchoative/causative derivational pattern (major finding)

New chapter (p.281 onward) presenting a genuinely systematic morphological pattern rather than a
lexical near-synonym cluster: deadjectival verb pairs where the **-е- suffix** derives an
**intransitive inchoative** verb ("the subject comes to possess the quality named by the base
adjective") and the **-и- suffix** derives a **transitive causative** verb ("the subject causes an
object to possess/appear to possess that quality"). Two sub-families surfaced in this page range:

1. **Color adjectives** (белый→белеть/белить, чёрный→чернеть/чернить, синий→синеть/синить): the
   -е- verb carries a *further*, imperfective-only extended sense beyond simple inchoative — "to
   be visibly perceptible as that color at a distance" (the source's own example: "Вдали белело
   какое-то здание," "some building showed white in the distance") — a genuine third meaning layer
   (become X / be visible as X) worth flagging for later mechanics-analysis work on how Russian
   color terms extend into perception-verbs. ЧЕРНИТЬ additionally develops its own figurative
   extension (чернить/очернить, "to defame," with a distinct perfective очернить rather than
   почернить) — a clean example of a causative verb acquiring an idiomatic sense not shared by its
   inchoative partner.
2. **Physical/psychological-state adjectives** (весёлый→веселеть/веселить, ледяной-adjacent
   →леденеть/леденить, холодный→холодеть/холодить, пьяный→пьянеть/пьянить, бледный→бледнеть/
   бледнить, молодой→молодеть/молодить, старый→стареть/старить, полный→полнеть/полнить,
   толстый→толстеть/толстить, худой→худеть/худить): the same become-X/make-X split, but here the
   -и- causative verb's dominant sense across nearly every pair in this sub-family is specifically
   **"to create a false/deceptive impression of X"** (толстить = make someone *look* fatter than
   they are, молодить = make someone *look* younger than they are, etc.) rather than "to actually
   change the object's state" — a subtly different causative semantics from the literal
   "make-it-actually-white" sense of БЕЛИТЬ. This deceptive/appearance-only causative sense is a
   strong, systematic candidate for later slang-mechanics work on how Russian lexicalizes
   "deceptive appearance" as a grammatical (not just lexical) category. ЛЕДЕНЕТЬ/ЛЕДЕНИТЬ and
   ХОЛОДЕТЬ/ОХЛАДЕТЬ/ОХЛАДИТЬ/ОХЛАДЕТЬ additionally show a further figurative layer (feeling
   intense cold/fear as a symptom of shock; losing emotional attachment) layered onto the
   literal-temperature base sense, and this sub-family is the first to show genuinely divergent
   perfective partners per sense (e.g. ХОЛОДЕТЬ takes ПОХОЛОДЕТЬ for its literal sense but ОХЛАДЕТЬ
   is a wholly separate perfective-only verb for the "lose emotional interest" sense) — worth
   cross-referencing against the earlier ПРОСИТЬ-cluster's cleaner one-imperfective/one-perfective
   pattern as a contrast case for how aspect-partnering can fragment under semantic extension.

This pattern continues into the next chapter, "Глаголы с суффиксами -ну-//-и-" (opened p.288,
глохнуть/глушить-слепнуть/слепить-крепнуть/крепить-сохнуть/сушить), which is a clear structural
parallel (a different suffix pair, same become-X/make-X split) and is left for the next shard.
