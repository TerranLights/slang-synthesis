## Grammar points

### Clipped-compound abbreviation formation (госэкзамен-type)

Exercise 3 (p. 79) explicitly teaches this pattern using the text's own example: **госэкзамены** ← государственные экзамены ("state exams"). Russian productively forms a clipped compound noun by taking the first syllable(s) of one word in a phrase and fusing it directly onto the (often unclipped) head noun of the phrase — structurally similar to English "sitcom" or "info-" compounds but far more systematic in Russian technical/bureaucratic register. The exercise's own drill list of parallel phrase → clipped-compound targets (worth recording as a template inventory for the mechanics-analysis phase, even though the exercise itself doesn't supply the resulting forms): Ленинградская область (→ Ленобласть-type), туристическое агентство (→ турагентство-type), специальная школа (→ спецшкола-type), сберегательный банк (→ сбербанк-type — Сбербанк is in fact Russia's largest bank, a real-world lexicalized instance of exactly this pattern), строительные материалы (→ стройматериалы-type), техническая помощь (→ техпомощь-type), профессиональный союз (→ профсоюз-type, "trade union," a fully lexicalized Soviet-era form), социалистический реализм (→ соцреализм-type, a well-known Soviet art-doctrine term), заграничный паспорт (→ загранпаспорт-type), заработная плата (→ зарплата-type, near-universally used over the full form), информационное бюро (→ инфобюро-type), террористический акт (→ теракт-type, standard contemporary Russian media shorthand). **This is one of the most productive and systematic word-formation slots found in the book so far** — a strong candidate template for the conlang-synthesis phase (adjective/participle-clipped-onto-noun compounding for institutional/bureaucratic register terms).

### Numeral-incorporating compound adjectives

Exercise 4 (p. 79) drills the same "двухуровневый"-type pattern already seen in the main text (два уровня → двухуровневый, "two-level(led)"): a cardinal numeral + linking vowel (usually -x-/-и-) + the noun stem + adjectival suffix. Drilled forms span один день → однодневный, два часа → двухчасовой, три месяца → трёхмесячный, etc. — confirms numeral-incorporation into adjectives is fully regular and productive for any noun+numeral combination in Russian, not limited to institutional vocabulary (unlike the госэкзамен-type pattern above, which is register-specific). A useful, cleanly rule-governed template for a conlang's own numeral-adjective derivation.
