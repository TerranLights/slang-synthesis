## Chapter 4 — «Муж на час» ("Husband by the Hour")

Printed pp. 28–31 (PDF pp. 31–33). Article about a Perm-based business renting out handymen ("husbands by the hour") to women. Subsections: «Предложение рождает спрос», «Муж-универсал», «Кроме "супружеского" долга». Source note: adapted from I. Zhukov, *АиФ-Петербург* ("Argumenty i Fakty — Petersburg"), No. 17, 2004. Dense, idiom-rich, register-relevant chapter — includes one borrowed-English slang term (**фейс-контроль**) and one homograph worth flagging (**брак** "marriage" vs. **брак** "defective goods/reject").

## Grammar points

### Scare-quoted neologism/repurposed-word marking

This chapter's source text repeatedly uses guillemet-quotes («мужа на час», «весомого», «фейс-контроль», «специалистов») to flag words being used in a non-standard, ad-hoc, or ironic sense — a punctuation convention worth tracking across the whole book as a signal of where the authors themselves recognize novel/slangy usage, distinct from ordinary direct-speech quotation. This is a useful signal for the mechanics-analysis phase: guillemet-marking in this corpus reliably correlates with either wordplay, a coined commercial term, or a borrowed/repurposed term (фейс-контроль).
