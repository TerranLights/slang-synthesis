## Grammar points

### Деепричастие (gerund/adverbial participle) and Причастие (participle) — pp. 27–30

Standard formation and usage rules (formation of imperfective/perfective деепричастие,
действительные/страдательные причастия full and the причастный оборот). This overlaps
extensively-covered territory (`002/003`, `006/007`, `012/013`) — no genuinely new paradigm or
exception found in this lesson's presentation; skipped per non-redundant-supplement scoping.

### Reading-text content (paraphrased, not lexically mined further)

- **"Чудесный доктор"** (по А.И. Куприну, pp. 25–27) — the well-known Kuprin short story: a
  destitute family is anonymously helped by a chance-encountered stranger who turns out to be the
  real historical surgeon Н.И. Пирогов. Used here purely as a reading text; incidental vocabulary is
  common-core and not separately tabulated.
- **"Н.И. Пирогов"** (pp. 37–38) — short biographical sketch of the historical surgeon; supplies the
  vocabulary box above.
- **Пословицы и афоризмы о здоровье** (proverbs/aphorisms about health, p. 39) — mostly
  cross-culturally attested proverbs (Sokrates, Emerson, Franklin quotes translated into Russian,
  plus "В здоровом теле здоровый дух" / "Здоровье дороже богатства" / "Умеренность — мать
  здоровья"); low unique-to-Russian value, not tabulated individually.
