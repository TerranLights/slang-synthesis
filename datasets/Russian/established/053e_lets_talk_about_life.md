## Grammar points

### Explicit pre-/post-glasnost and pre-/post-perestroika periodization
This chapter's vocabulary presentation repeatedly anchors terms to named historical thresholds
supplied by the source itself, not inferred: open discussion of секс dated to "период гласности";
absence of половое воспитание dated to "до перестройки"; and гомосексуализм's decriminalization
dated to the specific year 1993. This chapter has unusually precise, source-supported `Attested
Era` data compared to the rest of the book — worth flagging for any later timeline-building work in
the mechanics-analysis phase, since most chapters only support the coarse book-wide "contemporary
(1996)" default.

### Clipped-compound word formation recurs: роддом
роддом (родильный дом → роддом) is a fourth instance of the same Soviet-era clipped-compound
pattern already documented in Урок 1 (медпункт, детдом, главврач) and Урок 3 (спецрейс, профсоюз)
— now confirmed across four separate chapters, strengthening the case that this is a systematic,
book-wide productive word-formation mechanism worth treating as a named pattern in later mechanics
analysis rather than a one-off curiosity.

### Deverbal -ение/-ание abstract nouns as a taught, findable pattern
The Word Study box treats -ение/-ание not as an isolated vocabulary item but as a rule the student
is expected to apply productively (find five more instances in the assigned reading) — direct
textbook evidence that Russian deverbal noun formation via these suffixes is treated as
rule-governed and generative, not just a closed list of memorized forms.
