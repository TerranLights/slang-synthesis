## Grammar points

No new non-redundant grammar content — cause/effect expression constructions (потому что, так как,
поэтому, из-за того что, благодаря тому что, etc.) are standard and already covered in depth
elsewhere in this corpus.

### Reading-text content (paraphrased)

- **"Победитель"** (pp. 83–85) — on Michelangelo's sculpting of *Moses* and the artist's own
  perfectionism/persistence; the pre-text vocab box's победа/победитель framing plays on both the
  literal "victor" sense and figurative "triumph over one's own limitations."
- **"Чемпион"** (pp. 93–94) — a short story about an aging runner racing what may be his final
  competition against a younger rival, who collapses mid-race; the champion selflessly turns back to
  help him rather than finish. Supplies the sports/idiom vocabulary cluster above — a notably
  idiom-dense text for this book.
