## Grammar points

### Gendered-labor-division vocabulary as a coherent lexical field
The stereotype-elicitation word banks on p.166 (сплетничать/воевать/драться/манипулировать vs.
выносить мусор/стричь траву/содержать семью/заниматься детьми) present two parallel semantic
fields the textbook treats as gender-coded activity vocabulary for a discussion exercise. Useful as
a ready-made lexical-field pairing for later mechanics work on how gendered social-role vocabulary
clusters in Russian pedagogical materials.

### A self-directed word-formation exercise as source data
Unlike other chapters' pre-digested root-family boxes, this Word Study section (p.180) hands the
student a bare list of complex words and asks them to identify roots/prefixes/suffixes themselves.
The words chosen recur several already-documented compounding patterns from earlier chapters
(само- self-compounds, все- all-compounds, the пуст-/пуск-/пущ- release-root family), reinforcing
that these are indeed systematic, book-wide-recurring Russian word-formation strategies rather than
one-off curiosities — now attested in at least three separate chapters (Урок 3, 5/implicitly, 7).

### Loanword layer distinct from native-root compounding
The "Loan Words" list (эмиграция, эмансипированный, эгоистичный, цивилизация, парадокс) is presented
by the source as a separate category from the native-root word-formation exercise directly above
it — an explicit native-vs-borrowed vocabulary-layer distinction drawn by the textbook itself, worth
preserving for later work on register/formality associations of Latinate loans in Russian
intellectual/journalistic prose (all five items came from the same higher-register magazine-style
reading).
