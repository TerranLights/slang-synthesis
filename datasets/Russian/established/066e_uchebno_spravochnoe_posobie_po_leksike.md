## Grammar points

### Suffix semantics comment for `-н-//-ян-,-ан-,-ен-//-ист-` (p.112, paraphrased)

`-н-` adjectives on this pattern mean "relates to X" (водный "of water," земной "of the Earth,"
кожный "of skin," костный "of bone," стекольный "of glass"), with КАМЕННЫЙ flagged by the book
itself as the pattern's one exception (meaning "made of stone" rather than merely "relating to
stone"). `-ян-/-ан-/-ен-` adjectives mean "made of/consists of X" (водяной, земляной, кожаный,
костяной, травяной, стеклянный). `-ист-` adjectives signal an abundance of the base noun's referent
(водянистый, землистый, каменистый, костистый, травянистый); for водянистый/землистый/
травянистый specifically, `-ист-` carries an *additional* meaning layer — a pale/faded color quality
("cheeks a sallow, землистый color") not present in каменистый/костистый's straightforward
"abundant in" sense. This is a genuinely three-way productive suffix contrast (relational / material
/ abundance-or-quality), the richest suffix system documented so far in this section.

### The book's own addendum/note apparatus ("!!! Дополнение" / "!!! Обратите внимание")

This shard is the first to show the book's explicit marginal-note system in volume: a "!!!
Дополнение" (Addendum) heading introduces a sense the main entry initially omitted (КОСТНЫЙ's
"obtained from bone" sense, ВОДЯНОЙ's "water-powered" sense, ЗЕМЛЯНОЙ's "worked farmland" sense,
ЗЕМЕЛЬНЫЙ as a whole extra headword), while a "!!! Обратите внимание" (Note) heading flags a
synonymy relationship between two already-defined headwords (КОСТИСТЫЙ = КОСТЛЯВЫЙ in one sense;
ТРАВЯНИСТЫЙ = ТРАВЯНОЙ in one sense) rather than introducing new content. Both apparatus types are
themselves a form of the explicit-annotation the coverage rule asks to capture, since they represent
the book's own worked lexicographic judgment about sense boundaries and synonym overlap.

### New suffix set: `-ян-//-ов-, -ник-ов-//-овит-` and `-н-//-ов-//-овит-`

ЛЕДЯНОЙ-ЛЕДОВЫЙ-ЛЕДНИКОВЫЙ-ЛЕДОВИТЫЙ (p.121-123) is a four-way material/domain contrast: `-ян-`
for the concrete-object and figurative senses, `-ов-` for large-scale/geographic ice fields and
ice-based activities, `-ник-ов-` for glacier-caused geological formations, and `-овит-` restricted
to a single fixed geographic proper name (Северный Ледовитый океан) — an unusually narrow,
name-locked productivity pattern worth flagging for later mechanics work on frozen/lexicalized
derivational slots. ДЕЛЬНЫЙ-ДЕЛОВОЙ-ДЕЛОВИТЫЙ (opening on p.124, continues into the next shard)
begins a new `-н-//-ов-//-овит-` set built on a semantically unrelated root (дело, "business/task"),
distinguishing personal competence (ДЕЛЬНЫЙ) from institutional/functional business-relatedness
(ДЕЛОВОЙ, so far) — the ДЕЛОВИТЫЙ member's definition is not yet reached in this page range.
