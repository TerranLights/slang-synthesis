## Grammar points

### Continued antonym/near-synonym-pair organization with explicit `Комментарий` blocks

Every pair in this shard (ГЛУБОКИЙ-ГЛУБИННЫЙ, ВИНОВНЫЙ-ВИНОВАТЫЙ, ВРАЖЕСКИЙ-ВРАЖДЕБНЫЙ,
ЖЕЛАННЫЙ-ЖЕЛАТЕЛЬНЫЙ, ОБЫЧНЫЙ-ОБЫКНОВЕННЫЙ) opens with an explicit `Комментарий` block giving the
book's own worked discrimination logic and a minimal-pair example sentence — this is the noun
section's original format (see `066`), now firmly established as this book's steady-state format for
the remainder of the adjective section following the suffix-family stretch (pp.39-132) and the
transitional antonym-pair stretch without commentary (`066f`/`066g`).

### A recurring discrimination axis: emotional/intensifying vs. structural/legal register

Two of this shard's five pairs split along a similar axis to earlier ones: ВИНОВНЫЙ (serious,
legal-register "guilty of a crime") vs. ВИНОВАТЫЙ (everyday "at fault for a mishap") mirrors the
ДОЛГ/ОБЯЗАННОСТЬ/ОБЯЗАТЕЛЬСТВО-style formal/informal split seen in the noun section; ВРАЖЕСКИЙ
(narrow, military-register "enemy's") vs. ВРАЖДЕБНЫЙ (broad, register-neutral "hostile") is a
domain-restriction contrast rather than a formality contrast. ЖЕЛАННЫЙ (emotional longing) vs.
ЖЕЛАТЕЛЬНЫЙ (practical advisability, frequently used in the short/predicative form желательно as a
near-modal marker) is a genuinely useful data point for later slang-mechanics work on how Russian
distinguishes affect-laden vs. utility-laden near-synonyms morphologically (-анн- vs. -ательн-).
