## Grammar points

### Colloquial wish/desire constructions (Урок 4's core register cluster)

Framed box, p. 53, one of the richest colloquial-register clusters in the book:
- **`кто хотел бы + inf.`** — the neutral polite-conditional wish.
- **Bare infinitive + бы** (e.g. `отдохнуть бы`, `выспаться бы`) — a highly colloquial elliptical
  wish construction, dropping the subject/hотел entirely; the whole opening dialogue of this
  lesson (p. 54) is built entirely from chained instances of this pattern.
  - **`кто бы съездил куда`** and **`куда бы (+ бы)`** — same elliptical-wish family with a
    directional object fronted.
  - **noun in accusative/genitive + бы** (e.g. `чаю бы`, `В Китай бы`) — the wish attaches
    directly to a bare noun object, p. 53, 55.
  - **`хотя бы + что/чего/куда`** — scales the wish down to a minimal/fallback desire ("if only
    just X"), p. 53, 54.
- **`если и + infinitive, то X`** ("if [we do it] at all, then X") — a narrowing-conditional
  construction for negotiating a compromise, explicitly drilled as a two-part exchange where the
  second speaker's reply structurally echoes the first's frame (`куда, так куда`), p. 53, 56.
- **Anxiety/reassurance pair**: worry constructions (`я боюсь, что...`; `не окажется ли так,
  что...?`; `а не слишком ли там как...?`; `а что если/а вдруг + potential mishap`) are
  systematically paired with the formal reassurance register (`будьте уверены`; `Ваши опасения
  напрасны`) — modeling a full customer-anxiety/agent-reassurance script (travel-agent dialogue,
  pp. 58-59).

### Register note: idiom-dense "how to travel" advice column (p. 61-62)

A magazine excerpt (glossed журнал «Домовой») is unusually idiom-dense for expository prose —
worth flagging for `analysis/` as a register model of colloquial-inflected journalistic advice
writing (idioms embedded directly in otherwise-standard prose, not confined to dialogue).
