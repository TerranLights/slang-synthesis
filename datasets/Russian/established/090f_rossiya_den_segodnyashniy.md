## Grammar points

### Metaphor transfer: pastoral verb → organized-crime-adjacent slang

**пасти** ("to shepherd/tend livestock") applied to adults controlling child beggars is a clean, single-word example of a pastoral/agricultural verb being repurposed for organized street-economy control — structurally the same kind of domain-transfer already seen with **злостный уклонист** (Chapter 3, military → relationships) and **фейс-контроль** (Chapter 4, nightlife → hiring). Collectively these three chapters establish "borrow a term from an unrelated institutional/agricultural domain and apply it to an interpersonal or economic-underworld referent" as a recurring, productive slang-formation strategy in this corpus — worth carrying into the mechanics-analysis phase as a named pattern.
