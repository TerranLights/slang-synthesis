## Grammar points

### Suffix semantics comment for `-ичн-//-еск-/-ическ-`, drama/emotion cluster (p.99, paraphrased)

For this second application of the `-н-/-ичн-//-еск-/-ическ-` pattern (drama/emotion/art-domain
roots, as opposed to the earlier science/profession-domain roots in `066c`), `-ичн-` forms are used
purely for evaluating something (an impressionistic judgment — "this scene struck me as comic/tragic")
and lack the genre-technical meaning that `-еск-/-ическ-` forms carry (which name the formal artistic
genre/discipline itself — комедия, трагедия, лирика, поэзия, романтизм as movements/genres).
Six pairs/triples share this exact semantic split: драматичный/драматический,
комичный/комический/комедийный, лиричный/лирический, поэтичный/поэтический,
романтичный/романтический, трагичный/трагический/трагедийный — a highly regular and productive
suffix contrast across Russian's whole borrowed-Greek/Latin artistic-genre vocabulary.

### A recurring lexicographic pattern: "то же, что Х в N значении"

Across this shard, five of the six drama/emotion-cluster `-ичн-` headwords (комичный, лиричный →
partial, поэтичный, романтичный, трагичный) and one prior-cluster headword (реалистичный) are
defined by explicit cross-reference to a numbered sense of their `-еск-/-ическ-` counterpart
("то же, что комический в 1 значении") rather than by an independent definition — a clean signal
that the `-ичн-` member of each pair is semantically parasitic on (a proper subset of) its
`-еск-/-ическ-` counterpart, unlike the fully independent pairs seen in `066b`/`066c` (e.g.
ВЕЩЬ/ПРЕДМЕТ, СПОСОБ/МЕТОД) where both members have fully separate definitions.

### New suffix set: `-н-//-ист-` (p.107 onward)

Denotes an adjective characterized by an abundance of, or a strong tendency toward, the quality
named by the root, contrasted with a plain relational `-н-` adjective for the same root. Only the
`-н-` member of each of the three initial pairs (горный, лесной, болотный) is captured in this
shard's page range — the `-ист-` counterparts (гористый, лесистый, болотистый) and both headwords'
"located in X" second senses begin on the following page and continue into the next shard.
