## Grammar points

### ПОПАДАТЬ/ОКАЗЫВАТЬСЯ/ОЧУТИТЬСЯ: a three-tier expectedness gradient

A clean three-way scale by degree of unexpectedness/agency: ПОПАДАТЬ signals a *deliberate,
goal-directed* result of the subject's own movement (even against obstacles); ОКАЗЫВАТЬСЯ signals an
*unexpected* result or state, with no active-goal implication; ОЧУТИТЬСЯ (perfective-only) adds a
further layer of *strangeness/unusualness* on top of unexpectedness. This is directly comparable to
the earlier ДЕЙСТВИЕ/ДОСТИГАТЬ-style agency-gradient patterns and worth flagging for later
mechanics-analysis work on how Russian verbs encode degrees of agentive control over an outcome.

### ДОБИВАТЬСЯ vs. ДОСТИГАТЬ: subject-animacy as a grammatical discriminator, not just a stylistic one

Unusually for this book, the ДОБИВАТЬСЯ/ДОСТИГАТЬ discrimination includes a genuine *selectional*
restriction rather than a purely stylistic/register note: ДОБИВАТЬСЯ requires an animate or
collective-noun subject, while ДОСТИГАТЬ (per its sense 3) can take purely inanimate/measurable
subjects (température, size, weight) that ДОБИВАТЬСЯ could never take. This is a substantive
syntactic fact, not just a nuance of emphasis, and is a good example of the kind of concrete
grammatical constraint the extraction spec's "grammar point" clause is meant to capture from
verb-focused entries.

### ЧИНИТЬ vs. РЕМОНТИРОВАТЬ: register/domain split by object complexity and scope

This four-way "repair" cluster splits cleanly along two axes: object complexity/domain
(ЧИНИТЬ for simple household items vs. РЕМОНТИРОВАТЬ for complex mechanisms/structures) and
repair scope (ИСПРАВИТЬ implying a more thorough fix than ПОПРАВИТЬ, and — per the book's own
worked contrast — РЕМОНТИРОВАТЬ implying the whole object is addressed while ЧИНИТЬ implies only
the specific malfunctioning spot is). This is a good candidate for a "domain-plus-scope" discrimination
template alongside the register-plus-directionality template already logged for
ИНОСТРАННЫЙ/ЗАРУБЕЖНЫЙ/ЗАГРАНИЧНЫЙ/ИМПОРТНЫЙ (`066l`).
