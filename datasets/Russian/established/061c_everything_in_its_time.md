## Grammar points

### Temporal precedence: до vs. перед vs. накануне vs. под — a graded proximity scale
The book distinguishes four constructions for "one action happens before another," graded by how
close in time the two actions are understood to be: до + genitive is the loosest ("at some point
earlier than," До поездки в Россию он немного говорил по-русски — he already spoke some Russian
before the trip, with no claim about how long before); перед + instrumental specifically marks
*immediate* adjacency to the second action's onset (Перед поездкой в Россию он купил тёплую одежду —
buying the coat happened right before departure, not just "at some earlier point"); накануне +
genitive ("on the eve of") is a bookish/literary near-synonym of the immediate-adjacency sense; and
под + accusative ("toward/just before") is restricted to a small closed set of collocations — под
утро, под вечер, под Рождество, под Новый год — and is not freely productive with arbitrary time
nouns the way the other three are. The intensifier самый can be added to either до or перед
constructions to sharpen the proximity claim further (до самого утра = "until the very morning";
перед самым приездом = "right before the very arrival"), but attaches to different semantic slots
depending on which preposition it modifies — with до it can also mark an end-boundary-in-time sense
distinct from precedence (as in a duration construction: Мы не спали всю ночь до самого утра), which
is disambiguated only by context, not by any morphological difference.

### Aspect with precedence constructions
Перед + instrumental and the "earlier-than" reading of до + genitive both favor the perfective aspect
for the main verb, unless the action being described is itself repeated/habitual (Он пришёл перед
обедом; Она сдала все экзамены до июня — both single completed events). When до + genitive instead
carries its separate "end-boundary of an ongoing action" sense (covered in `061_...md`'s section 4),
the verb reverts to imperfective (Она сдавала экзамены до самого лета — she was in the process of
taking exams all the way up to summer, not a single completed handoff).
