## Grammar points

### Register-marked instrumental plural doublets (16.4, p. 190)

Most nouns form the instrumental plural regularly (-ы/-а → -ами; -и/-я → -ями). Four exceptions —
де́ти → детьми́, до́чери → дочерьми́, ло́шади → лошадьми́, лю́ди → людьми́ — take -ьми
unconditionally. But a further small set of nouns (e.g. **дверь**) has *both* forms available as a
genuine stylistic choice: -ями (двер**я́ми**) is the neutral/default form, while -ьми (двер**ьми́**)
is explicitly marked by the book as the "bookish" (i.e. literary/formal-register) alternative. This
is a rare case in this corpus of a register distinction encoded directly in a case ending rather
than in word choice — worth flagging for the eventual slang-mechanics analysis phase, since it shows
Russian register variation can attach even to inflectional morphology, not just the lexicon.
