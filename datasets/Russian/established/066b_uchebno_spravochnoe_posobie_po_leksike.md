## Grammar points

### Suffix-pair organizing principle (Раздел 2)

Unlike the noun section's organization by free-standing near-synonym semantic groups, the adjective
section is organized by shared derivational suffix contrasted against a shared root — a genuinely
useful morphological data point for Russian's fusional derivational system. Confirmed pairs/groups
covered in this shard: `-н-//-лив-` (бережный-бережливый ... шуточный-шутливый, ~14 pairs, p.39-58),
an isolated triple ГОРДЫЙ-ГОРДЕЛИВЫЙ and pair ДОЖДЕВОЙ-ДОЖДЛИВЫЙ appended to the -н-//-лив- set
without an explicit new suffix heading (pp.58-61), `-н-//-чив-` (обидный-обидчивый,
обманный-обманчивый, p.61-63), `-н-//-ск-` (генеральный-генеральский, соседний-соседский,
зрительный-зрительский, p.63-67), and `-н-//-ственн-` (starting p.67, headword list on p.67 names 8
pairs/triples: бедный-бедственный, безответный-безответственный,
дружный-дружественный-дружеский, искусный-искусственный, умный-умственный, явный-явственный,
двойной-двойственный-двояки, тройной-тройственный — only бедный-бедственный fully covered in this
shard's page range; the remaining 7 continue into the next shard).

### Suffix semantics comment (p.39, paraphrased)

`-н-` adjectives typically denote the mere presence of the quality named by the producing stem and
usually modify inanimate nouns. `-лив-` adjectives typically describe a person or that person's
action/behavior (a disposition/tendency, not a one-off state) — this maps cleanly onto Russian's
general adjectival-suffix semantics (quality-noun vs. propensity-noun derivation) and is the same
kind of systematic morphological fact that will matter for later slang-mechanics analysis of
suffixal productivity.

### Suffix semantics comment (p.63, paraphrased, for -н-//-ск-)

`-ск-` adjectives combine with inanimate nouns denoting things that can be owned (дом, одежда),
nouns denoting mental/psychological phenomena (интерес, любовь, привычка, внимание), and — from the
animate class — nouns denoting kinship relations (сын, дочь, жена) or people in someone's employ/
charge (адъютант, шофёр). `-н-` adjectives in this pairing more often form fixed terminological
collocations (генеральная репетиция, генеральный штаб, зрительный нерв) rather than a live
productive contrast — i.e. the -н-/-ск- split here tracks idiom-formation vs. live derivation rather
than a clean semantic opposition the way -н-/-лив- does.

### Register-tagged sub-senses found in this range

Per the extraction spec's register-annotation rule: РАСЧЁТНЫЙ sense 3 is tagged `(бухг.)` (accounting
register, p.46); СЛЁЗНЫЙ sense 1 is tagged `(анат.)` (anatomical/technical, p.47); СЛЕЗЛИВЫЙ sense 2
is tagged `(перен.)` (figurative, p.47); СОННЫЙ sense 2 is tagged `(перен.)` (p.50); and — the most
slang-mechanics-relevant find in this range — ГОРДЫЙ sense 3 is explicitly tagged `(разг.)`
(colloquial, p.58): a single headword whose core senses 1-2 are register-neutral but whose third
sense ("thinks oneself above others, condescending") is flagged by the source itself as colloquial —
a clean example of polysemy splitting across register within one lexeme, directly the kind of finding
the coverage rule's register-annotation clause is meant to catch.
