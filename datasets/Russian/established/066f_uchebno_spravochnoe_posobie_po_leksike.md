## Grammar points

### Idiom flags in this shard

Several fixed idioms surfaced whose meaning is not compositional from the headword's productive
senses, all worth flagging per the coverage rule's register/idiom-annotation clause: ветряная оспа
("chickenpox") is unrelated to ВЕТРЯНОЙ's wind-power sense; кровный враг / кровная вражда / кровная
месть / кровная обида form a small cluster of fixed figurative-intensifier idioms built on КРОВНЫЙ's
"deeply personal" sense; кровавый пот / до кровавого пота and кровавый цвет are similarly
lexicalized beyond КРОВАВЫЙ's compositional senses; ложное положение and в ложном свете are fixed
idiomatic uses of ЛОЖНЫЙ.

### Register note: КРОВНЫЙ sense 3 tagged `(разг.)`

Per the coverage rule's register-annotation clause: КРОВНЫЙ's third sense ("hard-earned, obtained
through toil" — кровный рубль, кровные деньги) is explicitly marked colloquial register by the
source, distinguishing it from the register-neutral kinship sense (1) and the figurative-but-neutral
"deeply personal" sense (2) — the same kind of polysemy-splits-across-register pattern already seen
with ГОРДЫЙ in `066b`.

### Structural shift: return to free-standing near-synonym pairs (РАЗНЫЙ-РАЗЛИЧНЫЙ)

After roughly 95 pages organized entirely around shared-suffix headword families (pp.39-132), the
book returns to the noun-section's original format — a `Комментарий` block followed by headwords
that do not share a derivational root — for РАЗНЫЙ-РАЗЛИЧНЫЙ. This may signal the adjective
section's remaining content (pp.134 onward) reverts to loose near-synonym groupings rather than
suffix families; later shards should confirm whether this is a one-off exception or the new pattern
for the section's remainder.
