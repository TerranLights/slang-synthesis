## Grammar points

### ВОЗГЛАВЛЯТЬ/РУКОВОДИТЬ/УПРАВЛЯТЬ/ЗАВЕДОВАТЬ/КОМАНДОВАТЬ: a domain-scoped five-way "lead/direct" cluster

Each verb occupies a distinct institutional/domain niche rather than differing purely by degree:
ВОЗГЛАВЛЯТЬ (formal top rank in an org or leading a movement), РУКОВОДИТЬ (general direction of
people/activity, broadest collocability), УПРАВЛЯТЬ (operating a mechanism, or directing an
organization, extending idiomatically to self-control of emotions), ЗАВЕДОВАТЬ (headship of a small
institution/department specifically, explicitly excluding production enterprises), and КОМАНДОВАТЬ
(exclusively military rank/operation-direction, with a distinct perfective for the "utter a command"
sense). This is a genuinely rich domain-partitioned near-synonym cluster, useful for later
mechanics-analysis work on how Russian lexicalizes institutional-authority distinctions.

### ПРОСИТЬ/ТРЕБОВАТЬ/ВЕЛЕТЬ/ПРИКАЗЫВАТЬ/РАСПОРЯЖАТЬСЯ: an obligation-gradient cluster with a clean register axis

A five-way "request/demand/order" cluster organized along a single obligation-strength axis (from
"the other party may, at discretion" through "the other party must") crossed with a register axis
(ВЕЛЕТЬ flagged colloquial, ТРЕБОВАТЬ/ПОТРЕБОВАТЬ and ПРИКАЗЫВАТЬ/ПРИКАЗАТЬ formal/official,
РАСПОРЯЖАТЬСЯ its own "acts on believed necessity" niche) — directly comparable to the earlier
ПРОСИТЬ-adjacent discrimination clusters and a strong candidate for cross-referencing against any
future `analysis/` writeup on Russian politeness/register systems (see also `063_10_urokov_
russkogo_rechevogo_etiketa.md`'s two-axis formality×intensity template, per the checklist).

### СООБЩАТЬ/ОБЪЯВЛЯТЬ/ЗАЯВЛЯТЬ/ИЗВЕЩАТЬ/ИНФОРМИРОВАТЬ: register cline opens, continues into next shard

This new five-way "inform/announce" cluster (opened p.267) again crosses register (СООБЩАТЬ neutral
→ ОБЪЯВЛЯТЬ/ЗАЯВЛЯТЬ official → ИНФОРМИРОВАТЬ bookish) with content-type (ОБЪЯВЛЯТЬ for facts/events
generally, ЗАЯВЛЯТЬ specifically for the speaker's own position/stance, ИЗВЕЩАТЬ for the notifier's
own forthcoming action specifically) — ЗАЯВЛЯТЬ/ЗАЯВИТЬ, ИЗВЕЩАТЬ/ИЗВЕСТИТЬ, and ИНФОРМИРОВАТЬ's
own entries are not yet reached in this page range and continue into the next shard.
