## Grammar points

No new non-redundant grammar content — this lesson is an explicit grammatical-review chapter
("Обобщение некоторых грамматических тем"), consolidating который/свой/себя/imperative material
already covered in depth throughout this corpus.

### Reading-text content (paraphrased)

- **"200 лет со дня рождения Александра Сергеевича Пушкина"** (pp. 206–209) — bicentennial
  retrospective on Pushkin's life and legacy, including his period of internal exile; supplies the
  elevated/literary-register vocabulary cluster above.
- **"Наташа, ангел мой!"** (pp. 218–219) — an account of the circumstances leading to Pushkin's
  fatal duel (an anonymous letter, society gossip about his wife Наталья Гончарова, mounting
  provocation) and a modern 1991 memorial service reuniting descendants of both families. Supplies
  the idiom-rich provocation/patience vocabulary above — a fitting, richly idiomatic close to the
  lesson sequence.
