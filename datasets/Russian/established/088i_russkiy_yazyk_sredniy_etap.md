## Grammar points

No new non-redundant grammar content — unprefixed motion verbs are among the most extensively
covered topics in this corpus already.

### Reading-text content (paraphrased)

- **"Судьба актёра"** (воспоминание актёра С. Менжинского, pp. 127–128) — memoir-style piece about
  actor Александр Остужев, who continued a celebrated stage career after losing his hearing.
  Supplies the theatrical/perseverance vocabulary cluster above.
- **"Театральный музей Бахрушина"** (pp. 140–141) — history of the Bakhrushin Theater Museum in
  Moscow, founded by merchant/philanthropist А.А. Бахрушин; supplies the museum/philanthropy
  vocabulary above, largely institutional/formal-register rather than slang-bearing.
