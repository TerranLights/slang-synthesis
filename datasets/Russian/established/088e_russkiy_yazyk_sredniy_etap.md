### Reading-text content (paraphrased)

- **"Пять минут гениальности"** (a фантастическая история / sci-fi story, pp. 71–72) — notably the
  one explicitly sci-fi-genre reading text found in this book so far; concerns a scientist who
  briefly experiences genius-level insight via an experimental "miracle pill" (чудо-таблетка) before
  it wears off, revealing him as otherwise "бездарный." Directly on-genre for this project's own
  sci-fi framing device, though the text itself is pedagogical filler rather than slang-bearing.
- **"Татьянин день"** (pp. 79–80) — history of Moscow State University's founding (1755, by decree
  of Elizaveta Petrovna at Lomonosov's urging) and of Св. Татьяна as patron saint of students/the
  university, explaining the modern "Татьянин день" (Students' Day) holiday.
- Пословицы и афоризмы об учении (proverbs about learning) and a brief excerpt of the student hymn
  *Gaudeamus* (p. 81) — not separately tabulated; low unique lexical value beyond the vocab box
  above.
