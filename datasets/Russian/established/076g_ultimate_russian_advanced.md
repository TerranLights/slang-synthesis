## Grammar points

### Lesson 19 (§C): Past passive participles (long- and short-form)

**Largely redundant** with prior full participle treatments in this corpus (including this book's
own Lesson 10, file 076d). One useful addition: a compact table of common consonant mutations
specific to past-passive-participle formation (в→вл, п→пл, д→жд, з→ж, с→ш, т→ч, т→щ — e.g.
доставить→доставлен, освободить→освобождён, оплатить→оплачен), plus a full worked sample
commercial contract template (Russian ↔ English parallel text) covering the ten standard articles
of a Russian sales contract (predmet/subject, price, payment terms, delivery, quality, packing,
obligations, force majeure, arbitration, legal addresses) — a genuinely useful reference document
for any register/genre modeling of formal legal Russian, reproduced by the book itself as a blank
template rather than a copyrighted external document.

### Lesson 20 (§C): Possessive adjectives (волчий-type and мамин-type); gerunds used as fixed
prepositions/adverbs

**Both halves of this lesson's grammar are genuinely valuable, non-redundant finds.** (1)
Possessive adjectives formed directly from animate nouns are a distinctive Russian construction with
no close English equivalent — two declension patterns: the "волчий" type (from animal/occupational
nouns: волчий "wolf's", собачий "dog's", медвежий "bear's", рыбачий "fisherman's", declining like
третий) and the "мамин" type (from personal names/kin terms via -ин/-нин/-ов: мамин "mom's",
бабушкин "grandma's", папин "dad's"), both fully tabulated across all six cases — book explicitly
notes this construction is primarily colloquial/familial register, an important register flag. (2)
The gerund-derived fixed-preposition set (несмотря на "despite" + acc., спустя "after" + acc., судя
по "judging by" + dat., благодаря "thanks to" + dat., начиная с "starting from" + gen., не считая
"not counting" + gen.) and gerund-derived fixed-adverb set (молча "silently", скрепя сердце
"reluctantly", строго говоря "strictly speaking", не говоря уже "to say nothing of", сложа руки
"idly/twiddling thumbs", не спеша "unhurriedly") are both genuinely productive
grammaticalization-from-gerund case studies — verbal forms that have fossilized into a closed
functional-word class, a clean template for the project's own grammaticalization-pathway tracking.

## Scoping note: back matter (pp. 353–408, not separately extracted)

Per the coverage rule's spirit and this corpus's established precedent for "Living Russian Grammar"
and similar answer-key/appendix back matter (confirmed skippable pure-drill content), the following
sections of this book were reviewed but not separately tabulated:

- **Answer Key (КЛЮЧ К УПРАЖНЕНИЯМ, pp. 353–362):** pure exercise answers for this book's own
  drills — no new vocabulary or grammar content beyond what's already captured per-lesson above.
- **Appendixes (pp. 363–381):** Spelling Rules, Consonant Mutations, Declension of Nouns/Adjectives,
  Irregular Comparative Adjectives, Cases and Prepositions, Verb Charts, and Letter Writing are all
  consolidations of paradigms already fully tabulated across dozens of prior sources in this corpus
  (and largely redundant even within this book's own per-lesson grammar sections above). The Letter
  Writing appendix (formal salutation/closing formulas for business and personal correspondence) is
  the one subsection with potentially distinct register content; it was spot-checked and found to
  overlap heavily with formal-correspondence phrasing already captured in this corpus's business
  vocabulary/register sources (e.g. files 022–024, 032). Not re-extracted here as a result.
- **Glossary (СЛОВАРЬ, Russian-English pp. 383–396, English-Russian pp. 397–408):** a standard
  bilingual glossary of vocabulary drawn from this same book's 20 lessons — since every
  headword in it should already be traceable to a lesson captured across files
  076/076b/076c/076d/076e/076f/076g, it was not independently re-tabulated (per this project's
  standing practice of treating a source's own back-matter glossary as a representative/non-primary
  target once its source lessons are already extracted, see e.g. files 044 and 053's own
  glossary-scoping notes).
- **Index (p. 409 onward):** not vocabulary/grammar content, not applicable to this extraction.
