## Grammar points

No new non-redundant grammar content — prefixed motion verbs are among this corpus's most
extensively covered topics already.

### Reading-text content (paraphrased)

- **"Багульник"** (pp. 145–146) — a story about a stray/found dog (breed markers боксёр/такса
  appear in the vocab box, suggesting a mixed-breed dog), named after the flowering shrub;
  supplies plant and dog-related vocabulary.
- **"Есенин и наши меньшие братья"** (pp. 157–158) — a piece on poet Сергей Есенин's poetry about
  animals, tying to his famous line/idiom "наши меньшие братья." Supplies poetry-criticism
  vocabulary and the idiom above.
