## Grammar points

### ОСОБЕННО/ОСОБО: register cline with a quasi-technical/official sub-sense

A compact two-way register pair (neutral ОСОБЕННО vs. bookish/expressive ОСОБО) that, unusually for
this source, includes an example suggesting the "bookish" member can specialize into a fixed
official/bureaucratic category rather than remaining purely stylistic — the "особо трудные условия"
example (implicitly linked to early-retirement eligibility criteria in Soviet/Russian labor law) is
a small but genuine data point for how register-marked vocabulary can calcify into technical/legal
terminology, worth flagging for later mechanics-analysis work on formal-register lexicalization.

### ПОЛНОСТЬЮ/ЦЕЛИКОМ: completeness split along omission-avoidance vs. non-separation axes

A tight semantic pair sharing "full extent," split by *what kind* of completeness is at stake:
ПОЛНОСТЬЮ frames completeness as the absence of omissions/abridgments (an editorial/procedural
framing — nothing was left out), while ЦЕЛИКОМ frames completeness as the absence of division/
separation (a physical/structural framing — nothing was cut off or apart). A clean minimal-pair
example from the source's own comparison exercise (paraphrased): a pie placed on the table ЦЕЛИКОМ
(whole, undivided) vs. eaten ПОЛНОСТЬЮ (nothing left over) — the same object, two different
completeness axes depending on which stage of the action is described.

### СЕЙЧАС/ТЕПЕРЬ: the "present tense" adverb pair, split by comparison-to-the-past

Continues this book's recurring "same core meaning, split by whether a comparison-to-the-past is
implied" pattern (paralleling ДАЛЕКО/ВДАЛЕКЕ's information-structure split and ПРОСИТЬ-cluster's
obligation-gradient split from earlier shards): СЕЙЧАС marks a pure present-moment reading with no
implied contrast, while ТЕПЕРЬ obligatorily implies a "this differs from/follows from how things
were before" contrast — visible in the source's own exercise sentences where ТЕПЕРЬ consistently
appears in "before X, now Y" constructions that would read oddly with СЕЙЧАС substituted.

### ТОЖЕ/ТАКЖЕ: same-action vs. same-quality, plus a genuine word-order convention

The richest single pair in this final shard: beyond the identical-action-vs-identical-quality
semantic split (with a clean, source-supplied three-sentence minimal-triple demonstrating the
distinction under controlled variation), the source adds an explicit `!!! Обратите внимание`
word-order convention (ТОЖЕ typically pre-verbal, ТАКЖЕ typically post-verbal) as a *separate*
observation from the semantic rule — an explicit acknowledgment that near-synonym choice in Russian
can be governed by both meaning *and* an independent syntactic-positioning tendency, worth
cross-referencing against any future word-order-focused mechanics-analysis work.

### В САМОМ ДЕЛЕ/НА САМОМ ДЕЛЕ: the book's final pair, closing on a discourse-particle use

Both phrases are multi-word adverbial idioms (not single lexemes) discriminated the same way as
every other pair in this chapter, and both develop a secondary use as an emotionally-colored
colloquial discourse particle/parenthetical for emphasis — directly comparable to the
discourse-particle content already flagged from `062_..._russian_punctuation_and_related_symbols.md`
and `036_chelovek_v_sovremennom_mire.md`'s discourse-particle inventories, making this a third
independent data point for a future consolidated discourse-particle mechanics writeup. НА САМОМ
ДЕЛЕ's extra third sense (correcting a false belief, per its own contrastive exercise sentences)
gives it a stronger "actually, in truth" contrastive flavor than В САМОМ ДЕЛЕ, whose sense 2 stays
closer to simple emphasis/confirmation — a subtle but real difference beyond pure stylistic register.
