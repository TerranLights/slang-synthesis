## Grammar points

No new non-redundant grammar content beyond what's already covered elsewhere for impersonal
dative-subject constructions and aspect-in-the-imperative.

### Reading-text content (paraphrased)

- **"Голос и глаз"** (pp. 113–115) — a story involving a blind character and themes of voice,
  perception, and inner beauty vs. appearance; supplies emotionally-marked descriptive vocabulary
  (voice quality, tenderness, fear/hope contrasts).
- **"Любовь"** (pp. 123–124) — a story contrasting two young women working at a rural tavern, one
  conventionally pretty (Аня) and one considered exceptionally unattractive (Маргарита, described
  with the pejorative урод/уродина vocabulary above) — a genuinely rich source of both period-marked
  register content (сударь, «Что вам угодно?») and pejorative/insult vocabulary, both flagged above
  for the mechanics-analysis phase.
