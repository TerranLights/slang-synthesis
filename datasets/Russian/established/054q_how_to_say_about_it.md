## Grammar points

### И ещё/А ещё: elaborating the same situation, not marking a parallel one (Раздел VI, subsection Б, pp. 251–254)

This closing subsection introduces a construction functionally distinct from every conjunction covered
in subsection А (тоже, и, также): rather than flagging a second, separate-but-similar event, И
ещё/А ещё continue elaborating on the **same** situation already under discussion, adding a further
fact about it. The book's opening worked contrast (p. 251) makes the distinction concrete: "Мне
говорили, что по этому роману будут снимать фильм. А ещё я слышал, что главную роль в фильме будет
играть мой любимый актёр" — the second sentence doesn't describe a parallel, analogous event; it adds
a further detail to the *same* film-adaptation situation already introduced. The book stresses (p.
251) that what matters is the *content* uniting the two sentences as "one situation," not matching
grammatical subjects — "Мне говорили" and "Я слышал" have different surface subjects but both reduce
to the same underlying "я знаю [this]" situation.

A further worked minimal-pair set (pp. 253–254, "И ещё нам надо выполнить..." vs. "И нам ещё надо
выполнить..." vs. "И нам надо ещё выполнить...") shows ещё's position within the и ещё/а ещё frame
can shift without changing the construction's core function — unlike the highly position-sensitive
scope-marking behavior of тоже documented in `054o`, here the positional variants appear to be closer
to free stylistic variants rather than marking distinct scope readings, though the book does not
draw this comparison explicitly itself (a distinction worth flagging for later cross-referencing
against the тоже positional-scope material). The section's own closing generalization (p. 252's
framed "Обратите внимание!" box) is the single functional claim: constructions built with а ещё/и
ещё mark their added information as **more significant** than what came before it in the exchange —
a genuine pragmatic-weight claim distinct from anything drawn for the тоже/и/также system in
`054o`–`054p`. A closing exercise (p. 254) drills the full four-conjunction system (и/тоже/также/и
ещё-а ещё) together across a final set of comparison and elaboration prompts (garden produce, chores,
baking), closing the book's teaching content.
