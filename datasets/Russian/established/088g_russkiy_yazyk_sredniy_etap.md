## Grammar points

No new non-redundant grammar content — purpose/condition/concession constructions (чтобы, если,
если бы не + instr., хотя, несмотря на то что) are standard and already covered extensively
elsewhere in this corpus.

### Reading-text content (paraphrased)

- **"Борис Кустодиев"** (pp. 96–98) — biographical piece on the painter, known for provincial/
  folk-life scenes; "Высказывания об искусстве" (p. 99) gives brief quotes about art, low unique
  lexical value.
- **"Случай с Шаляпиным"** (pp. 109–110) and **"Случай с Тосканини"** (p. 111) — two anecdotes about
  famous performers (bass Фёдор Шаляпин; conductor Артуро Тосканини), each involving backstage
  bets/incidents. Supplies the theatrical/musical vocabulary and idiom cluster above — genuinely one
  of the richer idiom-density lessons so far.
