## Grammar points

### Раздел II «Имя прилагательное» concludes at p.223

With НАСТОЯЩИЙ-ДАННЫЙ-ЭТОТ (p.221-223), the adjective section that began on p.39 (`066b`) concludes,
spanning roughly 185 printed pages and covering, by rough count across all adjective shards
(`066b` through this file), on the order of 30 suffix-family sets plus roughly 25 free-standing
near-synonym pairs/triples/groups — the largest single grammatical-category section in the book by a
wide margin (compared to the noun section's ~14 groups over 32 pages).

### Раздел III «Глаголы» (Verbs) begins at p.224 — a structural shift to government-pattern-first entries

Verb entries here are organized around aspectual pairs (imperfective/perfective given together as
one headword, e.g. ВЕРИТЬ/ПОВЕРИТЬ) and defined primarily through case/preposition government
(кому, чему, в кого, во что, + инф.) rather than through adjective-style attributive collocation
lists — a natural difference given verbs' argument-structure-centered grammar. This is directly
relevant to Russian's fusional/six-case typology tracking this project's checklist calls for: each
verb entry effectively doubles as a rection/government-pattern table. Three pairs are captured in
this shard: ВЕРИТЬ/ПОВЕРИТЬ vs. ДОВЕРЯТЬ/ДОВЕРИТЬ (belief-in-truthfulness vs. entrusting/judging
competence), ВЫДУМЫВАТЬ/ВЫДУМАТЬ vs. ПРИДУМЫВАТЬ/ПРИДУМАТЬ (fantastical invention vs. realizable
invention), and the opening of ОБДУМЫВАТЬ/ОБДУМАТЬ vs. ПРОДУМЫВАТЬ/ПРОДУМАТЬ (decisive weighing vs.
thorough detail-review).

### Aspectual irregularity flagged: ПЕРЕДУМАТЬ has no perfective/imperfective partner

The very last lines captured on p.228 flag a genuine aspectual irregularity — ПЕРЕДУМАТЬ lacks a
partner verb of the opposite aspect entirely, while РАЗДУМАТЬ's would-be imperfective counterpart
(раздумывать) has drifted to an unrelated meaning (a case of aspectual "defectiveness"/lexicalized
divergence rather than a clean pair) — this is exactly the kind of verbal-aspect quirk the
Russian morphological-typology note in `00_Extraction_Checklist.md` flags as worth capturing
in detail; the next shard should verify and fully capture this entry's discrimination.
