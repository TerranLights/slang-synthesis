## Grammar points

### Youth-subculture label formation (this chapter's standout finding)

This chapter's "Средний класс" section catalogs a small, explicit taxonomy of ~2004 Russian youth consumer-subcultures by name — неформалы, славянофилы, эстетки, «в джинсах», «странные люди» — with rough population percentages given for each. Structurally these labels are formed by (a) recycling an existing intellectual/political-history term with a narrowed pop-culture sense (славянофилы), (b) a French/Greek-derived adjective nominalized and feminized (эстетки), (c) a clipped adjective from a broader "unofficial" sense (неформал), and (d) bare prepositional phrases used directly as group-labels («в джинсах»). All four strategies recur independently elsewhere in Russian slang formation and are worth carrying forward as a small, concrete "labelling toolkit" for the mechanics-analysis and eventual conlang-synthesis phases.
