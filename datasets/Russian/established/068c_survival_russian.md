## Grammar points

### Present/past tense of жить, verb aspect review — Lesson 4 (p. 46)

жить conjugates жив-у́/жив-ёшь/жив-ёт/жив-ём/жив-ёте/жив-у́т (stressed -ё- throughout, an
irregular stress pattern flagged against the -ать/-ять Conjugation I paradigm already given for
рабо́тать). Past tense formation confirmed as regular across verb classes: infinitive stem + -л
(m.) / -ла (f.) / -ло (n.) / -ли (pl.), e.g. жить→жил/жила́/жи́ло/жи́ли, рабо́тать→рабо́тал(а/о/и),
учи́ться→учи́лся/учи́лась/учи́лось/учи́лись (reflexive -ся/-сь appended after the gender/number
suffix). быть is irregular but follows the same suffix pattern: был/была́/бы́ло/бы́ли.

### Adjective declension (nominative case, four agreement forms) — Lesson 4 (p. 46-47, Table 12)

Masculine singular: -ой (stressed) or -ый/-ий (unstressed, -ий after velars к/г/х, sibilants
ш/щ/ч, and ж). Feminine singular: -ая (hard stem) / -яя (soft stem). Neuter singular: -ое (hard) /
-ее (soft). Plural (all genders): -ые (hard) / -ие (soft). Each form answers a distinct
interrogative-adjective question word: како́й? (m.), кака́я? (f.), како́е? (n.), каки́е? (pl.) — a
grammatical-gender-marked question-word paradigm, itself a further point of fusional agreement
beyond the possessive-pronoun agreement already seen in Lesson 2.

### Prepositional case oddities: -ия nouns and country-name exceptions — Lesson 4 (p. 47, Table 13)

Country names ending in -ия take -ии in the prepositional (Росси́я→в Росси́и, Фра́нция→во
Фра́нции, Герма́ния→в Герма́нии, Ита́лия→в Ита́лии, А́нглия→в А́нглии, Япо́ния→в Япо́нии) — a
distinct declension outcome from the plain -е ending shown for most nouns in Lesson 3's
prepositional table (университе́т→в университе́те). The source explicitly flags a contrastive
"but" group of exception country/city names that do NOT follow the -ия pattern and take a plain -е
locative instead: Москва́→в Москве́, Аме́рика→в Аме́рике, Кита́й→в Кита́е, Коре́я→в Коре́е — worth
noting that Коре́я actually still ends in the visually similar -ея (not -ия) and behaves like the
regular -е group rather than the -ия group, a genuine minimal-pair-style declension-class boundary
case the source doesn't call out explicitly but which is visible directly from its own table.
