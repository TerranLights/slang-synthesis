## Grammar points

### 89 〜あっての
Meaning: "existing only because of ~ / possible only thanks to ~" — usually in the pattern "AあってのB," meaning "B exists precisely because A exists." Connects to `[noun] + あっての`. Example (p.144): 日々の練習あっての勝利だ — "This victory exists only because of daily practice." Fixed idiom given: 命あっての物種 — "nothing matters without your life" (i.e., don't take reckless risks).

### 90 〜からある／〜からの
Meaning: "as much/many as ~ / no less than ~" — emphasizes that a quantity (size, weight, length, number) is strikingly large. Connects to `[noun (quantity expression)] + からある`.

### 92 〜しまつだ
Meaning: "it ended up at the point of ~ / things got to the state of ~" — always describes an undesirable outcome, often used with このしまつだ / そのしまつだ / あのしまつだ. Connects to `[verb/i-adj/na-adj/noun noun-modifying form] + しまつだ`. Example (p.146): 弟は昔から両親に手を焼かせていましたが、ついに家出をして、警察のやっかいにまでなるしまつです — "My younger brother has long given our parents trouble, and it's finally come to the point of running away from home and becoming a police matter."

### 93 〜だに
Meaning: (sense A, given here) "even ~ / merely ~ (is enough to...)" — a literary intensifying particle emphasizing that even the smallest instance of something produces a strong effect. Connects to `[noun/verb, literary pattern]`; explicitly marked 文語体 (literary style).

### 95 〜でなくてなんだろう／〜でなくてなんであろう
Meaning: "if this isn't ~, then what is? / this can only be ~" — a rhetorical question used to assert something very strongly. Connects to `[noun] + でなくてなんだろう`. Example (p.153): 自分の命を犠牲にして多くの人を救ったあの男が英雄でなくてなんだろう — "That man who sacrificed his own life to save many others — if he isn't a hero, then what is?"

### 96 〜てやまない
Meaning: "~ deeply and unceasingly" — expresses a strong, enduring wish or feeling the speaker holds continuously. Connects to `[verb-て form] + やまない`. Example (p.153): 君たちの努力を期待してやまない — "I sincerely and continually hope for your efforts [to succeed]."

### 98 〜というところだ／〜といったところだ
Meaning: "roughly ~ / about ~, at most/best" — expresses that a given figure or state is about as much/good as it gets, neither more nor less. Connects to `[verb-dictionary form / noun] + というところだ`. Example (p.155): パーティーの参加者は、70人から80人といったところでしょう — "Party attendance will probably be around 70 to 80 people, give or take."

### 99 〜ないではおかない
Meaning: "will inevitably ~ / will make sure that ~ happens" — expresses near-forcible inevitability, that the speaker (or circumstance) will not let things rest without a certain result occurring. Connects to `[verb-ない form] + ではおかない`. Example (p.155): 今度こそ、本当のことを言わせないではおかないぞ — "This time, I will absolutely make you tell the truth." Cross-referenced to No.54 〜ずにはおかない as a near-synonym.

### 101 〜にして
Meaning: two senses given, both taking `[noun] + にして`. (A) "precisely because ~ / even ~" (often in the pattern 〜にしてはじめて, "only once reaching ~ does..."), used when the preceding noun denotes a high or admirable degree — e.g. この味は経験を積んだプロの料理人にしてはじめて出せる味だ, "this flavor can only be produced once one is an experienced professional chef." (B) "being ~ and at the same time" — e.g. 彼は出版社の社長にして詩人でもある, "he is a publishing-company president and, at the same time, a poet."

### 102 〜の至り
Meaning: "utterly ~ / the height of ~" — an old, formulaic expression of extreme degree, usually in set phrases of gratitude or embarrassment. Connects to `[noun] + の至り`. Example (p.162–163): こんな立派な賞をいただきまして、光栄の至りです — "To receive such a splendid award is the height of honor." Fixed idiom given: 若気の至り — "a youthful folly," i.e., something rash done because of youth.

### 103 〜の極み
Meaning: "the utmost ~ / ~ to the extreme" — similar to の至り, expressing that a degree has reached its absolute limit ("there is nothing more ~ than this"). Connects to `[noun] + の極み`. Example (p.163): 徹夜続きで、疲労の極みに達している — "After consecutive all-nighters, I've reached the absolute limit of exhaustion."

### 104 ひとり〜だけでなく／ひとり〜のみならず
Meaning: "not only ~ (alone), but also..." — usually in the pattern "ひとりAだけでなくBも," meaning "not just A alone, but B too." ひとり〜のみならず is the literary-register variant. Connects to `ひとり + [verb/i-adj/na-adj noun-modifying form; noun] + だけでなく`. Example (p.164): 喫煙はひとり本人に有害であるのみならず、周囲の者にとっても、有害かつ迷惑なものである — "Smoking is harmful not only to the smoker alone but is also harmful and a nuisance to those around them."

### 105 〜を皮切りに(して)／〜を皮切りとして
Meaning: "starting with ~ / taking ~ as the starting point (and continuing from there)" — used when a series of similar events follows on from an initial one. Connects to `[verb-dictionary/た form + の; noun] + を皮切りに`. Example (p.165): 朝の連続ドラマに主演したのを皮切りに、彼女はスターへの道を歩み始めていた — "Starting with her starring role in the morning drama series, she began her path toward stardom."
