**Morpheme breakdown (illustrative — academic-discipline compounds).** This lesson's 新出語 appendix's ことばのネットワーク section is a productive, near-complete demonstration of Japanese's `[field]+学` ("study of ~") derivational pattern:

> **心理学** (しんりがく) = 心理 (しんり, "psychology/mentality") + 学 (がく, "study of") — the same `~学` suffix recurs across nearly every entry above (物理学, 化学, 地理学, 医学→薬学 is irregular, 生物学, 農学, 水産学, 法律学, 政治学, 経済学, 経営学, 教育学, 社会学, 歴史学, 言語学), productively naming an academic discipline from its subject-matter noun. 機械工学/土木工学/電子工学 instead compose with 工学 ("engineering") as the bound head rather than bare 学, showing the pattern nests (工学 itself = 工 "craft/engineering" + 学 "study of").
