## Grammar points

*(Points 59-115. See Part I's header note for sourcing/paraphrase conventions.)*

### 59. 〜こそ／〜からこそ — "very; only; precisely" (emphasis)

Emphasizes a word, phrase, or clause. 今度こそ試合に勝ちたい "This time, (of all times,) I want to
win the match." あなたが手伝ってくれたからこそ、仕事が早くできたのです "It's precisely because you
helped that the work got done quickly."

### 60. 〜さえ／〜でさえ — "even" (non-conditional)

Used with non-conditional clauses (contrast point 47's conditional 〜さえ〜ば); shows an extreme
example implying lesser cases are also included. The particles が and を are omitted before さえ; all
other particles are retained. 専門家でさえわからないのだから、わたしたちには無理でしょう "If even
the experts don't know, it's probably impossible for us."

### 61. 〜など／〜なんか／〜なんて — "such as; etc." / dismissive tone

(A) giving an example or speaking lightly — 「彼など詳しいと思いますよ」 "I think someone like him,
for instance, would know"; (B) making light of or belittling something — 忙しくて、テレビなど見てい
られない "I'm too busy to even think about watching TV or anything like that."

### 62. 〜に関して(は)／〜に関しても／〜に関する — "concerning; regarding"

Same meaning as 〜について but more formal. 経済に関する本はたくさんある "There are many books
concerning economics."

### 63. 〜に加えて／〜に加え — "in addition to"

電気代に加えて、ガス代までが値上がりした "In addition to electricity, even the gas rate went up."

### 64. 〜にこたえて／〜にこたえ／〜にこたえる／〜にこたえた — "in response to"

社員の要求にこたえて、労働時間を短縮した "In response to employee demands, working hours were
shortened."

### 65. 〜に沿って／〜に沿い／〜に沿う／〜に沿った — "along with; in line with"

決まった方針に沿って、新しい計画を立てましょう "Let's make a new plan in line with the policy
that's been decided."

### 66. 〜に反して／〜に反し／〜に反する／〜に反した — "contrary to; against"

人々の予想に反し、土地の値段が下がり続けている "Contrary to people's expectations, land prices keep
falling."

### 67. 〜に基づいて／〜に基づき／〜に基づく／〜に基づいた — "based on"

彼の意見は、長い経験に基づくものだから納得できる "His opinion is convincing because it's based on
long experience."

### 68. 〜にわたって／〜にわたり／〜にわたる／〜にわたった — "spanning; over"

Describes a period of time, space, or scope. 兄は5時間にわたる大手術を受けた "My older brother
underwent major surgery spanning five hours."

### 69. 〜ばかりか／〜ばかりでなく — "not only A, but also B"

Connects a normally-expected first item with a normally-unexpected second one; sometimes paired
with さえ. アンナさんは頭がいいばかりでなく、親切で心の優しい人です "Anna isn't only smart — she's
also kind and warm-hearted."

### 70. 〜はもとより／〜はもちろん — "not only A, but also B (as a matter of course)"

〜はもとより is the more polite/keigo variant. 私が生まれた村は、電車はもとより、バスも通っていない
"The village I was born in doesn't have trains, let alone buses."

### 71. 〜もかまわず — "regardless of; not minding"

人の迷惑もかまわず、電車の中で携帯電話で話している人がいる "There are people who talk on their cell
phone on the train, not caring that it bothers others."

### 72. 〜をこめて — "with all one's (feeling); full of" (〜を込めて)

Used with emotion nouns. 母はわたしのために心をこめて、セーターを編んでくれた "My mother knitted me
a sweater with all her heart."

### 73. 〜を通じて／〜を通して — "throughout (time) / through (medium)"

(A) throughout a period — あの地方は、1年を通じて雨が多い "That region has a lot of rain throughout
the year"; (B) through a medium/intermediary — 社長は忙しいから、秘書を通じて頼んだほうがいい "The
president is busy, so it's better to make requests through his secretary."

### 74. 〜をめぐって／〜をめぐる — "regarding; surrounding (a contested topic)"

その法案の賛否をめぐって、活発な議論が交わされた "Lively debate was exchanged regarding whether to
support or oppose the bill."

### 75. 〜あまり — "because of too much ~; so ~ that ~"

Marks a cause involving excessive action or feeling. 驚きのあまり、声も出なかった "I was so
surprised that I couldn't even make a sound."

### 76. 〜一方(で) — "on the one hand ~, on the other hand ~"

Describes two concurrent, contrasting actions/states (distinct from point 36's "only keeps
increasing" sense of 一方だ). 私の仕事は夏は非常に忙しい一方、冬は暇になる "My work is extremely
busy in summer, while in winter it becomes slow."

### 77. 〜上で(は)／〜上の／〜上でも／〜上での — "after/upon ~" / "from the viewpoint of ~"

(A) a preparatory action before a more important one — みんなの意見を聞いた上で決めました "I decided
after hearing everyone's opinions"; (B) viewpoint, usually followed by advice/warning — 法律の上で
は平等でも、現実には不平等なことがある "Even when equal under the law, there can be inequality in
reality."

### 78. 〜かぎり(は)／〜かぎりでは／〜ないかぎり(は) — "as long as; as far as"

Four sub-senses: (A) defining a duration — 学生であるかぎり、勉強するのは当然だ "As long as one is a
student, studying is naturally expected"; (B) a specific range/limit of knowledge — 電話で話したか
ぎりでは、彼はそんなに怒っていませんでした "As far as I could tell from our phone call, he wasn't
that angry"; (C) a maximum-effort limit — 力のかぎりがんばろう "Let's do our best with all our
strength"; (D) a condition under which something is/isn't done — 雨や雪が降らないかぎり、毎日ジョギ
ングを欠かさない "As long as it doesn't rain or snow, I never skip my daily jog."

### 79. 〜(か)と思うと／〜(か)と思ったら — "as soon as (noticed); almost immediately"

As soon as one notices a thing, it (or the situation) has already changed. 空が暗くなったかと思った
ら、大粒の雨が降ってきた "No sooner had the sky darkened than large raindrops started falling."

### 80. 〜か〜ないかのうちに — "just as; at that instant; no sooner than"

チャイムが鳴るか鳴らないかのうちに、先生が教室に入って来た "The teacher entered the classroom just
as the bell started ringing."

### 81. 〜からいうと／〜からいえば／〜からいって — "from the standpoint of; as for"

中国は人口からいうと世界一だが、人口密度は日本よりずっと低い "China is the world's largest by
population, but its population density is far lower than Japan's."

### 82. 〜からといって — "just because ~ (doesn't mean ~)"

Introduces a reason/idea while conveying disapproval; what follows is often contradictory to what
precedes it. お金があるからといって偉いわけではない "Just because one has money doesn't make one
admirable."

### 83. 〜から見ると／〜から見れば／〜から見て(も) — "seen from the point of view of"

どこから見ても、あの人は紳士だ "No matter how you look at it, that person is a gentleman."

### 84. 〜きり(だ) — "just; only" / "unchanged since"

(A) "that and nothing else" — 今朝コーヒーを飲んだきりで、何も食べていない "I've had nothing but
coffee this morning, nothing else to eat"; (B) a situation remains unchanged after something
happened — あの人は、アメリカへ行ったきりだ "That person went to America and that's the last (we
heard)." Becomes っきり in conversation.

### 85. 〜くせに — "although; and yet" (contemptuous tone)

Expresses the speaker's contempt, anger, or disagreement. 彼は学生のくせに、高級車に乗っている "He's
just a student, and yet he drives a luxury car."

### 86. 〜ことから — "because" (A is the origin/basis for B)

この鳥は目のまわりが白いことから、メジロと呼ばれている "Because this bird has white around its
eyes, it's called a mejiro (white-eye)."

### 87. 〜ことに(は) — "to my (surprise, etc.)"

Emphasizes feelings/emotions. 驚いたことに、私の祖父と恋人のおじいさんは小学校時代の友達だったそう
だ "To my surprise, my grandfather and my partner's grandfather turned out to have been childhood
friends."

### 88. 〜上(は)／〜上も／〜上の — "from the point of view of ~"

Often preceded by a Sino-Japanese (kanji) word. 健康上の理由で会社を辞めた "He quit the company for
health reasons."

### 89. 〜た末(に)／〜た末の／〜の末(に) — "after ~ (for a long time); in the end"

States the end result reached after a long period of thinking/discussing/effort. いろいろ考えた末、
会社を辞めることにした "After much deliberation, I decided to quit the company."

### 90. 〜たところ — "when (X was done, Y was the result)"

Often paired with 〜してみたところ. 新しいワープロを使ってみたところ、とても使いやすかった "When I
tried using the new word processor, it turned out to be very easy to use."

### 91. 〜ついでに — "while doing A, take the chance to also do B"

デパートへ買い物に行ったついでに、着物の展示会を見て来た "While I was at the department store
shopping, I took the chance to also see the kimono exhibition."

### 92. 〜というと／〜といえば — "speaking of ~"

(A) introducing a related topic — スポーツの祭典というと、まずオリンピックですな "Speaking of
sporting festivals, the Olympics come to mind first"; (B) recalling something associated with the
topic — イタリアといえば、パスポートを落として困ったことを思い出します "Speaking of Italy, it
reminds me of the trouble I had losing my passport there."

### 93. 〜といったら — "how (surprising/extreme) ~ is"

Expresses admiration, surprise, or horror. その景色の美しさといったら、口で言い表せないほどです
"The beauty of that scenery is beyond words."

### 94. 〜というより — "rather than ~; more like ~"

あの人はきれいというより上品だ "That person is less 'pretty' and more 'elegant.'"

### 95. 〜ば〜ほど — "the more ~, the more ~"

練習すればするほど上手になります "The more you practice, the better you get."

### 96. 〜ほど — "the more one thing changes, so does the other"

頭がいい人ほど自慢しない "The smarter a person is, the less they tend to boast." (A distinct use of
ほど from point 32.)

### 97. 〜かのようだ／〜かのような／〜かのように — "like; as if" (metaphor)

激しい雨と風は、まるで台風が来たかのようだ "The violent rain and wind were as if a typhoon had
come."

### 98. 〜つつある — "be gradually doing; continuing to" (progress)

景気は徐々に回復しつつある "The economy is gradually recovering."

### 99. 〜てたまらない／〜てしようがない — "can't help ~ing; can't stand/bear ~"

Expresses that the speaker can't cope with a feeling; the subject is limited to the first person.
大学に合格して、うれしくてたまらない "I passed the university entrance exam and am so happy I can't
stand it."

### 100. 〜てならない — "can't help ~ing; irresistibly ~"

Slightly more formal than 〜てたまらない; can't substitute for 〜てたまらない with spontaneous verbs
like 思える／思い出される. 母の病気が心配でならない "I can't help worrying about my mother's
illness."

### 101. 〜にきまっている — "it's definitely the case that"

Often used in conversation. ぜんぜん練習していないんだから、今度の試合は負けるにきまっている "Since
we haven't practiced at all, we're definitely going to lose this next match."

### 102. 〜にすぎない — "nothing more than; merely"

These express that something is nothing more than what's stated in terms of amount, degree, or
significance. これは少年犯罪の一例にすぎない "This is nothing more than one example of juvenile
crime."

### 103. 〜に相違ない — "definitely; without a doubt"

この地域の民族紛争を解決するのは難しいに相違ない "There's no doubt that resolving this region's
ethnic conflict will be difficult."

### 104. 〜に違いない — "there's no mistake that" (softer than 〜に相違ない, point 103)

かぎがない。どこかに落としたに違いない "The key is gone — there's no mistake I dropped it
somewhere."

### 105. 〜べき／〜べきだ／〜べきではない — "should; ought to"

Expresses the speaker's judgment that someone should do something. どんなに親しい仲でも、借りた物
はきちんと返すべきだ "No matter how close you are, you should properly return what you've
borrowed."

### 106. 〜(より)ほか(は)ない／〜ほかしかたがない — "nothing else to do but"

だれにも頼めないから、自分でやるほかはない "I can't ask anyone, so I have no choice but to do it
myself."

### 107. 〜向きだ／〜向きに／〜向きの — "suitable for; aimed at (general suitability)"

このスキー場は初心者向きです "This ski resort is suitable for beginners."

### 108. 〜向けだ／〜向けに／〜向けの — "intended/made for (deliberate targeting)"

Distinguished from 向き (point 107) by deliberate design/targeting rather than general suitability.
高齢者向けに、安全や住みやすさを考えた住宅が開発されている "Housing designed with safety and
livability in mind is being developed specifically for the elderly."

### 109. 〜もの(です)か — "absolutely not; by no means" (strong denial)

Often becomes もん(です)か in casual conversation between friends. あの絵が名作なものか。ピカソの模
倣にすぎない "That painting, a masterpiece? Absolutely not — it's nothing but an imitation of
Picasso."

### 110. 〜ものだ／〜ものではない — four sub-senses

(A) admiration/grief/heartfelt feeling — いつか京都に行ってみたいものだ "I really do want to visit
Kyoto someday"; (B) common-sense generalization — 地震のときは、だれでもあわてるものだ "Anyone would
panic during an earthquake — that's just how it is"; (C) a light suggestion/command — 人の陰口を言
うものではありません "One shouldn't speak ill of others behind their back"; (D) nostalgic
recollection of the past — 子供のころ、いたずらをして、よく父に叱られたものだ "As a child, I used to
get scolded by my father often for mischief."

### 111. 〜わけだ — two sub-senses

(A) states a natural effect of a given reason — 暗いわけだ。蛍光灯が1本切れている "No wonder it's
dark — one of the fluorescent lights is burnt out"; (B) explains what results/has resulted from an
action — 5パーセントの値引きというと、1万円の物は9,500円になるわけですね "So a 5% discount means a
10,000 yen item becomes 9,500 yen, right?"

### 112. 〜わけではない／〜わけでもない — "it doesn't mean that" (partial denial)

Denies what's implied by the previous statement. 甘い物が嫌いなわけではありませんが、ダイエットして
いるんです "It's not that I dislike sweets — I'm just dieting."

### 113. 〜っけ — sentence-final recollection/confirmation marker

Used when trying to remember or confirm something. そうだ。今日はお母さんの誕生日だっけ "Oh right —
today's my mother's birthday, wasn't it?"

### 114. 〜とか — "I heard that" (secondhand information)

Used when stating information heard or read; also used in letter-closing phrasing. 昨日はこの冬一番
の寒さだったとか "I heard yesterday was the coldest day of the winter so far."

### 115. 〜どころではない／〜どころではなく — "far from; not even close to"

Indicates something is far from the expected state. 年末から母の入院で、お正月どころではありません
でした "With my mother hospitalized since the end of the year, New Year's was far from a priority."
