# Japanese — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: A Guide to Learning Hiragana and Katakana (Part II: Katakana)

**Source:** Kenneth G. Henshall with Tetsuo Takagaki, *A Guide to Learning Hiragana and Katakana*
(Charles E. Tuttle Company, 1990), Part II "Katakana," PDF pp. 70–108 (printed pp. 71–108). See
`001_guide_to_hiragana_katakana.md` for the book-level grammar points, source-wide vision-reading
notes, and the hiragana character inventory; this file continues that extraction with katakana.

**Coverage note.** Every katakana symbol-practice page and every mini-review/review page in Part II
is covered below — all distinct vocabulary items, no sampling. Vision-read throughout (no text
layer); no marginalia was found in this section of the book. `Source Type` = `grammar_reference`;
`Transcription Confidence` = `n/a`; `Attested Era` = contemporary, source published 1990, for all
entries; `Weight/Frequency` = `—` throughout (pedagogical ordering, not real frequency data).
`Vision Reading Confidence` = `plausible_unverified` unless noted — a genuine, flagged risk in this
section specifically: several katakana symbols are visually similar and easy to misread on a scan
(シ *shi* vs. ツ *tsu* vs. ン *n* vs. ソ *so*, distinguished mainly by stroke direction/slant), so
entries containing these symbols were double-checked against their printed romanization gloss
rather than read from the kana shape alone.
