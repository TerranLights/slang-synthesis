all ~22 review pages, no sampling. One page (printed p. 25) carried excluded handwritten marginalia

separate from the printed drill rows. `Source Type` = `grammar_reference`; `Transcription
Confidence` = `n/a`; `Attested Era` = contemporary, source published 1990, for all entries;
`Weight/Frequency` = `—` throughout. `Vision Reading Confidence` = `plausible_unverified` unless
noted (`verified` for common words independently cross-checkable against general Japanese-learner
