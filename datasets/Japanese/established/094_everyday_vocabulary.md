2. **加藤由紀子・伊藤かんな・河合瑞恵・藤森秀美・三輪郁子・三輪実希・六郷明美 (Kato Yukiko et al.). *留学生のための分野別語彙例文集* ("Field-Specific Vocabulary & Example-Sentence Collection for International Students").** PDF: `Ryuugakusei no tameno Bunyabetsu Goi Reibunshuu Vocabulary Building.pdf`, 158 pages. Cited below as **Book 2**.

Both PDFs were produced by an I.R.I.S. scanner (per `pdfinfo`) and confirmed via `pdftotext` to have **no usable text layer at all** (extraction on sampled pages returned zero characters) — these are pure image/vision-read sources, not a font-substitution-cipher case. PDF metadata gives a 2009 scan/creation date for both; no explicit print-edition copyright year was visible on the sampled front-matter pages, so **Attested Era is given as "contemporary (source scanned 2009; exact print-edition year unconfirmed)"** for every row below, per the spec's coarse whole-book dating allowance.

### Coverage strategy (both sources are large image-only scans — sampled, not exhaustive)

**Book 1** (251 pp.) is built around 33 narrative lessons (課) — twin diary entries ("クリスの日記" / "まり子の日記") and dialogue scenes that each introduce a handful of noun+verb/adjective **collocations** (bolded in the printed text, e.g. 電話代がかかる, 給料が上がる). The book's own back matter (roughly pp. 227–249) compiles **two cumulative indices**: (1) a general collocation index, headwords in gojūon order, each listing the verbs/adjectives it collocates with plus page references back into the 33 lessons; (2) a second, explicitly labeled "動詞・形容詞" (Verbs/Adjectives) index that cross-indexes the same collocation data from the opposite direction (verb/adjective headword → collocating nouns). Given the page count, this extraction samples index pages spread across the full gojūon range (あ/う/お/か/き/く/さ/す/じ/な/ね/の/は/ひ/む/や/ゆ/よ/ら/り/れ/ろ/わ, from both indices) rather than reading all 251 pages, plus two narrative-lesson pages (Lesson 1 "結婚記念日のプレゼント" p.19, Lesson 7 "オフィスにて" p.49) sampled directly for in-context collocation usage.

**Book 2** (158 pp.) is organized into 10 domain chapters (国際社会/環境/経済/産業/仕事/家庭/教育/福祉/健康/文化[伝統的・現代的]), each a 五十音-ordered glossary of field-specific vocabulary with **English/Chinese/Korean translations**, an example sentence, and sub-entries (set phrases, antonyms via ⇔, synonyms via /) with their own translations and examples — genuinely a specialized multilingual dictionary, not a plain wordlist. The book's own front-matter Table of Contents (pp. iv–vi) prints the **complete headword list for all 10 chapters** (confirmed vision-read in full), so this extraction captures that full per-chapter headword census but samples (~12–16 per chapter, ~150 total) for full English glosses rather than vision-reading all ~500 headwords' individual dictionary entries; three headwords (意志, 意識, 一員, Ch.1 p.3) were read in full dictionary-entry form (gloss + example sentence + sub-collocations) as a depth check and are flagged `verified` below. Chapters 6 (家庭) and 7 (教育) TOC captures were obtained from a facing-page spread where the scan crops two physical pages per image (the known "two-pages-per-scan" PDF gotcha) — their headword lists are drawn from the captured portion and may be very slightly incomplete at the margin.

No marginalia (handwritten annotation) was observed on any sampled page of either source — all sampled content is clean typeset print.
