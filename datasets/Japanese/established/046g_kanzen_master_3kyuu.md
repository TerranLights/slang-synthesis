## Grammar points

### 114. こ・そ・あ・ど
Full demonstrative paradigm table given: これ/この/こんな/こう (proximal, "this"), それ/その/そんな/
そう (medial, "that [near listener/previously mentioned]"), あれ/あの/あんな/ああ (distal, "that [far
from both]"), どれ/どの/どんな/どう (interrogative, "which/what kind/how"). この本はいくらですか ("how
much is this book?"); あんな家に住みたいです ("I want to live in a house like that"). Usage note
distinguishing あ〜 from そ〜: あ〜 is used for something known to both speaker and listener; そ〜 is
used otherwise, and specifically when referring to something just previously mentioned —
illustrated with a worked contrast: 田中さんを知っていますか answered with その人はどんな人ですか (listener
doesn't know Tanaka — そ, referring to the just-mentioned name) vs. answered あの人はほんとうに
おもしろい人ですね (listener has personally met Tanaka — あ, shared direct knowledge).

### 115. 〜さ
"<used to nominalize adjectives>." い-adj(〜い→〜) / な-adj(〜な→〜) + さ (irregular いい→よさ). その
へやの広さはどのくらいですか ("how large is that room?" — 広い→広さ). Distinguished from the related
nominalizer 〜み by example only (not explicitly contrasted in-text, but both forms coexist for some
adjectives per the drill set, e.g. 重い→重さ/重み both attested in practice items).

### 116. 〜らしい
"typical of 〜, just like 〜" — distinct from item 77's conjectural らしい (hearsay/appearance), this
is a lexical suffix meaning "typical/characteristic of." N + らしい. きょうは春らしい天気です ("today's
weather is typical spring weather"); あの人のやりかたはあまり男らしくないと思います ("I don't think that
person's way of doing things is very masculine").

### 117. 〜の／こと
"<used to nominalize the previous clause>" — two nominalizers with partly overlapping, partly
distinct distribution. V plain form + の or こと, per a usage table:
- With verbs of direct sensory perception (見る/見える/聞こえる), only の is grammatical: 鳥がとんでいる
  のが見える ("I can see a bird flying") — ×...ことが見える is explicitly marked ungrammatical in a
  parallel example about hearing someone sing.
- With verbs like 好き/きらい/聞く/知る/わすれる, either の or こと works interchangeably: 電話するの／こと
  をわすれた ("I forgot to make the phone call").

### 118. 〜ということ
"the fact that 〜 <used to change a sentence into a noun clause>." V plain form + という + こと.
レストランに電話してもだれも出ないということはきょうは休みかもしれません ("the fact that no one answers
when I call the restaurant might mean they're closed today").

### 119. 〜ちゃ／じゃ
"<colloquial contraction of 〜ては／では>." 〜てはいけません→〜ちゃいけません; 〜ではいけません→〜じゃい
けません. これ、食べてもいい？ ／ ううん。それはおきゃくさまに出すおかしだから、食べちゃだめよ ("can I eat
this? — no, that's a snack for guests, so you mustn't eat it" — casual-register contraction of
食べては). まだ中学生なんだから、おさけを飲んじゃいけませんよ ("you're still a junior-high student, so
you mustn't drink alcohol" — contraction of 飲んでは).

### 120. 〜の・〜だい・〜かい
"<sentence-final particle used in informal questions>" — three variants compared in a single table
by compatibility with yes-no vs. WH-questions:
- **(1) 〜の** — V/い-adj plain form, or な-adj/N plain form (〜だ→〜な), + の. Works for both
  yes-no (行くの？) and WH-questions (いつ行くの？). きのう来なかったね。どうしたの ("you didn't come
  yesterday — what happened?").
- **(2) 〜だい** — V/い-adj plain form + ん, or な-adj/N plain form (〜だ→〜なん), + だい. Works only
  for WH-questions (いつ行くんだい？); invalid for a bare yes-no question (×行くだい). 田中さんは何時に
  来るんだい ("what time is Tanaka-san coming?") — an older-generation/masculine colloquial register.
- **(3) 〜かい** — V/い-adj plain form, or な-adj/N plain form (〜だ→〜な), + （の）かい. Works only for
  yes-no questions (行く（の）かい？); invalid for a WH-question (×いつ行くかい). かいぎは10時からなのかい
  ("is the meeting from 10 o'clock?").
