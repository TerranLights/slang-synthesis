### Classical month-name pair (弥生/師走)

This batch's 師走 (Shiwasu, "another old name for December," p. 76/78) pairs directly with 弥生
(Yayoi, "another old name for March") captured in `105_moons_months_seasons.md`. The reader
consistently calls out these classical lunar-calendar month names (一つの古い呼び方) at the head of
only some of its twelve chapters — worth noting for the mechanics-analysis phase as evidence that
the old 和風月名 (Japanese-style month name) system was still considered pedagogically relevant
vocabulary for a pre-intermediate learner as of this book's publication.

### Era-dating corroboration

天皇誕生日 (Ten'nō Tanjōbi, "the Birthday of Emperor Akihito") in the December section independently
corroborates the "approximately early 1990s" publication-date estimate already suggested by the
1990年現在 statistic found in the May vocabulary (see `105b_moons_months_seasons.md`): Emperor
Akihito's reign (Heisei era) ran 1989–2019, so this source must postdate January 1989.
