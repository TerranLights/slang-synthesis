# Japanese — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Nihongo Notes 7 & 8 (Situational Japanese 2 & 3)

**Source:** Osamu Mizutani & Nobuko Mizutani, *Nihongo Notes 7: Situational Japanese 2* (The Japan
Times, 1st ed. May 1986) and *Nihongo Notes 8: Situational Japanese 3* (The Japan Times, 1st ed.
September 1987). Full volumes: vol. 7 is a compilation of 74 columns from *The Japan Times*
(Sept. 16, 1984 – Feb. 9, 1986), printed pp. 8–155 (index at p.156); vol. 8 is a compilation of 74
columns (Feb. 16, 1986 – Jul. 19, 1987), printed pp. 8–155 (index at p.156). This is the
second/third pair in the 10-volume *Nihongo Notes* series (see sibling file `established/054` for
vols. 9–10, which confirms the same series format).

**Coverage note:** No text layer in either PDF (`pdftotext` returns empty output on both); both
volumes were vision-read from rendered page images (150dpi, `pdftoppm`). Each PDF page image is a
two-printed-page spread, not one page per image — confirmed empirically against the printed page
numbers visible on each spread (image *i* → printed pages `2i-2`/`2i-1`, stable across both volumes
and matching the mapping already confirmed for vols. 9–10 in `established/054`). Front matter
(title page, copyright, foreword, table of contents, romanization note) occupies printed pp. 1–7
(images 1–4); the 74 situational-usage essays occupy printed pp. 8–155 (images 5–78), one essay per
2-page spread, exactly matching the table-of-contents entry list in each volume; the word/phrase
index (p.156+) was not extracted (a pure index, no new content). Every grammar/usage essay and every
distinct illustrative vocabulary/phrase item across all 148 essays (74 + 74) is captured; direct
example sentences are reproduced only as short illustrative quotes, per copyright discipline — the
surrounding narrative framing and explanatory prose is paraphrased throughout.

**Vision-reading guard note:** Both volumes' title pages (image 1) carry handwritten/stamped library
accession marks in the margin — vol. 7: a date stamp reading "MAR 15 19[xx]" in the upper-right
margin; vol. 8: two separate date stamps, "MAR 15 1999" (upper right) and "NOV 20 1994" (rotated,
left margin), plus what appears to be a library ink stamp overlapping part of the printed title
graphic itself ("nihongo notes 8"/the decorative title box). All of these are post-print library
additions, not typeset source content, and were excluded entirely from extraction — flagged here per
the spec's marginalia-guard requirement. No other handwritten marginalia (underlining, answer-filling,
marginal notes) was observed anywhere else in either volume.
