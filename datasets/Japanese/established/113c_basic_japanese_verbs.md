## Grammar points

### Compound verbs (verb stem + attaching verb)

A compound verb is formed by attaching a second verb directly to the pre-*masu* stem of a first
verb, modifying the first verb's meaning. The source lists 14 common attaching verbs, each
combinable with a a characteristic set of stems:

- ***-akiru*** 飽きる — "to be sick/tired of" (commonly with *tabe-*, *mi-*, *yomi-*, *kiki-*,
  *asobi-*)
- ***-au*** 合う — "to do something together/with each other" (commonly with *hanashi-*,
  *tasuke-*)
- ***-dasu*** 出す — "to start suddenly/nonvolitionally" (commonly with *furi-*, *hashiri-*,
  *naki-*)
- ***-hajimeru*** 始める — "to begin" (commonly with *narai-*, *hataraki-*, *yomi-*, *kaki-*)
- ***-kaeru*** 換える — "to change/switch" (commonly with *nori-*, *kai-*, *kaki-*)
- ***-kiru*** 切る — "to do something to completion/to the end" (commonly with *tsukai-*,
  *tsukare-*, *nige-*, *tabe-*)
- ***-machigaeru*** 間違える — "to mistake, to mis-(verb)" (commonly with *kaki-*, *yomi-*,
  *kiki-*, *nori-*)
- ***-mawaru*** 回る — "to (verb) around" (commonly with *aruki-*, *hashiri-*, *tobi-*, *asobi-*)
- ***-naosu*** 直す — "to do over, to fix" (commonly with *mi-*, *tsukuri-*, *kaki-*, *yomi-*)
- ***-nareru*** 慣れる — "to become used to" (commonly with *tsukai-*, *haki-*, *sumi-*,
  *tsukuri-*)
- ***-nokosu*** 残す — "to leave undone" (commonly with *yari-*, *tabe-*)
- ***-owaru*** 終わる — "to finish" (commonly with *yomi-*, *tsukuri-*, *kaki-*, *arai-*)
- ***-tsuzukeru*** 続ける — "to continue, keep doing" (commonly with *hataraki-*, *aruki-*,
  *hanashi-*)
- ***-wasureru*** 忘れる — "to forget (to do)" (commonly with *kake-*, *shime-*, *ake-*, *kaki-*,
  *keshi-*)

Illustrative example (paraphrased, one of the source's 14): *tabe-akimashita* (from *taberu* "to
eat" + *-akiru*) means "became sick of eating" — "I am sick of eating this food" (*kono ryōri wa mō
tabe-akimashita*). (List of Compound Verbs, unpaginated appendix)

### Noun + *suru* compound verbs

A large and productive class of Japanese verbs consists simply of a noun followed by *suru* "to
do" (e.g. *benkyō suru* "to study," from the noun *benkyō* "study"). These conjugate by conjugating
*suru* alone — e.g. the *masu* form of *annai suru* is *annai shimasu*, the causative is *annai
sasemasu*. Because the noun component carries essentially all of the lexical/semantic content and
*suru* contributes only the (entirely regular, irregular-Group-3) verbal conjugation, each is listed
below as an ordinary Vocabulary entry rather than requiring its own grammar point. (List of Suru
Verbs, unpaginated appendix)
