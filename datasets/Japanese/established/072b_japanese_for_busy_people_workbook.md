## Coverage note — mostly redundant drill material, skipped per coverage rule

This workbook is overwhelmingly **handwriting-practice drill**: hiragana/katakana stroke-order charts,
voiced/semi-voiced syllable (゛/゜) writing boxes, and reading-then-copying exercises. It introduces
essentially no new grammar and very little new vocabulary beyond what the Kana Textbook
(`072_japanese_for_busy_people.md`) already covers — per the coverage rule, this repetitive kana-
writing-practice content was **not extracted row-by-row**. However, a "Reading & Writing" exercise on
p.24 surfaced several vocabulary items not captured from the sampled Kana Textbook pages, so those are
recorded below as a genuine non-redundant supplement. No marginalia (handwritten or otherwise) was
found on the sampled pages — this is a clean workbook scan with printed answer-key text only.
