# Japanese — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Nihongo Chuukyuu J301 — Lessons 8–9

**Source:** Toki Satoshi et al., *日本語中級J301 — 基礎から中級へ* (English edition), 3A Network, 1995/2004. See `083_nihongo_chuukyuu_j301.md` for full source citation, methodology notes, and all sampled grammar points (Lesson 9's grammar points are written up there in full).

**Coverage note:** Lesson 8 (クジラと日本人 — "Whales and the Japanese") covers the whaling/cultural-relativism debate (international criticism of whaling vs. Japanese perspectives on whale meat as traditional food/protein source); Lesson 9 (サルの視力検査 — "A Monkey's Eyesight Test") is a science-reading passage about a psychological experiment on primate color/pattern perception. No marginalia found on these pages.
