## Grammar points

### zutsu (ずつ) — the equal-division particle
Paraphrased from p.259, the dictionary's final entry. Marks an equal division or rate across a group or repetition ("a little at a time", "one at a time", "two each"), closing out the alphabetical particle list that began with "ba" in part 1 of this series.
