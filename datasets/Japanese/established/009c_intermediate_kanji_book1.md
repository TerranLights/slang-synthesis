## Grammar points

### Kanji sharing one phonetic marker share the same on-reading — Lesson 5 §①
The book gives nine sets of kanji that all share a single phonetic component (音記号) and,
correspondingly, a single on-reading: 官/館/管(カン←官), 故/固/個(コ←古), 交/効/郊(コウ←交),
正/政/整(セイ←正), 低/底/邸(テイ←氏), 点/店/貼(テン←占), 洋/様/養(ヨウ←羊), 周/週(シュウ←周),
通/痛(ツウ←甬). This is presented as a practical mnemonic/decoding tool: once a phonetic marker's
reading is known, an unfamiliar kanji containing it can often be read correctly on sight.

### Same-sound suffixes distinguished by kanji and domain — Lesson 5 §②
Several on-readings correspond to multiple distinct kanji used as suffixes in different domains:
カ splits into 科 (school subjects: 国語科,数学科; hospital departments: 内科,外科,耳鼻科,婦人科,
精神科) and 課 (company sections: 会計課,人事課,総務課,営業課); キ splits into 器 (tools: 食器,
楽器,消火器; body organs: 臓器,消化器,呼吸器,循環器) and 機 (machines: 洗濯機,掃除機,印刷機,飛行機);
ショ splits into 所 (places: 役所,事務所,案内所,裁判所) and 署 (institutions: 警察署,消防署,税務署);
リョウ splits into 料 (materials: 原料,資料,衣料,食料,調味料; fees: 手数料,入場料,水道料,使用料) and
量 (quantities: 雨量,積雪量,排水量,使用量).

### Three distinct kinds of Japanese homophone (同音語) — Lesson 5 §③–⑤
The book distinguishes three homophone phenomena that look superficially similar but require
different disambiguation strategies: (1) **accent-distinguished homophones** — identical kana
reading, different pitch accent, e.g. 一階/一回, 向上/工場, 伝記/電気, 公害/郊外; (2) **meaning-
distinguished homophones with no shared kanji at all** — pairs like 改訂/海底, 回転/開店, 期間/機関/
器官/気管, 期限/紀元, 休場/球場, 公害/郊外 (also appears here with a second, distinct accent
pattern), 口外/校外, 主張/首長, 消化/消火, 生計/整形, 生前/整然, 創造/想像, 低下/定価, 良心/両親 —
these must simply be memorized individually, since neither spelling nor accent reliably
disambiguates all of them; (3) **homophones sharing exactly one common kanji**, where the shared
kanji anchors the meaning and the differing kanji carries the distinguishing nuance — 機械/機会
(share 機), 教会/協会 (share 会), 強調/協調, 共同/協同, 強力/協力, 故人/個人, 個体/固体, 国家/国歌,
署名/書名, 整理/生理, 不通/普通, 保健/保険, 洋式/様式.

### 形声文字 (phonetic-ideographic characters) and phonetic-marker derivation chains — Review 1
Most Japanese kanji (the book states over 80%) are 形声文字: a semantic radical (部首) combined with
a phonetic component (音記号) that cues the on-reading, e.g. 食(しょくへん,"eat")+官(カン)=館
("building, hall," on-reading カン); 木(きへん,"tree")+官=棺 ("coffin," カン); 竹(たけかんむり,
"bamboo")+官=管 ("pipe," カン). Phonetic markers themselves can spawn derived phonetic markers in
a chain: from 古(コ, "old") the marker 固(コ, "firm") and 胡(コ) both derive, and from 胡 a further
set — 湖("lake"),糊("paste"),瑚(as in 珊瑚"coral") — all keep the コ reading. The review also
catalogs standalone-vs-phonetic-marker reading shifts (e.g. 央 alone=オウ as in 中央, but as a
phonetic marker=エイ in 英語,映画; 己 alone=コ/キ, as marker=キ in 日記,紀元; 者 alone=シャ, as marker
=ショ in 署名,残暑; 占 alone=セン, as marker=テン in 書店,点数) and phonetic markers whose derived
characters shift vowel/consonant somewhat unpredictably (台 タイ/ダイ → 始シ,治ジ; 主シュ → 注チュウ,
住ジュウ; 方ホウ → 訪ホウ,放ホウ,防ボウ; 丁テイ/チョウ → 訂テイ,停テイ,庁チョウ,町チョウ; 各カク →
格カク,客キャク,落ラク). Finally, it documents **exceptional-reading family members**: groups of
kanji sharing one phonetic marker where all but one member share the predicted reading — 寺時持待特
(all ジ except 特=トク), 反飯販阪返 (all ハン except 返=ヘン), 古故固湖苦 (all コ except 苦=ク),
青晴静精情 (all セイ except 情=ジョウ), 交郊効校較 (all コウ except 較=カク), 白泊拍迫百 (all ハク
except 百=ヒャク) — explicitly framed as a tool for *predicting* an unknown kanji's reading from its
phonetic marker while flagging that single-member exceptions do occur and must be memorized
separately.
