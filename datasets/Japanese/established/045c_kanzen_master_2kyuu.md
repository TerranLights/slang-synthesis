## Grammar points

*(Points 116-173, the book's final Part. See Part I's header note for sourcing/paraphrase
conventions.)*

### 116. 〜からして — "even (bare minimum)" / "given; judging from"

(A) shows a bare minimum that can't even be met, implying everything above it is impossible by
association — 田舎は空気からして違う "In the countryside, even the air is different (let alone
everything else)"; (B) shows the foundation for an opinion/judgment — 彼の健康状態からして、登山は無
理だろう "Given his health, climbing a mountain is probably out of the question."

### 117. 〜からすると／〜からすれば — "for; seen from the point of view of" / "given; judging from"

(A) viewpoint — 親からすると、子供はいくつになっても子供で、心配なものだ "From a parent's
perspective, a child is always a child, no matter their age, and always something to worry about";
(B) basis for a guess — あの車は形からすると10年ぐらい前のものだと思う "Judging from its shape, I
think that car is from about ten years ago."

### 118. 〜からには／〜からは — "since; now that; once"

Shows a strong connection between the preceding and following clauses, in the speaker's own
opinion. 試合に出るからには、勝ちたい "Now that I'm entering the match, I want to win."

### 119. 〜ことだから — "typical of; can be expected that"

Explains something expected, followed by a presumption. あなたのことだからきっといい論文が書けると
思います "Knowing you, I'm sure you'll write a great thesis."

### 120. 〜だけ／〜だけあって／〜だけに／〜だけの — three sub-senses

(A) "as expected of/becoming of" — この大学は歴史が長いだけあって伝統がある "As befits its long
history, this university has real tradition"; (B) cause-and-effect "because/so" — 昨年はみかんが不
作で、値段が高かっただけに今年の豊作がうれしい "Because mandarins had a poor harvest last year and
prices were high, this year's bumper crop is all the more welcome"; (C) "as long/much as" —
ほしいだけ本が買えたらどんなにいいだろう "How nice it would be to be able to buy as many books as I
wanted."

### 121. 〜にあたって／〜にあたり — "at; on the occasion of; before"

Indicates the occasion of doing something (formal register). 開会にあたって、ひとことごあいさつ申
し上げます "On the occasion of the opening, allow me a few words of greeting."

### 122. 〜にかけては／〜にかけても — "when it comes to; as far as ~ is concerned"

数学にかけては、彼女はクラスでいつも一番だった "When it comes to math, she was always first in her
class."

### 123. 〜に際して／〜に際し／〜に際しての — "when; on the occasion of"

Used on special occasions. 留学に際して、先生や友人から励ましの言葉をもらった "On the occasion of
studying abroad, I received encouraging words from teachers and friends."

### 124. 〜に先立って／〜に先立ち／〜に先立つ — "before; preceding; prior to" (formal)

映画の一般公開に先立って、主演女優のサイン会が開かれた "Prior to the film's general release, a
signing event with the lead actress was held."

### 125. 〜にしたら／〜にすれば／〜にしても — "from the perspective of ~"

あの人にしたら、わたしたちの親切はかえって迷惑かもしれません "From that person's perspective, our
kindness might actually be an inconvenience."

### 126. 〜につき — "due to; because" (formal, often on signs/notices)

雨天につき、試合は延期いたします "Due to rain, the match will be postponed."

### 127. 〜につけ／〜につけて(は)／〜につけても — "each time; whenever" / "either way"

(A) each time — 戦争のニュースを聞くにつけ、心が痛む "Every time I hear news of war, it pains me";
(B) regardless of which extreme — 暑いにつけ寒いにつけ、うちのおばあさんは体の調子が悪いと言う "Hot
or cold, my grandmother always says she feels unwell."

### 128. 〜のもとで／〜のもとに — "based on; under; owing to"

Under the control or influence of someone/something. 鈴木教授のご指導のもとで、卒業論文を完成させた
"I completed my graduation thesis under Professor Suzuki's guidance."

### 129. 〜はともかく(として) — "regardless of; leaving aside"

Marks something to be set aside from consideration in the following sentence. この洋服は、デザイン
はともかく、色がよくない "Leaving the design aside, the color of these clothes isn't good."

### 130. 〜をきっかけに(して)／〜をきっかけとして — "through; by; since" (an event sparks a change)

留学をきっかけに、自分の国についていろいろ考えるようになった "Studying abroad became the catalyst
for me to think a lot about my own country."

### 131. 〜を契機に(して)／〜を契機として — "since" (an event triggers subsequent change, more formal/written than 130)

オイルショックを契機に新エネルギーの研究が進められた "Triggered by the oil shock, research into new
energy sources advanced."

### 132. 〜を〜として／〜を〜とする／〜を〜とした — "as" (object-marked variant of point 1)

Cross-referenced explicitly by the book as functionally the same as point 1, but using を makes the
marked noun the grammatical object rather than the subject, shifting emphasis elsewhere in the
sentence. チンさんを先生として、中国語の勉強会を開いた "We held a Chinese study group with Mr. Chin
as the teacher."

### 133. 〜あげく(に) — "after much ~; in the end; finally" (typically a negative outcome)

その男はお金に困ったあげく、銀行強盗を計画した "After much financial trouble, the man ended up
planning a bank robbery."

### 134. 〜以上(は) — "since; now that; as long as"

日本に住んでいる以上、日本の法律を守らなければならない "As long as one lives in Japan, one must obey
Japanese law."

### 135. 〜上は — "now that; since" (close to point 134)

弁護士になると決めた上は、苦しくてもがんばらなければならない "Now that I've decided to become a
lawyer, I must persevere no matter how hard it is."

### 136. 〜ことなく — "without"

ロボットは24時間休むことなく働いている "The robot works around the clock without resting."

### 137. 〜際(は)／〜際に — "when; on the occasion of; at the time of"

パスポートを申請する際に必要なものを教えてください "Please tell me what's needed when applying for a
passport."

### 138. 〜つつ／〜つつも — "while" / "although" (conflicting clauses)

(A) two concurrent actions — 人は皆、お互いに助け合いつつ生きている "Everyone lives while helping
one another"; (B) conflicting sentiment — 悪いと知りつつ、うそをついてしまった "Knowing it was wrong,
I still ended up lying."

### 139. 〜てからでないと／〜てからでなければ — "unless/until I ~, I can't ~"

この地方では4月になってからでなければ、桜は咲かない "In this region, cherry blossoms won't bloom
until April comes."

### 140. 〜といっても — "although (I/they) say/said ~" (clarifying something potentially misleading)

庭があるといっても猫の額ほどです "Although I say I have a garden, it's about the size of a cat's
forehead (i.e., tiny)."

### 141. 〜どころか — "far from; not just; even"

彼は貯金するどころか借金だらけだ "Far from saving money, he's buried in debt."

### 142. 〜としたら／〜とすれば — "if (a hypothetical scenario)" / "if, from someone's perspective"

(A) speculating about a scenario — もし、もう一度生まれ変われるとしたら、男と女とどちらがいいですか
"If you could be reborn, would you rather be a man or a woman?"; (B) speculating from someone's
perspective — 足の悪い人が地下鉄を利用するとしたら、どんな設備が必要だろうか "If a person with a leg
impairment were to use the subway, what facilities would be needed?"

### 143. 〜ないことには — "if not ~, then not ~ (either)"

実際に読まないことには、この小説のおもしろさはわからないだろう "Unless you actually read it, you
won't understand what's interesting about this novel."

### 144. 〜ながら — "although; even though; nevertheless" (written/formal register)

Flagged as used in written or formal spoken Japanese. 一緒に暮らしていながら、母の病気に気づかなかっ
た "Even though we lived together, I didn't notice my mother's illness." Also forms set phrases:
残念ながら "regrettably" (lit. "while being regrettable").

### 145. 〜にしては — "for; considering" (deviating from an expected standard)

兄はアメリカに20年いたにしては英語が下手だ "For someone who lived in America for 20 years, my
brother's English is poor."

### 146. 〜にしろ／〜に(も)せよ／〜にしても — "even though; even if" / "whether ~ or ~"

(A) "even though/if" — いくら体が丈夫であるにせよ、そんなに働いたら病気になりますよ "No matter how
robust your health, working that much will make you sick"; (B) "whether ~ or ~" — ビールにしろ、日
本酒にしろ飲んだら運転できない "Whether beer or sake, you can't drive after drinking either."

### 147. 〜にかかわらず／〜に(は)かかわりなく — "regardless of" (no logical connection between clauses)

このゲームは、年齢や性別にかかわらず、だれでも楽しめる "This game can be enjoyed by anyone,
regardless of age or gender."

### 148. 〜にもかかわらず — "though; despite; in spite of"

Distinguished from point 147 by specifically showing no relation to a preceding event/situation. 彼
は熱があるにもかかわらず、サッカーの試合に出場した "Despite having a fever, he played in the soccer
match."

### 149. 〜ぬきで(は)／〜ぬきに(は)／〜ぬきの／〜をぬきにして(は)／〜はぬきにして — "without"

Doing something without a normally-present element. 社長ぬきでは、この件を決めることはできない "This
matter can't be decided without the president (present)."

### 150. 〜のみならず — "not only ~ but also; besides" (formal)

彼女は成績優秀であるのみならず、人柄も申し分ない "Not only are her grades excellent, her character
is beyond reproach too."

### 151. 〜反面／〜半面 — "on the other hand"

この薬はよくきく反面、副作用がある "This medicine is highly effective, but on the other hand it has
side effects."

### 152. 〜ものなら — "if ... is (seemingly) possible, then ..."

Used for near-impossible/unobtainable scenarios; becomes もんなら in conversation. 自分一人でやれる
ものならやってみなさい "If you really think you can do it alone, go ahead and try."

### 153. 〜ものの — "although"

The set pattern「名詞」というものの is common. 免許はとったものの、車が買えない "Although I got my
license, I can't afford a car."

### 154. 〜わりに(は) — "considering; for" (an unexpected result given A)

あのレストランの料理は、値段のわりにおいしい "Considering the price, that restaurant's food is
tasty."

### 155. 〜(よ)うではないか／〜(よ)うじゃないか — "let's ~; shall we ~" (strong volitional suggestion)

Flagged as a strong expression, suited to rhetorical/public-speech contexts. 自然保護の運動を広めよ
うではないか "Let us spread the movement for nature conservation."

### 156. 〜得る／〜得ない — "to be (un)able to do" (formal/literary)

Conjugates うる／えない／えます／えて／えた. こんな低い山で遭難することはあり得ないと思う "I don't
think it's possible to get stranded on such a low mountain."

### 157. 〜かねない — "capable of; in danger of" (doing something bad)

Used to describe worrying capability or to caution someone. あんなにスピードを出したら、事故を起こし
かねない "Driving that fast, you could well cause an accident."

### 158. 〜かねる — "difficult to do (easily)" (often in polite refusals)

そんな多額な寄付には応じかねます "I'm afraid I cannot agree to such a large donation."

### 159. 〜ことか — exclamatory emphasis

あなたの返事をどんなに待っていたことか "How I waited for your reply!"

### 160. 〜ことだ — "should (do)"

大学に入りたければ、一生懸命勉強することだ "If you want to get into university, you should study
hard."

### 161. 〜ざるを得ない — "can't avoid doing; have no choice but to" (formal)

原料がどんどん値上がりしているのですから、製品も値上げせざるを得ません "Since raw material prices
keep rising, we have no choice but to raise product prices too."

### 162. 〜次第だ／〜次第で(は) — two sub-senses (distinct from point 25's "as soon as" 次第)

(A) explaining how something came about, working like の／ん — このたび日本政府の招きにより、親善大
使として日本に来た次第です "This is how it came about that I came to Japan as a goodwill ambassador
at the invitation of the Japanese government"; (B) "depend on; according to" — 考え方次第で幸せに
も不幸せにもなる "Whether one is happy or unhappy depends on one's way of thinking."

### 163. 〜っこない — "impossible; never; can't possibly" (conversational)

宝くじなんて当たりっこないよ "There's no way I'll ever win the lottery."

### 164. 〜というものだ — stating the speaker's own thoughts/impressions

若者が高価な車を買うのはぜいたくというものだ "For young people to buy expensive cars — now that's
what I'd call extravagance."

### 165. 〜というものではない／〜というものでもない — "doesn't mean that; not necessarily (true)"

お金さえあれば幸せに暮らせるというものではない "It's not necessarily true that having money alone
means living happily."

### 166. 〜ないことはない／〜ないこともない — "it's not that ~ (isn't the case)" (qualified affirmation)

難しいが、やり方次第ではできないことはないだろう "It's difficult, but depending on the approach, it's
not that it can't be done."

### 167. 〜ないではいられない／〜ずにはいられない — "can't help but"

With a third-person subject, must pair with らしい／ようだ etc. あの映画のラストシーンは感動的で、涙
を流さずにはいられなかった "That movie's final scene was so moving I couldn't help but cry."

### 168. 〜に限る／〜に限り／〜に限って — three sub-senses

(A) "only; limited to" — 参加者は女性に限る "Participation is limited to women"; (B) "of all ~, only
this particular one" — あの人に限って、人をだますようなことはしない "Of all people, that person would
never do something like deceive someone"; (C) "be the best (option)" — 夏はビールに限る "In summer,
nothing beats beer."

### 169. 〜に限らず — "not limited to; not only"

この講座は学生に限らず、社会人も聴講できる "This course isn't limited to students — working adults
can also audit it."

### 170. 〜にほかならない — "be nothing but; be simply ~" (formal)

戦争というものは、大量殺人にほかならない "War is nothing other than mass murder."

### 171. 〜もの — "because" (reason/explanation marker)

Explicitly flagged in the book as used mainly by women and children; becomes もん in conversation —
a genuine demographic/register annotation worth preserving per the extraction spec's dialectal/
register rule, rather than silently generalizing it to "colloquial." 「一人で行ける。」「うん、大丈
夫、地図を持っているもの。」 "'Can you go alone?' 'Yeah, it's fine — I've got a map, you know.'"

### 172. 〜ものがある — "there is a (certain) quality/feeling of ~"

この絵には人を引きつけるものがある "There's something about this picture that draws people in."

### 173. 〜ようがない／〜ようもない — "no way to (do something)" — the book's final grammar point

ここまで壊れてしまった車は直しようがない "A car this badly broken is beyond repair (there's no way to
fix it)."
