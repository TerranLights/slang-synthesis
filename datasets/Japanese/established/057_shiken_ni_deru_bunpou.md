## Grammar points

Organized by Day, grouped under the book's own chapter headings as empirically identified. Numbers are the book's own continuous point numbers. Paraphrased from the Japanese explanation boxes; illustrative examples are my own short glosses, not verbatim reproduction.

### Chapter 1 — particles/connective words (Days 1–7, points 1–50)

**Day 1, p.20** — `〜に関して`/`〜に対して`/`〜について`/`〜をめぐって`/`〜によって` (points 1–8)
- **〜に関して** (1): "in relation to/concerning" — topic marker, roughly equivalent to 〜に関係して.
- **〜に対して** (2): two senses — (a) "toward/directed at" (動作の対象), e.g. 消費者団体は会社に対して抗議した "the consumer group protested [directed] at the company"; (b) marks an explicit **contrast** between two clauses, e.g. A校は経済学部が有名なのに対して、B校は政治学部が有名だ "A is known for economics, in contrast, B for politics."
- **〜について** (4): marks the *content* of speaking/thinking/studying/researching — narrower than に関して, which can also mean "in connection with."
- **〜をめぐって** (5): "centering on/with X as the point of contention" — used for disputes, debates, conflicting opinions circling a shared topic (e.g. 遺産をめぐる兄弟の争い "siblings' fight over the inheritance").
- **〜によって** (6–8): three distinct senses the book explicitly separates: (a) "varies depending on X" (文化は国によって違う); (b) means/method, ≈〜で (テレビによって情報を得る); (c) cause, ≈〜で (不注意によって事故が起きる).

**p.21** — `〜に` (9, passive-sentence agent marker: アメリカはコロンブスによって発見された), `〜によっては` (10, "depending on the particular case of X," narrower than plain によって — some but not all cases), `〜によると`/`〜によれば` (11, hearsay/reported-information source marker: ニュースによると「地震があったということだ」).

**p.29, points 48–50 (near Chapter 1's end)**
- **〜はおろか** `[1級]` (48): "not to mention X, not even Y" — stacks an extreme case on top of an already-notable one. あの監督の映画は国内はおろか海外でも高く評価されている "acclaimed not just domestically but even abroad."
- **〜というより** (49): "rather than say X, it's more accurate to say Y" — softens a correction/reframing.
- **〜たりとも…ない** `[1級]` (50): "not even a single [coin/moment/day]" — emphatic total negation attached to a minimal-unit noun (一円たりとも, 一瞬たりとも). cf. the related but distinct 何人たりとも "no person whatsoever [is permitted]."

### Chapter 2 (くらい/ほど/まで-family), Days 8–10, points ~51–89

**Day 8, p.38, points 68–73 — くらい/ほど discrimination.** The book explicitly cross-notes substitutability at every point — a genuinely useful comparative feature not found in the Kanzen Master series' per-point format:
- **くらい/ぐらい** (68): rough degree ("about X"). ほど is also usable, but くらい is more common; critically, **「同じぐらい」cannot be replaced with ほど** (this ぐらい is obligatory in equal-comparison constructions).
- **くらい (negligible-degree sense)** (69): "something as trivial/minor as X" — e.g. ひらがなぐらいすぐ書ける. **ほど cannot be substituted** in this sense.
- **くらい…だ / て…くらいだ** (70): "so much that—" (ほど also usable): 泣きたくなるくらい痛かった / 痛くて泣きたくなるくらいだった.
- **くらい…はない/いない** (71): "nothing/no one is more X than this" (ほど also usable): 地震くらいこわいものはない.
- **ほど** (72): plain approximate degree (くらい also usable): 400メートルほど行ったところに.
- **ほど…だ / て…ほどだ / ほどの** (73): "to the point that—" (くらい also usable): 涙が出るほどうれしかった.

**Day 10, p.42, points 84–89 — まで-family**
- **〜までだ** `[1級]` (84): "merely did X (nothing more, nothing special)" — often a deflecting/modest disclaimer (置けと言われたから置いたまでです).
- **〜までもない** `[1級]` (85): "no need to go as far as—" (わざわざ買うまでもない); fixed idiom cf. **言うまでもない** "goes without saying."
- **〜ないまでも** `[1級]` (86): "even granting that X isn't fully achieved, at least—" (優勝はできないまでも、せめて3位には入りたい).
- **〜ばそれまでだ** `[1級]` (87): "if X happens, that's simply the end of it" — fatalistic/resigned conclusion (死んでしまえばそれまでだ).
- **〜までして** (88): "going so far as to even do X" (unusual, often surprising effort): 徹夜までして料理を作っていた.
- **〜てまで** (89): same "going that far" sense as 88 but typically carries disapproval/surprise rather than admiration: 1時間待ってまでラーメンを食べようとする日本人に驚いた.

### Chapter 4 (わけ/こと/もの/ところ-family), Days 13–16

**Day 14, p.56, points 118–122 — わけ-family**
- **〜わけがない** (118): "there's no way that—" (water なしで1カ月も生きられるわけがない).
- **〜わけだ**, two senses (119–120): (a) logical-conclusion "so it follows that—," often as 「〜というわけだ」; (b) "that explains/makes sense of X" — conversational confirmation pattern, frequently following どうりで ("no wonder…").
- **〜わけではない** (121): partial denial — "it's not (exactly) that—" (特におもしろいわけではないが…).
- **〜わけにはいかない** (122): "can't very well do X" — a social/moral/circumstantial constraint, not a physical impossibility (明日はテストだから酒を飲むわけにはいかない).

**Day 16, p.60, points 136–144 — もの-family (dense: 9 points on one page)**
- **〜ものだ**, three senses (136–138): (a) exclamatory "really, isn't it—!" (感慨); (b) past habitual recollection, almost always as 「〜たものだ」(子供のころ、いたずらばかりして先生を困らせたものだ); (c) general social norm, often used to scold/instruct (約束をしたらきちんと守るものだ).
- **〜ものではない** (139): "one shouldn't—," violates common sense/etiquette (人の失敗を笑うものではない).
- **〜というものだ** (140): "that's simply—" stating the obvious/natural conclusion (お礼も言わないのは非常識というものだ).
- **〜というものではない** (141): "it's not necessarily the case that—" (安ければ売れる、というものではない).
- **〜ものだから** (142): "because of X (giving a reason, often as an excuse)" (電車が遅れたもんだから).
- **〜もの/〜もん** (143): colloquial sentence-final particle expressing a petulant/self-justifying "because…," almost always 「〜んだもの/んだもん」 — genuinely slang-adjacent register content (だって、起きられなかったんだもん。).
- **〜ものがある** (144): "there's a quality/element that makes one feel—" (人の心を打つものがある).

### Chapter 6 (から-family) and Chapter 7 (接続の言葉-その2), Days 20–~29

**Day 20, p.74, points 183–190** — a cluster of viewpoint/standpoint markers the book explicitly groups as near-synonyms:
- **から言うと/から言えば/から言って** (183), **からすると/からすれば** (184), **から見ると/から見れば/から見て** (185): all three families mean "viewed from the standpoint of—," functionally near-interchangeable across the book's own examples.
- **にしたら/にすれば** (186): standpoint marker specifically for *how someone feels* about something (負担になることもある, from the person's own emotional perspective), narrower than 183–185.
- **からして** (187): "just taking X as a representative/first example, it's already—" (食器からして高級品だ).
- **てからでないと…ない** (188): "not without first doing X" (両親と相談してからでないと決められない).
- **にしては** (189): "surprisingly X, for something that is supposedly—" (都心にしては静かだ).
- **わりに(は)** (190): "comparatively more/less than the expected standard for—" (安いわりにおいしい).

**Day 22, p.78, points 199–203**
- **からには/からは** and **以上/上は** (199–200): both mean "given that X is the case… (strong resolve/judgment follows)" — the book presents them as functionally equivalent pairs.
- **ともなると/ともなれば** `[1級]` (201): "once it reaches the specific/elevated state of X—" (上級ともなると、難しい漢字を習う).
- **にひきかえ** `[1級]` (202): "in stark/surprising contrast to—" (勉強家の兄にひきかえ、弟の私は…).
- **もさることながら** `[1級]` (203): "not only X — which goes without saying — but also Y" (映像の美しさもさることながら、ストーリーもなかなか感動的だ).

**Chapter 7, p.87, points 219–220**
- **といえば** (219): associative topic-shift — "speaking of X, that reminds me of Y" (「ビールといえば、最近また新製品が…」).
- **といったら…ない/否定的表現** (220): extreme-degree marker usually followed by a negative predicate — "words can't do justice to how—" (彼の練習のすごさといったら、とても真似ができないほどです); cross-references p.106's 〜といったらない.

**p.91, point 236** — **〜につき**: formal "because of/due to," characteristically used in public notices/signage addressed to many readers at once (本日は定休日につき、休ませていただきます; 「工事中につき、立ち入り禁止」). Same-page メモ box tabulates collocation sets for を問わず vs. にかかわらず (経験/天候/国籍/性別/学歴 take を問わず; 経験のあるなし/天気が良い悪い/どこの国の人であるか/男か女か/地位の高い低い/行く行かない phrasings take にかかわらず) — a genuinely useful structured usage-restriction note.

**p.95, points 249–251**
- **なり…なり** `[1級]` (249): "either X or Y (whichever)," presenting options (本を読むなりテレビを見るなりして待っていてください).
- **つ…つ** (250): fixed-form only — alternating repeated action idiom, limited to set compounds like 抜きつ抜かれつ ("neck-and-neck, overtaking each other") and 行きつ戻りつ ("back and forth").
- **ては** (251): "does X then Y, repeatedly, alternating" (書いては消し、書いては消し…).

### Chapter 8 (ざるを得ない-family), Days 29–31

**Day 29, p.100, points 252–257**
- **ざるを得ない** (252): "can't help but—, have no real choice" (責任者として出席せざるを得ない).
- **しかない** (253), **(より)ほかない/ほかしかたがない** (254): "nothing to do/no way but X," functionally near-synonymous pairs.
- **にすぎない** (255): "merely/nothing more than—" (単なるうわさにすぎない).
- **てしょうがない** (256), **てたまらない** (257): "unbearably—," near-synonymous intensifiers (暴走族が多いからうるさくてしょうがない / くやしくてたまらない).

**Day 31, p.104, points 266–269**
- **にほかならない** (266): "is truly nothing other than—" (遊び感覚の万引きは犯罪にほかならない).
- **でなくてなんだろう** (267): rhetorical "if this isn't X, then what is?" (これが汚職でなくてなんだろう).
- **ないではいられない/ずにはいられない** (268): "can't help but—" (感動せずにはいられないだろう).
- **ずにはおかない/ないではおかない** `[1級]` (269): "is bound to—, is sure to cause/produce X" — stronger than 268, implies an inevitable effect on others (原爆ドームは見る人に戦争の悲惨さを訴えずにはおかない).

### Chapter 9 — compound-forming suffixes (複合語として使われる言葉)

**p.113, points 286–287**
- **っぽい** (286): "seems/tends toward X, has an X-ish quality" — highly productive, colloquial-leaning suffix (子供っぽい, 飽きっぽい性格, 白っぽい服装). Notable as one of the most slang-relevant/productive items sampled in this whole source.
- **めく** `[1級]` (287): "takes on the character/air of X" — productive but restricted to a known small set (春めく "feels like spring," 皮肉めいた言い方 "sarcastic-sounding").
- **Memo box (same page)** — explicit collocation restrictions for near-synonymous suffixes: 〜得る/〜得ない attaches to cognition/judgment verbs (考える・知る・予想する・予測する・解決する・判断する・理解する・感知する); 〜がたい expresses "can't bring oneself to—" but is restricted to volitional/attitude verbs (捨てがたい ✓, but the book explicitly marks ✗熱くて食べがたい and ✗重くて持ちがたい as ungrammatical); 〜にくい means "difficult but not impossible" and *does* cover physical-constraint cases (入りにくい、食べにくい — exactly the cases がたい rejects); 〜めく is listed as a closed/fixed set: 春めく・皮肉めく・冗談めく.

**p.117, points 303–304**
- **まみれ** `[1級]` (303): "covered all over in X" — restricted to substances that soil/cover unpleasantly (汗まみれ, 血まみれ, 泥まみれ).
- **ずくめ** `[1級]` (304): "nothing but X, entirely X" — restricted to abstract/categorical totalities (黒ずくめ "all in black," いいことずくめ "nothing but good news," 規則ずくめ "nothing but rules").

### Chapter 10 (その他の基本文法 cluster), Day 37

**p.122, points 312–318**
- **おそれがある** (312): formal "there is a risk/concern that—," common in weather/disaster warnings (大雨のおそれがあります).
- **きらいがある** `[1級]` (313): "tends to have the (undesirable) tendency to—" (他人を批判するきらいがある).
- **代わりに** (314): "instead of—/in exchange for—" (私の代わりに部下を行かせます).
- **くせに** (315): "even though—," carries a nuance of blame/contempt toward the subject (真相を知っているくせに話そうとしない).
- **なりに** `[1級]` (316): "within the scope/level appropriate to X" (子供は子供なりに考えている). **Book's own cf. note:** don't confuse with the homonymous but unrelated 道なりに "following the curve of the road."
- **あまり** (317): "to the extent of excessive—" (緊張のあまり何も食べられなかった).
- **ぬきで/ぬきにして** (318): "without X, omitting X" (卵ぬきで作ってあります; 仕事の話はぬきにして).

### Chapter 11 (を…として/にして-family)

**p.129, point 335** — **を契機に/にして/として**: "taking X as the (new) opportunity/turning point for—" (定年を契機に都会を離れた; テロ事件を契機として空港のチェックが厳しくなった).

**p.131, points 342–344** `[1級]` tier
- **極まる/極まりない** (342): "this is as X as it gets" — attaches to na-adjectives expressing a negative extreme (失礼極まる, 危険極まりない, 退屈極まりない).
- **の至り/の極み** (343): fixed honorific-register superlatives — 光栄の至り "the height of honor," 感激の極み "utmost emotion," 恐縮の至り, ぜいたくの極み. Formal/written register, often in speeches or formal letters.
- **だに** (344): "even merely—" (想像するだに恐ろしい "frightening even to imagine"; 予想だにしなかった "didn't even expect").

**p.141 — "応用問題" (applied-form items):** ten items demonstrating that a familiar grammar point can appear on the real exam in a non-standard surface form, each cross-referenced back to its standard-form page: ものですか(=ものか); とまでは言わないが(=とは言わないまでも); というと (here used in a different sense than the standard といえば/というと topic-shift pattern — simple "every time X, Y" association); を…とする(=を…として); おもしろいほど〜 (idiomatic intensifier, "to a surprising/delightful degree," 自分の思いどおりになる様子); ようで…(="seems like X but actually isn't," 一見〜ようだがほんとうはそうではない); ようによっては(="depending on how one does it," =やりかたによっては); ようとも(=〜ようと, concessive "even if"); によらず(=にかかわらず／いかんによらず); に足りないでしょう (negated form of 〜に足る, "not worth—").
