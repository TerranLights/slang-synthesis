## Grammar points

### Near-synonyms built on a shared kanji component — Lesson 10 §①–②
Two kanji with overlapping meaning frequently combine with a common second kanji to form a pair or
triple of true near-synonyms where the shared-component kanji contributes a consistent semantic
core: 値-価 (値段/価格, everyday vs. formal-register "price"), 企-計 (企画/計画), 完-終 (完了/終了),
基-根 (基本/根本), 育-養 (育成/養成), 到-達 (到着/到達), and a three-way set 助-援-救 (救助/援助/救援).
A larger catalog of shared-kanji synonym sets is given for vocabulary built around a single anchor
kanji: 意(意思,意志,意図), 延(延期,延長,遅延), 革(変革,改革,革命), 慣(習慣,慣例,慣習), 基(基本,基礎),
区(区分,区別), 決(決意,決心,決定), 講(講演,講義,講習), 順(順番,順序,順位), 対(対照,対称,対比— the
first two of which are also homophones), 点(欠点,弱点,難点), 展(進展,展開), 討(討論,討議,検討),
発(発展,発達,開発), 分(分割,分離,分解), 弁(弁護,弁解,弁明), 了(終了,完了,修了 — first and third
homophonous), 論(討論,議論,論争).

### Shared-anchor-kanji suffix families applied to occupation/place/negation vocabulary — Lesson 10 §③
Extending the Lesson 6 suffix-family concept specifically to near-synonym pairs: money-suffixes
料-費-代-賃 (授業料/学費, 賃貸料/住居費, 部屋代/家賃); occupation-suffixes 者-師 (医者/医師, everyday
vs. formal-title register), 手-士 (運転手/運転士), 員-人 (公務員/役人, neutral vs. mildly pejorative
register), 屋-店-業 (本屋/書店/出版業); place-suffixes 室-所 (事務室/事務所), 室-院 (美容室/美容院),
室-館 (図書室/図書館); negation-prefixes 非-無 (非常識/無教養), 不-未 (不確実/未確認).

### Near-synonyms distinguished by their required subject/object type, not meaning alone — Lesson 10 §④–⑤
The book's central claim for this lesson: apparent synonyms are frequently NOT freely
interchangeable, because each one restricts what kind of subject or object it can take. 完了する
(a task/duty ends), 終了する (a match/time period ends), 修了する (an educational course is
completed), and 終結する (a war/incident concludes) are near-identical in gloss but differ by what
noun can fill the subject slot. The same logic applies to 発達する/進歩する/発展する (science+
technology vs. general social progress vs. society-specific expansion), 企画する/計画する (an
organization's novel project vs. any individual/organization's plan), and 改革する/変革する/改良する
(fixing flaws in a system vs. wholesale societal/systemic change vs. improving a product's quality).
A second group is distinguished instead by REQUIRED PARTICLES: 達成する(〜を, a target), 成功する
(〜に/が, an endeavor), 到達する(〜に, a numeric goal), 到着する(〜へ/に, a place); 区別する(AとBを/
AをBと) vs. 分別する(same pattern, different domain) vs. 区分する(AをBとCに, 3-way division).

### Fine-grained definitional contrasts for superficially-identical near-synonyms — Lesson 10 §⑥–⑦
Where particles don't disambiguate, the book falls back to explicit prose definitions distinguishing
near-synonyms: 仕度する(casual)/用意する(general)/準備する(more formal, e.g. for insufficient
preparation) all mean "to prepare"; 様子(casual)/状況/状態 (more formal) all mean "a situation/
condition." For a harder-to-disambiguate set, the book gives full contrastive prose definitions
rather than just register labels: 欠点("a lacking/bad point")/弱点("a point weak under pressure")/
短所("a point inferior in quality/performance")/難点("a point that causes problems"); 基本("the
unchanging core")/基礎("the initial stage")/根本("the fundamental root cause of something");
方向("direction of travel from a point")/方角("a compass direction from a reference point")/
方面("a general direction/region, or a field of endeavor"); 改革("fixing the bad parts of a system,"
VN)/変革("greatly changing a society/system," VN)/革命("a sudden root-level drastic change," N
only — no suru-verb form given); 議論("stating opinions and debating," VN)/論議("debating a narrow,
specific question," VN)/討論("formally debating at an organized meeting/forum," VN)/論争("disputing
with opposing claims," VN).

### Same-sound, near-synonym pairs (同音の類義語) — Lesson 10 §⑧
A final, especially tricky category combines BOTH same-pronunciation AND overlapping-meaning,
making these pairs doubly hard to distinguish (cross-referenced back to Lesson 5's homophone
section): いし (意思"intention, cognitive"/意志"will, volitional"/遺志"last wishes, after death");
しゅうりょう (終了"an event/period ends, VN"/修了"a course is completed, VN"); たいしょう (対照
"to compare, VN"/対象"an object/target, N only"/対称"symmetry, N only"); ほしょう (保証"to guarantee
quality, VN"/保障"to protect/secure from disaster, VN, e.g. national security"/補償"to compensate
for a loss, VN").
