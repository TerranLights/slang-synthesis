## Grammar points

### 1. ~方 (かた)
Verb stem (ます-form minus ます) + かた forms a compound noun meaning "the way of.../how to...":
作文の書き方「how to write a composition」, 走り方「one's style of running」. (p. 74)

### 2. ~前に "before..." & ~から "after..."
⟨Sentence 1 (verb plain present)⟩前に、⟨Sentence 2⟩ = "Before S1, S2"; ⟨Sentence 1 (verb-て)⟩から、
⟨Sentence 2⟩ = "After S1, S2". The -て form precedes から; the plain present form precedes 前に —
and Sentence 1's tense conforms to Sentence 2's tense, not its own apparent time reference. (p. 74)

### 3. ~なくちゃ — "must; have to..."
~なくてはいけない expresses obligation; なくちゃ is its familiar-speech contraction of なくては
(which can itself shorten further to なくちゃいけない): もっと野菜を食べなくちゃ。「Gotta eat more
vegetables.」(p. 75)

### 4. Verb-ば
V-ばいいじゃないですか ("Why don't you...?") shortens to bare V-ば in familiar speech: 友達に頼めば。
「Why don't you ask your friend?」(p. 75)

### 5. Verb-たり、Verb-たりする — "do such things as... and..."
The たり form adds り to the plain past form (食べた→食べたり). Used to pick out representative actions
among others, often implying "and so on": 日曜日には手紙を書いたり、本を読んだりします。「On Sundays I
write letters, read books, (and so on).」(p. 75–76)

### 6. Verb-stem-なさい
A polite imperative — used to convey commands without being rough, but not used by students toward
teachers or children toward parents (asymmetric politeness constraint): 早く起きなさい。「Get up
early.」(p. 76)

### 7. Verb-て ある
A transitive verb + てある indicates a thing is in a certain resulting state because of someone's
prior action (distinct from intransitive +ている which just states a state): 本に名前を書きました。→
本に名前が書いてあります。「A name is written in the book.」(p. 76–77)

### 8. Sentence (plain form) ことになっている
Literally "It has been decided that...". Commonly expresses rules/regulations, and can also express
a fixed schedule: ドライバーはみんなシートベルトを締めなくてはいけないことになっています。「All drivers
have to wear seatbelts (it's the law).」(p. 77)

### 9. -たら — "if..., when..."
Adds ら to the plain past form of verbs, adjectives, and the copula (書いた→書いたら、きれいだった→きれい
だったら). Unlike なら, -たら implies strict temporal sequence: in ⟨S1たら⟩、⟨S2⟩, S1's event must
precede S2's. (p. 77)
