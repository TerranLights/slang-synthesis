## Grammar points

### 47 〜が最後
Meaning: "once ~ happens, that's it / if ~ happens, there's no going back" — describes an action that, once taken, leads irreversibly to a fixed continuing state. Connects to `[verb-た form] + が最後`. Example (p.78): うちの息子は寝入ったが最後、雷が鳴ろうが地震が起ころうが、絶対に目を覚まさない — "Once my son falls asleep, he absolutely will not wake up, whether there's thunder or an earthquake." Related colloquial form noted: 〜たら最後.

### 48 〜かたがた
Meaning: "while also ~ / for the combined purpose of ~" — a formal expression for one act serving two purposes. Connects to `[noun] + かたがた`. Example (p.78): 無事に卒業できたので、恩師に報告かたがた、手紙を書いた — "Since I graduated safely, I wrote a letter both to report this and (implicitly) to thank my mentor." Cross-referenced to point 7's 〜がてら as a related, less formal expression.

### 51 〜ごとき／〜ごとく
Meaning: two senses. (A) "like ~ / as if ~" (literary simile), `[verb-dict/た form (+が); noun-の] + ごとき` — example: その少年は飛ぶがごとき勢いで、駆け去った, "the boy dashed off with a speed as if flying." (B) "~ or the like / mere ~" with a dismissive/negative connotation — used when framing something as unworthy of serious consideration.

### 53 〜ずくめ
Meaning: "nothing but ~ / all ~" — expresses that the whole of something is in one uniform state. Connects to `[noun] + ずくめ`. Example (p.86): 試験には合格するし、恋人はできるし、いいことずくめですね — "I passed the exam, I got a girlfriend/boyfriend — nothing but good things lately." Also used for clothing: 黒ずくめの男 — "a man dressed all in black."

### 54 〜ずにはおかない
Meaning: "will inevitably ~ / won't let things rest without ~ing" — expresses either the speaker's firm intention that something will definitely be done, or a natural/inevitable state that will certainly occur. Connects to `[verb-ない form] + ずにはおかない` (する becomes せずにはおかない). Example (p.86): そんなことをしてみろ。痛い目にあわせずにはおかないぞ — "Just try that. I'll absolutely make you regret it."

### 59 〜ともなく／〜ともなしに
Meaning: "without any particular intention of ~ing / absent-mindedly ~ing" — describes doing something without clear conscious awareness or purpose. Connects to `[verb-dictionary form] + ともなく`. Example (p.94): 見るともなくぼんやり外を見ていたら、不意に大きなカラスが飛んできた — "I was gazing outside without really meaning to look at anything, when suddenly a large crow flew by." Note: the same verb typically appears both before and after this pattern.

### 60 〜ともなると／〜ともなれば
Meaning: "once it becomes ~ / when it reaches the point of ~" — marks a threshold condition whose reaching changes the situation significantly. Connects to `[verb-dictionary form / noun] + ともなると`. Example (p.94): 12月ともなると、街にはジングルベルのメロディーがあふれる — "Once December arrives, the streets are full of 'Jingle Bells' melodies."

### 65 〜なしに(は)
Meaning: "without ~ / without doing ~" — states that something is done, or cannot be done, in the absence of a given prerequisite. Connects to `[noun] + なしに`. Example (p.102): きちんとした手続きなしには入学することはできない — "One cannot be admitted without going through the proper procedures."

### 66 〜ならでは
Meaning: "uniquely possible because of ~ / something only ~ could do" — usually describing a positive, distinguishing quality. Connects to `[noun] + ならでは`. In the form "AならではBない" it means "if not for A, B would not be possible." Example (p.102): この味はおふくろの手作りならでは出せない味だ — "This flavor is one only my mother's homemade cooking could produce."

### 70 〜に足る
Meaning: "worthy of ~ / sufficient to ~" — usually used with verbs of trust, praise, or belief. Connects to `[verb-dictionary form / noun] + に足る`. Example (p.105): あの人は信頼するに足る人物です — "That person is worthy of trust." A companion negative form is given in a 参考/reference box: 〜に足らない／〜に足りない, "not worth ~ / not enough to ~" — e.g. 宇宙の大きさを思うと、今の私の悩みなんて取るに足らないものだ.

### 71 〜にひきかえ
Meaning: "in contrast to ~ / contrary to ~" — sets up an explicit contrast between two situations. Connects to `[verb/i-adj/na-adj noun-modifying form + の; noun] + にひきかえ` (noun/na-adj can also take "~であるの"). Example (p.111): 先月は食料品の売上げが著しく伸びたのにひきかえ、衣料品の売上げが落ち込んだ — "In contrast to last month's marked rise in food sales, clothing sales dropped."

### 72 〜にもまして
Meaning: "even more than ~ / surpassing ~" — in the pattern "AにもましてB," means "A is already the case, but B is even more so." Connects to `[verb/i-adj/na-adj noun-modifying form + の; noun] + にもまして`.

### 77 〜べく
Meaning: "in order to ~ / with the intention of ~" — states a purpose, in a somewhat formal register. Connects to `[verb-dictionary form] + べく` (するべく often becomes すべく). Example (p.119): 恩師に会うべく、久しぶりに母校を訪れた — "In order to see my old teacher, I visited my alma mater for the first time in a long while."

### 78 〜まじき
Meaning: "should not ~ / unbecoming to ~" — expresses that an action is naturally/morally inappropriate, often for a given role or status. Connects to `[verb-dictionary form] + まじき` (するまじき can become すまじき), and is always followed by a noun. Example (p.119): 弱い者をいじめるなんて、許すまじきことです — "Bullying the weak is something that must never be forgiven."

### 83 〜を禁じ得ない
Meaning: "cannot suppress ~ / cannot help but feel ~" — almost always used with emotion-nouns. Connects to `[noun] + を禁じ得ない`. Example (p.127): 彼女の身の上話を聞いて、涙を禁じ得なかった — "Hearing her life story, I couldn't hold back my tears."

### 84 〜をもって
Meaning: two senses given. (A) "by means of ~ / with ~" (method/means), e.g. 彼は人一倍の努力をもって優勝カップを手にすることができた — "Through more effort than anyone else, he managed to win the championship cup." (B) "at/as of ~" (marking a start, end, or boundary point — e.g. announcing the opening or closing of an event), both taking `[noun] + をもって`.
