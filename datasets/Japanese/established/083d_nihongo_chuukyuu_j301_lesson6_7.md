# Japanese — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Nihongo Chuukyuu J301 — Lessons 6–7

**Source:** Toki Satoshi et al., *日本語中級J301 — 基礎から中級へ* (English edition), 3A Network, 1995/2004. See `083_nihongo_chuukyuu_j301.md` for full source citation, methodology notes, and all sampled grammar points (Lesson 7's grammar points are written up there in full).

**Coverage note:** Lesson 6 (お化けと幽霊 — "Obake and Yuurei: Two Kinds of Japanese Ghosts") is a folklore-comparison reading passage distinguishing two categories of Japanese ghost/monster; Lesson 7 (あのときはどうも — "Thanks for Back Then") is about the giri/ninjō (義理人情) social-obligation custom of belatedly thanking someone, and carries unusually rich culturally-specific vocabulary (on/giri/honne-tatemae-adjacent concepts). No marginalia found on these pages.
