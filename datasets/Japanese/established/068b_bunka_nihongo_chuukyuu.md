## Grammar points

*(Full coverage of all 8 lessons' numbered 文型・表現 list, from the textbook's own "内容一覧"
content-overview pages pp.6–9 — paraphrased, with the book's own short example sentence kept
illustrative rather than reproduced at length. Each lesson also has a short list of 接続詞/副詞
(conjunctions/adverbs) taught separately from the main patterns, included below each lesson.)*

### Lesson 1 — ニュースと新聞 (News and newspapers)
1. **〜だけあって** (precisely because, true to expectations): "さすがにホテルで仕事をしているだけ
   あって、終始笑顔が絶えなかった" — a hotel worker's smile never faltering, befitting their job.
2. **〜わけにはいかない** (can't simply do X, given the circumstances): "家族の一員として過ごすのだから、
   遊んでばかりいるわけにはいかない" — as a family member [in a homestay], one can't just play all
   the time.
3. **〜からといって** (just because X, doesn't mean Y): "外国人だからといって甘えていないで、当然
   手伝いもしよう" — don't lean on being a foreigner as an excuse; help out as a matter of course.
4. **もちろん〜も, 〜など日常のささいなことも** (listing escalation): everyday trivial matters,
   bath habits, wake-up times, down to post-meal cleanup, should all be asked about proactively.
   Adverbs taught: さすが(に), 終始, すっと.

### Lesson 2 — 世界旅行 (World travel)
1. **〜てきました (resultative/experiential with motion)**: "今日はグランドキャニオンに行ってきまし
   た" — went to and came back from the Grand Canyon.
2. **360度見渡す限り** (as far as the eye can see, 360 degrees): describing an immense vista.
3. **〜にわたって** (spanning/extending across): a canyon stretching 350km.
4. **ほど〜 / おかげで**: Switzerland described as beautiful; "thanks to Noriko" finding an
   off-the-beaten-path spot not in guidebooks.
5. Grammar also taught: 助数詞 (counters) as a dedicated コラム.
6. **〜によって〜できた**: most of this region's terrain was formed by volcanic activity.
7. **〜をはじめ**: Aso and other active volcanoes still emitting smoke, Aso among them.
8. **〜もいれば〜もいる / 〜というふうに**: some seek cool in summer, others seek skiing in
   winter — visitors come year-round, in this kind of varied pattern.
   Adverbs: わざわざ, ようやく.

### Lesson 3 — 異文化間コミュニケーション (Cross-cultural communication)
1. **〜という** (definitional "to call X Y"): defining what a stereotype is.
2. **〜と言い切る** (to assert/declare flatly): calling someone "a workaholic" outright, as an
   example of stereotyping.
3. **〜に固執した結果** (as a result of clinging to X): clinging to a stereotype, disregarding
   individuality, risks something.
4. **〜どころか、かえって〜**: far from X, it actually [backfires into] Y — understanding doesn't
   progress, misunderstanding/gaps arise instead.
5. **〜とはいうものの**: although it's said that times have changed, people still view things
   through their own "prism."
6. **〜を通して**: viewing Japan through America's own particular lens/prism.
7. **いかに〜かについて**: discussing just how important it is to eat everything offered.
8. **よく言ったものだ**: "well said" — a rabbit-hutch-sized house, aptly described.
9. **〜からこそ**: precisely because Japan has four distinct seasons [does X follow].
10. **〜なくては何もわからない**: without even trying it, nothing can be understood.
    Adverbs: たまたま, 知らず知らず, 依然として, さほど〜ない, たいてい, きまって, やっと. Also a
    dedicated コラム on カタカナ言葉 (katakana/loanword vocabulary) and on ことわざ (proverbs).

### Lesson 4 — スポーツと余暇 (Sports and leisure)
1. **〜ものもあれば〜ものもある**: some become world champions by honing their skill, others by
   physical/mental training — variation among paths to the same goal.
2. **〜てはじめて**: only after overcoming fear does the path to victory open up.
3. **〜かねない**: one misstep could easily turn into a serious accident.
4. **〜から〜にかけて**: a jump's approach descending from the 3rd basement floor of a Tokyo
   building, reaching 90km/h.
5–6. **〜にもかかわらず**: despite a painful period, the athlete's results right after returning
   to competition were their best ever (repeated with a variant clause order for contrast).
7. **〜のに対し**: women's top answer is "time with friends," contrasted with men's "contact with
   nature."
8–9. **〜と比べると / 強まっている**: compared to a previous survey, the "nature" response has
   strengthened; also 〜である体 (the plain/formal である writing style) taught as a コラム.
   Conjunctions/adverbs: 次いで, これに対し, すでに, 一応.

### Lesson 5 — 日本の歴史 (Japanese history)
1. **〜に代わって**: from the 12th century, samurai began holding political power in place of
   the nobility.
2. **〜にまで**: there was no longer any need to send envoys across the dangerous crossing to the
   continent.
3. **すべて〜のせいだと**: people of the time believed illness and disasters were entirely the
   fault of evil spirits.
4. **というのは〜からである**: explaining why a decoded diagram greatly surprised him — because it
   differed clearly from Chinese medical texts.
5. **〜のあまり**: out of sheer shock, the two of them kept rereading their own words of
   self-reproach.
6. **〜だけに**: precisely because it was an era without dictionaries, [the achievement] was not
   an easy one.
7. **〜にすぎないが**: though it was merely a single word's meaning [understood], their joy was
   immense.
   Conjunctions/adverbs: その結果, 徐々に, より, たびたび, ごく, さっぱり〜ない, 大いに, 明らかに,
   まったく, 早速, 第一. Also a コラム on 「手」のつく慣用句 (idioms built on 手 "hand").

### Lesson 6 — 言葉と文化 (Language and culture)
1. **〜に関する**: Eskimo languages have countless unique terms relating to ice and snow.
2. **〜を通じて〜ことはない**: in regions where it almost never rains year-round, [snow-related
   vocabulary] can hardly be imagined.
3. **〜とは限らない**: what influences language is not limited to the natural environment.
4. **〜にしたがって**: in Mongolia, names for horses change as they grow/mature.
5. **〜において**: in Japan, both wild and domesticated horses are called by the single word 馬.
   Also a コラム on 擬音語・擬態語 (onomatopoeia/mimetic vocabulary).
6. **〜ところから**: a daruma doll is named for the way it rights itself after falling — the
   etymology lesson for L6's second text.
7. **〜は〜と共に**: daruma, together with the phrase "七転び八起き" (fall seven times, rise eight),
   spread nationwide.
8. **〜際に / 〜なら**: when making a wish, one paints in one eye [of the daruma]; if it comes
   true, the other is filled in.
   Conjunctions/adverbs: 同様に, 微妙に, 如実に, どうか.

### Lesson 7 — 日本人と食生活 (Japanese people and diet)
1. **〜に至るまで**: eel has been valued as a nutritious food continuously from the Nara period
   up to the present.
2. **〜(よ)うとして〜な**: don't let the eel [you're trying to catch] slip away in the river — a
   proverb-flavored warning.
3. **〜ずにはいられない**: lured by the smell of grilling eel and a "doyō no ushi no hi" sign, one
   can't help but eat. Also a コラム on 短歌 (classical 31-syllable poetry) tied to this text.
4. **〜ざるを得ない**: it's genuinely difficult to find food free of pesticides/additives — one is
   forced to admit as much.
5. **〜ことに**: alarmingly, there's a real possibility of effects reaching even genetic material.
6. **〜がちである**: consumers tend to judge food mostly by price or appearance.
   Conjunctions/adverbs: さて, すなわち, にもかかわらず, もしくは, さっと, まさに, 非常に, いかにも,
   端的に.

### Lesson 8 — 生活と環境 (Life and environment)
1. **〜つつあります**: the earth's sky and greenery are currently in the process of losing their
   brightness.
2. **〜と同時に**: as that balance collapses, worldwide-scale problems are also being born.
3. **〜ばかりに**: having let the harmony with nature slip from mind, we have let the environment
   worsen as a result.
4. **〜べきなのか**: how should such problems be improved going forward — a rhetorical policy
   question.
5. **〜ということは〜ということだ**: the environmental crisis is a result of our own lives and
   policy decisions — meaning the power to contain the crisis also lies within ourselves.
6. **〜っぱなしにしない**: don't leave the TV or lights on continuously — switch them off.
7. **〜で済む**: electricity can be saved so that a third of the energy use is all that's
   needed.
8. **〜うえでも有効です**: recycling is also effective toward reducing the amount of trash.
   Also a コラム on 接頭語・接尾語 (prefixes/suffixes). Conjunctions/adverbs: 逆に, あるいは, なかで
   も, もはや, 相当.
