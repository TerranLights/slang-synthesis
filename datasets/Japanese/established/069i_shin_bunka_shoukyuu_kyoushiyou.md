## Grammar points

### Lesson 31 (東京発鹿児島行き623便 — "Flight 623, Tokyo to Kagoshima"), pp. 122–130
Goals: use conditional なら/たら to request or give advice; describe travel/airport procedures.
1. **〜なら (advice-giving conditional, verb-antecedent)** — extends the noun-antecedent なら from L22 to verb antecedents. Two illustrated discourse patterns: (1) the speaker freely hypothesizes several possible paths toward the listener's stated goal and gives advice for each (安く確実に行くならモノレールですが、楽に座って行くならタクシーのほうがいいです); (2) the listener's wish is already clear/specific, and the speaker's なら-clause directly echoes it before giving a single piece of advice. Teaching note: advice given this way should end in よ to mark it as a recommendation, not a neutral statement.
2. **〜ことにします／〜ことにしました (deciding on a course of action)** — present-tense ことにします marks a decision made on the spot/in the moment; past-tense ことにしました marks a previously-made decision being reported.
3. **〜たら (conditional, "once X happens")** — the book provides the teacher a four-way taxonomy of たら's overall range of uses (1: natural/expected sequential realization, "once X happens, Y follows as a matter of course" — the sense actually drilled in this lesson; 2: hypothetical future assumption; 3: counterfactual, contrary-to-fact; 4: direct と/ば substitutability), explicitly restricting this lesson's own drilling to sense 1 only, with senses 2–4 flagged as out of scope for this book (sense 4 cross-referenced to the already-taught と/ば patterns from L12/L22, with たら noted as the more colloquial/spoken-register option among the three near-equivalent conditionals). A separate, distinct "surprising discovery upon arrival" sense of たら (駅に着いたら、もう人がおおぜいいました) is explicitly deferred to L32 文型5, flagged here only as existing, not drilled.
4. **〜ことになっています (stated rule/institutional convention)** — see Vocabulary table for the full register nuance (softer than なくてはいけません/てはいけません); book explicitly says students are unlikely to need to produce this construction often, so only comprehension is targeted, not active drilling.
5. **〜ようです／〜みたいです (simile, "as if/like")** — restricted in this lesson to the sentence-final predicate-complete form only (まるで、おもちゃのようです/みたいです), explicitly marked for emotionally engaged/evocative description (感動を…豊かに表現しようとする言い方); the verb/adjective-modifying connective form (ロボットがまるで人間のように歩いています) is deferred to L33.
6. **い形容詞/な形容詞+する (causative state-change by a volitional agent)** — companion pattern to the already-taught い形容詞/な形容詞+なる (L12, spontaneous/non-agentive change): 〜する marks a person deliberately acting on an object to bring about a state (音を大きくする), directly paralleled to the L26 自動詞/他動詞 pairing logic (水が止まる ↔ 水を止める mirrors 大きくなる ↔ 大きくする) — an explicit, deliberate cross-reference connecting two separately-taught grammatical subsystems (adjectival state-change and verbal transitivity) as expressions of the same underlying agentive/non-agentive contrast.
7. **見える／聞こえる (spontaneous perception verbs)** — treated as lexically independent from 見る/聞く, denoting an object simply falling within the range of unmediated sight/hearing, explicitly contrasted with the POTENTIAL forms of 見る/聞く (見られる/聞ける), which instead denote that the conditions exist for someone to see/hear something IF they intend/try to (worked minimal pair: 大きい飛行機に乗ると、中で映画が見られます — availability of the option to watch, contingent on intent — vs. simply 見えます, unmediated/automatic visibility). This is a clean lexicalized volitional-vs-spontaneous-perception contrast, not a derivational rule.
