## Grammar points

### 和語動詞 (native-Japanese verbs) vs. 漢語動詞 (Sino-Japanese verbs) — Lesson 3 §①
When a native Japanese verb and one or more Sino-Japanese verbs express related meanings, the
native verb typically covers a *wider* semantic range, which Sino-Japanese vocabulary then splits
into fine, mutually-exclusive distinctions with specific argument-structure/collocation
requirements — e.g. わける ("to divide," generic) splits into 分割する (land/territory/payments),
分担する (work/tasks/expenses), 分類する (classification systems), 分配する (money/profit
distribution), 分解する (disassembling machines/compounds), 分断する (cutting off an enemy army or
road). The same pattern recurs for なおす→修理する/治療する/改正する/修正する/訂正する, やめる→
中止する/停止する, のばす→延期する/延長する, かわる/かえる→変動する/変化する/変色する/変更する,
たすける→救助する/救出する/救援する/援助する/支援する/応援する, つくる→製造する/制作する/作成する
/創作する/創造する, and そだてる→保育する/養育する/教育する/養成する. The book also notes a
transitive/intransitive split layered on top of this: 予定が変わる (intrans.) = 予定が変更になる,
予定を変える (trans.) = 予定を変更する; 病気が治る = 病気が回復する/治癒する, 病気を治す = 病気を
治療する.

### Shortening/omission patterns in Sino-Japanese headline style — Lesson 3 §③
Japanese newspaper headlines compress Sino-Japanese vocabulary in three distinct ways: (1) an
ordinary verb phrase shortens to its two-kanji noun form (色が変わる→変色; 時期を延ばす→延期; 日本を
訪れる→訪日; 戦いに参加する→参戦); (2) a long descriptive phrase compresses into a single compound
word (子どもを育てるのにかかるお金→養育費; 難民を助けるためのセンター→難民救援センター; 自動車を
作るための工場→自動車製造工場; 日本語の教師を育てるための講座→日本語教師養成講座); (3) particles
and する can be omitted entirely in headline style (新大陸発見 = 新大陸を発見した; 中国訪問決定 =
中国を訪問することが/を決定した).

### Internal structure of Sino-Japanese (Kanji) verbs — Lesson 3 §④–⑤
出-prefixed verbs illustrate four internal argument structures: V+V (出発=出る+発つ, "to do both V1
and V2"), V+N(place)ヲ meaning "to leave a place" (出港=港を出る), V+N(place)ニ meaning "to arrive
at/appear at a place" (出場=場に出る), and Vt+N(object)ヲ meaning "to put something out" (出題=
(問)題を出す). Longer (4+ kanji) compounds can be decomposed into two or three such 2-kanji units
and read back out as a plain-language paraphrase, e.g. 出発延期→出発/延期→「出発の時期を延ばすこと」;
人質救出不可能→人質/救出/不可能→「人質を助け出すことができない」. (Cross-referenced to BK L.20,
L.25, L.35 reviews in the series' earlier *Basic Kanji Book* volumes for the general compound-word-
structure rules.)

### 和語形容詞 (native adjectives) underlie 漢語ナ形容詞 (Sino-Japanese na-adjective) families — Lesson 4 §①
A native Japanese i-adjective frequently has a whole family of Sino-Japanese na-adjectives built on
its semantic root, each adding its own nuance: 重い→重大な/重要な/厳重な/貴重な; 軽い→軽薄な/軽率な/
軽快な/軽妙な; 等しい→平等な/均等な/高等な/対等の(noun-type); 強い→強力な/強硬な/強情な/強引な;
弱い→軟弱な/薄弱な/貧弱な/病弱な; 正しい→正確な/正常な/正当な/正式の(noun-type); 幸せ→幸福な/幸運
な/不幸な/薄幸の(noun-type); 健やか→健康な/健全な; 確か→確実な/正確な/明確な/的確な.

### Three sentence positions of ナ形容詞 and a bare-stem fixed-compound exception — Lesson 4 §②
Na-adjectives take three distinct surface forms depending on syntactic position: pre-nominal な
(熱心な先生), pre-verbal に (熱心に教える; some, like 有名/危険/重大/貧乏, combine ONLY with なる/
する in this position), and sentence-final だ/である (あの先生は熱心だ). A subset of na-adjectives
can instead attach directly to a following noun in their bare stem (no な, no の) to form a fixed
Sino-Japanese compound whose meaning can differ from the な- or の-forms of the same adjective:
自由な生活 ("a free life") vs. 自由の女神 ("the Statue of Liberty") vs. 自由貿易 ("free trade," ×
自由の貿易); similarly 平和な世界 vs. 平和の祭典 vs. 平和公園 (×平和な公園); 健康食品 uses neither な
nor の (meaning "food for health," not "healthy food" literally).

### Nominalizing Sino-Japanese na-adjectives: bare, さ, or 性 — Lesson 4 §③
Three distinct noun-forming strategies exist for the same adjective stem, and native-speaker
acceptability is lexically idiosyncratic rather than fully predictable — the book marks disputed
judgments with △ per item rather than giving a blanket rule: (a) used bare as a noun (自由を実現する
"to achieve freedom" is accepted, but ×自由さ and ×自由性 are both rejected for some speakers while
△自由さ is accepted by others); (b) with さ added (a degree/intensity noun: 熱心さ "enthusiasm" is
accepted but ×熱心性 is universally rejected); (c) with 性 added (an abstract-quality/nature noun:
可能性 "possibility" is accepted but ×可能さ is rejected). 的な is the standard suffix for forming a
na-adjective from a noun (機械的な "mechanical"), but Sino-Japanese na-adjectives themselves do not
normally take 的 — 健康的な and 平和的な are flagged as exceptions to that general restriction.
