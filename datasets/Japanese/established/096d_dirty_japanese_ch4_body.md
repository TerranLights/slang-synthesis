# Japanese — Established Vocabulary: Dirty Japanese, Chapter 4 "Body Japanese" (Karada ni Kansuru Nihongo)

**Source:** Matt Fargo (illus. Lindsay Mack), *Dirty Japanese: Everyday Slang from "What's Up?" to
"F*%# Off!"* (Ulysses Press, ISBN 978-1-56975-565-5), Chapter 4 "Body Japanese" (体に関する日本語),
printed pages 57–72 (DJVU pages 65–80). FLAGSHIP slang dictionary source.

**Coverage note:** DJVU file has no text layer (vision-read page by page). Page-numbering offset
verified directly: printed p.56 = DJVU page 64 (end of Chapter 3, the "Coke" entry); printed p.57
= DJVU page 65 ("CHAPTER 4 / BODY JAPANESE" title page); printed p.73 = DJVU page 81 ("CHAPTER 5 /
HORNY JAPANESE" title page), confirming Chapter 4 runs exactly DJVU 65–80 with no overlap. This
chapter is structured mostly as "HE/SHE IS/HAS..." fill-in-the-blank descriptor lists (body-ideal
and body-ugly adjectives) plus several full headword sections ("Coke" tail end excluded as out of
scope, "Piss," "Shit," "Farts," "Snot/boogers/etc.," "Sickness") with illustrative example-sentence
trios. Every distinct descriptor and every bolded slang term embedded in an example sentence is
captured below as its own row; purely grammatical connector fragments (e.g. "kare/kanojo wa..." =
"he/she is/has...") are not tabulated since they carry no lexical content of their own — only the
slang words that fill those templates are extracted. As flagged in the extraction brief, this
chapter has a notable concentration of blunt/taboo anatomical and bodily-function terms (piss,
shit, farts, snot, earwax, eyegunk, pus, vomit); these are tagged `taboo` per the book's own framing,
while milder body-descriptor slang (ugly/fat/unhip insults, fashion/attractiveness slang) is tagged
`colloquial` or `slang` as appropriate. No handwritten marginalia was found anywhere in this
chapter — all content is printed source material.
