## Grammar points

### (1) 受身 (Passive) section

#### 41. 受身
Full Group I/II/III passive-conjugation table given, then four distinct senses by use-case:
- **(A) direct passive** — "be 〜ed <when an action is directed at another person>." Pattern: A は
  B を [verb]。→ B は A に [verb-passive]。私は先生にほめられました ("I was praised by the
  teacher").
- **(B) possessor's passive (迷惑の受身 variant)** — "be 〜ed <something is done to an object
  belonging to someone else, and they feel annoyed/troubled by it>." Pattern: A は B の物を
  [verb]。→ B は A に物を [verb-passive]。田中さんはどろぼうにさいふをとられました ("Mr./Ms. Tanaka
  had their wallet stolen by a thief" — note the possessor, not the object, is the passive
  sentence's subject).
- **(C) non-personal/impersonal passive (非情の受身)** — "be 〜ed <when it is not necessary to
  mention the person who does/did the action>," typically for institutional/historical events: この
  ホテルは60年前に建てられました ("this hotel was built 60 years ago").
- **(D) adversity passive (迷惑の受身)** — "be 〜ed <used to express an undesirable feeling caused
  by some event or someone else's action>," classically with intransitive verbs where no direct
  "active" counterpart inconveniences the speaker as directly: 私はきのう雨にふられました ("I was
  rained on yesterday [and it was a nuisance]").

### (2) 使役 (Causative) section

#### 42. 使役
Full Group I/II/III causative-conjugation table, plus a structural note distinguishing intransitive-
verb causative sentences (A は B を [causative] — 兄は弟をなかせました, "the older brother made the
younger brother cry") from transitive-verb causative sentences (A は B に 物/こと を [causative] —
先生はせいとに本を読ませました, "the teacher made the student read the book"). Three senses:
- **(A) coercion** — "make 〜 to 〜." 先生は悪いことをしたせいとを立たせました ("the teacher made the
  student who misbehaved stand").
- **(B) permission** — "allow 〜 to 〜." ちょっとこの電話を使わせてください ("please let me use this
  phone for a moment").
- **(C) causation** — "make 〜 to 〜 <non-volitional emotional causation>." 病気になって、りょうしん
  をしんぱいさせてしまいました ("I got sick and [unintentionally] made my parents worry").

### (3) 使役受身 (Causative passive) section

#### 43. 使役受身
Full Group I/II/III causative-passive conjugation table, with the note that (except for 〜す-ending
Group I verbs) Group I verbs may also form the causative passive with the contracted 〜される ending
(e.g. 走らせられる = 走らされる, but ×話さされる is not valid for す-stem verbs). "Be made/forced to do
something." Structural pattern distinguished for intransitive (A は B を [causative]。→ B は A に
[causative-passive]。先生はせいとを走らせる→せいとは先生に走らせられる) and transitive verbs (A は B
に 物 を [causative]。→ B は A に 物 を [causative-passive]。母は私にピアノを習わせた→私は母にピアノを
習わせられた). 子どものとき、私は母にじゅくに行かせられました ("as a child, I was made to go to cram
school by my mother").

### (4) 可能 (Expressions of potential) section

#### 44. 可能
Full Group I/II/III potential-form conjugation table. Two senses: (A) "can 〜, be able to 〜
<ability>" (山田さんはピアノがひけます — "Mr./Ms. Yamada can play the piano"); (B) "can 〜, be
possible to 〜 <possibility>" (その映画館で日本の古い映画が見られます — "old Japanese films can be
seen at that theater"). Notes a particle shift: 日本語を話します ("speak Japanese," を) becomes
日本語が話せます ("can speak Japanese," が) in the potential form; destination verbs similarly keep
へ/に but add potential marking (病院へ/に行きます→病院へ/に行けます); verbs of riding/boarding take
に even in potential form (オートバイに乗ります→オートバイに乗れます).

#### 45. 〜（こと）が できる
Same two senses as item 44 — (A) ability: 山田さんはピアノをひくことができます; (B) possibility: その
映画館で日本の古い映画を見ることができます — but formed periphrastically with V dictionary form/N +
ことが/が + できる rather than via verb-internal potential conjugation. Functions as a more
formal/written-register paraphrase of the same meaning as item 44.

#### 46. みえる・きこえる
"can be seen, be visible" (見える) / "can be heard, be audible" (聞こえる) — intransitive verbs of
involuntary perception, paired with が rather than を (海を見ます "I look at the sea" [volitional]
vs. 海が見えます "the sea is visible" [involuntary]; similarly 音楽を聞きます vs. 音楽が聞こえます).
Explicitly distinguished from the potential form 見られる: 見られる indicates that one's intention of
seeing can be realized (deliberately looking and being able to), while 見える indicates an object
simply comes into one's field of vision without intent — illustrated with a contrast pair: そのどう
ぶつえんでめずらしい動物が見られます (○, deliberately visiting a zoo to see a rare animal) vs. ×見えます
(✕, wrong register here); conversely へやのまどから古くてきたない工場が見えます (○, an unsightly
factory simply visible from a window, unintended) vs. ×見られます (✕).

### (5) 授受表現 (Giving and receiving) section

Opens with an overview table mapping give/receive verb choice to social register: giving upward
(目上の人/senior person) uses さしあげる; giving to equals/named third parties (Aさん・Bさん) uses
あげる; giving downward (下の人/junior person, animals, plants) uses やる. Receiving from a senior
person uses いただく; receiving from an equal or junior person uses もらう. "Give to me" uses くださる
(from a senior person) or くれる (from an equal/junior person).

#### 47. くれる・あげる
- **くれる** — "give (something to me, my side, my friends, etc.)." Aさんは {私/私の家族・友だち} に
  somethingをくれる。田中さんは私に花をくれました ("Mr./Ms. Tanaka gave me flowers").
- **あげる** — "give [to someone other than the speaker/the speaker's in-group]." Aさんは Bさんに
  somethingをあげる (never ×Aさんは私はあげる — あげる cannot take the speaker as the recipient,
  that's くれる's job). 田中さんは大川さんに花をあげました.

#### 48. くださる
"give (something to me...) <respectful form of くれる>" — cross-referenced to item 92 (特別な尊敬語,
special respectful-verb forms). Senior person は（私に）somethingをくださる — contrasted with the
plain-register くれる used for an Aさん/junior-person giver. そつぎょうするとき、先生は私にこの本を
くださいました ("when I graduated, my teacher gave me this book").

#### 49. さしあげる・やる
- **さしあげる** — "give <humble form of あげる>," cross-referenced to item 95 (特別な謙譲語). 私は
  senior personにsomethingをさしあげる — contrasted with plain あげる used between equals/named
  third parties. おきゃくさまにプレゼントをさしあげます ("I give the customer a present").
- **やる** — "give [to a junior person, or to animals/plants]." 私/Aさんは{junior person/animals,
  plants}にsomethingをやる — contrasted with あげる used between equals. 子どもにおもちゃをやります;
  さっきねこに魚をやりました; 毎朝花に水をやってください.

#### 50. いただく・もらう
- **いただく** — "receive <humble form of もらう>," cross-referenced to item 95. 私はsenior person
  に/からsomethingをいただく。ぶちょうに旅行のおみやげをいただきました ("I received a travel
  souvenir from the department head").
- **もらう** — "receive [from an equal or junior person]." 私はBさん/junior personに/から
  somethingをもらう。友だちに旅行のおみやげをもらいました. A worked equivalence box clarifies the
  give/receive symmetry: 私はAさんに〜をもらいます = Aさんは私に〜をくれます (valid); Bさんは
  Aさんに〜をもらいます = Aさんは Bさんに〜をあげます (valid); but ×Bさんは私に〜をもらいます is
  invalid — the giver can never be marked with に when the speaker is the recipient in a もらう
  sentence; it must instead be rephrased as 私はBさんに〜をあげます.

#### 51. 〜てくださる／くれる
"something is done (for me, my side, my friends, etc.)." V て-form + くださる (respectful,
cross-referenced implicitly to the くださる/くれる register pairing from item 48) or くれる (plain).
先生が（私に）本を貸してくださいました ("the teacher kindly lent me a book"); 友だちが（私に）本を貸し
てくれました (same meaning, plain register, equal-status giver).

#### 52. 〜ていただく／もらう
"receive a favour (from someone)." V て-form + いただく (respectful) or もらう (plain) — the
benefactive counterpart of もらう/いただく applied to an action rather than an object. 先生にきっぷの
よやくのしかたを教えていただきました ("I had the teacher kindly teach me how to reserve a ticket");
友だちにきっぷのよやくのしかたを教えてもらいました (same, plain register).

#### 53. 〜てさしあげる／あげる／やる
"do something (for someone)." V て-form + さしあげる (to a senior person) / あげる (to an equal) /
やる (to a junior person or animal/plant) — the benefactive counterpart of 49's object-giving verbs,
applied to actions. 新しいカメラで、先生の写真をとってさしあげました; 友だちの写真をとってあげました;
弟の写真をとってやりました. A structural note covers three object-marking patterns for the benefactive
くれる construction specifically: person を〜 (Aさんは私をくうこうまで送ってくれました, "Mr./Ms. A
saw me off to the airport"); person に something を〜 (Aさんは私に英語を教えてくれました); person の
something を〜 (Aさんは私の仕事をてつだってくれました, "A helped with my work").
