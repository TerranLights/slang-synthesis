## Grammar points

### 1 〜いかん (~ikan)
Meaning: "depending on how ~ is / according to ~" — expresses that an outcome depends on the state or nature of something. Connects to `[noun (-の)] + いかん`. Example (p.2): 試験の結果いかんでは、卒業できないこともある — "Depending on the exam results, graduation may not happen." Noted as mainly used in formal/ceremonial registers. Two fixed idiomatic extensions are given: いかんともしがたい ("regrettably, nothing can be done") and いかんせん ("regrettably... there's no help for it").

### 2 〜いかんによらず／〜いかんにかかわらず／〜いかんを問わず
Meaning: "regardless of how ~ is" — a negating extension of point 1's いかん, meaning the outcome does *not* depend on the stated factor. Connects to `[noun-の] + いかんによらず`. Example (p.3): 理由のいかんによらず、殺人は許されないことだ — "Regardless of the reason, murder is unforgivable." Also noted as a formal-register expression.

### 3 〜(よ)うが／〜(よ)うと
Meaning: "even if ~ / no matter whether ~" — concessive, built on the volitional form. Connects to `[verb-volitional / i-adj-かろう / na-adj-だろう] + が`. Example (p.3): 周囲がいかに反対しようが、自分でやると決めたことは最後までやりぬくつもりだ — "No matter how much people around me object, I intend to see through what I've decided to do myself."

### 4 〜(よ)うが〜まいが／〜(よ)うと〜まいと
Meaning: "whether ~ or not" — doubles the same verb (volitional + negative-volitional/dictionary form) to express total indifference to which of two outcomes occurs. Example (p.4): 雨が降ろうが降るまいが、わたしは出かけます — "Whether it rains or not, I'm going out." Note: for Group II/III verbs, the negative half can take the ない-form instead of まい.

### 5 〜(よ)うにも〜ない
Meaning: "even if one tries to ~, one can't" — expresses an attempted action thwarted by circumstance. Connects to `[verb-volitional] + にも + [verb-ない form] + ない` (the second verb, when repeated, takes its potential form). Example (p.4): お金がなくて、新しいパソコンを買おうにも買えない — "I have no money, so even if I wanted to buy a new computer, I couldn't."

### 6 〜かぎりだ
Meaning: "extremely ~ / terribly ~" — expresses the speaker's own strong emotion. Connects to `[i-adj-い / na-adj-な / noun-の] + かぎりだ`. Example (p.5): 山道で日が暮れて、心細いかぎりでした — "Night fell on the mountain path, and I felt utterly uneasy." Explicitly marked as expressing the speaker's own feelings (not describing a third party).

### 7 〜がてら
Meaning: "while also ~ing / on the occasion of ~ing" — doing a second thing incidentally while performing a primary action. Connects to `[verb-ます stem / noun] + がてら`. Example (p.11): 散歩がてら、たばこを買って来よう — "I'll go buy cigarettes while out on my walk." Related forms cross-referenced: No.48 かたがた, 〜を兼ねて.

### 8 〜が早いか
Meaning: "no sooner than ~ / the instant ~" — one action is immediately followed by another, or something happens at that exact moment. Connects to `[verb-dictionary/た form] + が早いか`. Example (p.11): わたしの顔を見るが早いか、彼は性急にしゃべりはじめた — "The instant he saw my face, he began speaking hastily." Related forms: No.32 〜なり, No.43 〜や／〜や否や, 〜たとたん(に).

### 9 〜きらいがある
Meaning: "tends to ~ / has a tendency to ~" — usually describing an undesirable tendency. Connects to `[verb-dictionary form / noun-の] + きらいがある`. Example (p.12): 彼は最近、どうも飲み過ぎのきらいがある — "He's had a tendency to drink too much lately."

### 10 〜ずにはすまない
Meaning: "can't get away without ~ing / things won't be settled unless ~" — from a sense of one's own obligation, social norms, or the facts of the situation, something must be done or the matter won't be resolved. Connects to `[verb-ない form] + ずにはすまない`.

### 13 ただ〜のみ
Meaning: "only ~ / nothing but ~" — a literary-flavored restriction. Connects to `ただ + [verb-dict / i-adj-い / noun] + のみ`. Example (p.19): 今はただ事故にあった方々の無事を祈るのみです — "Right now all I can do is pray for the safety of those in the accident."

### 14 ただ〜のみならず
Meaning: "not only ~ (but also)" — literary register; often paired with "A のみならず B(まで)(も)" where A and B are contrastive, parallel, or similar content, and ただ intensifies it. Connects to `ただ + [verb/i-adj/na-adj/noun plain form] + のみならず` (na-adj's だ becomes である; noun's だ may drop or become である). Example (p.19–20): 富士山はただ高いのみならず、姿も美しいので、日本の象徴として愛されている — "Mt. Fuji is not only tall but also beautifully shaped, which is why it's beloved as a symbol of Japan."

### 15 〜たところで
Meaning: "even if ~ (it won't help) / no matter if ~" — a concessive hypothetical expressing the speaker's judgment that the hypothesized content is pointless, ineffective, or won't affect the outcome much. Connects to `[verb-た form] + ところで`. Example (p.20): 今さらぐちを言ったところで、どうにもならない — "Complaining about it now won't change anything."

### 16 〜っぱなし
Meaning: "left ~ / stays ~ continuously" — with intransitive verbs, means an unchanging state persists; with transitive verbs, usually implies the speaker's dissatisfaction or criticism that nothing was done afterward. Connects to `[verb-ます stem] + っぱなし`. Example (p.21): 新幹線が込んで、大阪から東京までずっと立ちっぱなしだった — "The bullet train was so crowded I had to stand the whole way from Osaka to Tokyo."

### 19 〜ではあるまいし／〜じゃあるまいし
Meaning: "since it's not the case that ~ (so...)" — gives a reason by negating an implied premise, often rhetorically. Connects to `[verb-dict/た form + の/ん; noun] + ではあるまいし`. Example (p.28): 冬山登山をするのではあるまいし、大げさな格好は要りません — "It's not like we're climbing a winter mountain, so there's no need for such an elaborate outfit." Note: じゃあるまいし is explicitly marked as the spoken-register variant.

### 20 〜とあって
Meaning: "because ~ / given that ~" — states a reason, often for an unusual or noteworthy situation. Connects to `[verb/i-adj/na-adj/noun plain form] + とあって` (na-adj/noun's だ is usually dropped). Example (p.28): 人気スターがやって来るとあって、大勢の人たちが待ち受けていた — "Because a popular star was coming, a large crowd was waiting."

### 25 〜ときたら
Meaning: "speaking of ~ / when it comes to ~" — raises a topic, usually followed by a critical or negative comment. Connects to `[noun] + ときたら`. Example (p.37): 隣の犬ときたら、人が通る度にキャンキャンほえて、うるさくてたまらない — "That dog next door — it yaps every single time someone walks by, it's unbearable."

### 26 〜ところを
Meaning: "at the moment when ~ / in the midst of ~ (despite)" — marks a situation or moment, often one that is then interrupted, or one during which a polite request/thanks is being expressed. Connects to `[verb-dict/た form; i-adj-い; na-adj-な; noun-の] + ところを`. Example (p.37): その試合は、もう少しで終わるところを、雨で中断された — "The match, just about to end, was interrupted by rain." Also used in set polite phrases: お忙しいところをおいでくださいまして — "Thank you for coming despite being busy."

### 34 〜にあって
Meaning: "at / in (a time, opportunity, place, situation)" — a stiff/formal way of singling out a time, opportunity, location, or circumstance. Connects to `[noun] + にあって`. Example (p.48): 彼女は戦時中、思想統制下にあってなお、自由な精神を持ち続けた — "Even under wartime thought-control, she continued to hold an independent spirit."

### 35 〜に至る／〜に至るまで／〜に至って(は)／〜に至っても
Meaning: "to reach the point of ~ / even up to ~ / even when it comes to ~" — indicates an endpoint, result, or extent reached. Connects to `[verb-dict form / noun] + に至る`. Example (p.49): 兄が起こした会社は発展を続け、海外に支店を出すに至った — "The company my brother founded kept growing, to the point of opening branches overseas." A fixed idiom is also given: ことここに至っては ("things having come to this point") — 事已至此, "nothing more can be done about it now."

### 36 〜にかかわる
Meaning: "relates to ~ / has a bearing on ~" — connects something to an effect or consequence, often a serious one (life, honor, reputation). Connects to `[noun] + にかかわる`.

### 37 〜にたえない
Meaning: two distinct senses. (A) "can't bear to ~ / can't stand ~" with `[verb-dict form] + にたえない` — e.g. ああいう陰口は聞くにたえません, "I can't stand to listen to that kind of backbiting." (B) "feel ~ very strongly" with `[noun] + にたえない`, an expression of deep, formal gratitude or sympathy — e.g. いろいろお世話になりまして、感謝にたえません, "I am deeply grateful for all your help" (roughly "unable to bear [containing] my gratitude").

### 38 〜にたえる
Meaning: "is (just barely) worthy of ~ / can withstand ~" — the positive counterpart to 37A, meaning something reaches a standard good enough to bear scrutiny or use. Connects to `[verb-dict form / noun] + にたえる`. Example (p.55): 趣味で始めた焼き物だが、ようやく市販するにたえる作品ができるようになった — "The pottery that began as a hobby has finally reached the point of being marketable."

### 43 〜や／〜や否や
Meaning: "as soon as ~ / almost simultaneously with ~" — one event is immediately followed by (or coincides with) another. Connects to `[verb-dictionary form] + や`. Example (p.63): エレベーターのドアが開くや、猫が飛び出してきた — "The instant the elevator door opened, a cat jumped out." Related forms cross-referenced: No.8 〜が早いか, No.32 〜なり, 〜たとたん(に).

### 44 〜ゆえ(に)／〜ゆえの
Meaning: "because of ~ / for the reason that ~" — a literary-register causal expression. Connects to `[verb/i-adj/na-adj/noun noun-modifying form] + ゆえ` (na-adj's な and noun's の may sometimes be dropped). Explicitly marked as 文語体 (literary style).

### 46 〜んばかりだ／〜んばかりに／〜んばかりの
Meaning: "as if about to ~ / looking just about to ~" — describes a state that appears on the verge of happening (without actually happening). Connects to `[verb-ない form] + んばかりだ` (する becomes せんばかり). Example (p.65): 大風が吹いて、街路樹の枝が今にも折れんばかりだ — "A strong wind blew, and the street trees' branches looked as if they were about to snap." Note: んばかりに takes a following verb/adjective/adverb, while んばかりの takes a following noun.
