## Grammar points

### (1) 尊敬語と謙譲語 (Respectful and humble expressions) — overview

Opens with a register-assignment principle rather than a numbered item: **respectful** expressions
mark the subject/agent as (1) the speaker's senior/superior, (2) someone without a close relationship
to the speaker, or (3) someone outside the speaker's in-group (family, company); **humble**
expressions mark the subject/agent as (1) the speaker or (2) someone belonging to the speaker's own
in-group. A key relativity rule is illustrated with a worked company-hierarchy example: when the
speaker (Bさん, a B-company employee) talks to an outsider (Aさん) about Bさん's own superior
(さとう社長), humble expressions are used for the superior's actions despite the superior's normally
higher status — because relative to the external listener, the speaker's entire company (including
the superior) counts as "in-group." Demonstrated with a telephone dialogue: さとう社長、いらっしゃいます
か (caller, respectful) answered with さとうは、今ほかの電話に出ておりますが (B company employee,
humble おります for their own company president, because the listener is external).

### (2) 尊敬語 (Respectful expressions) section

#### 92. 特別な尊敬語
Full special-respectful-word substitution table given — see Vocabulary section above for each
individual verb. General principle: several plain verbs (いきます/きます/います for existence/
motion, たべます/のみます, みます, いいます, します, くれます, ききます/たずねます, しっています) have a
dedicated irregular respectful substitute verb rather than a productive affix. 社長はへやにいらっし
ゃいますか ("is the company president in the room?").

#### 93. お〜になる
Productive respectful-formation pattern for ordinary verbs lacking a special form: お + V
ます-form(stem) + に + なる. Restriction notes: cannot be used for Group III verbs (×お来になります,
×お食事になります — these must use other respectful means) nor for verbs with only one mora before
ます (×お見になります for 見ます, ×おねになります for ねます — again because the お〜になる pattern
needs at least a two-mora stem to attach to). 先生は何時ごろおもどりになりますか ("around what time
will the teacher return?").

#### 94. 〜（ら）れる（尊敬）
Full Group I/II/III respectful-form (〜れる/〜られる) conjugation table — explicitly noted as
identical in form to the regular passive conjugation (item 43's table), disambiguated only by
context. おきゃくさまはもう帰られました ("the guest has already gone home" [respectful]).

### (3) 謙譲語 (Humble expressions) section

#### 95. 特別な謙譲語
Full special-humble-word substitution table given — see Vocabulary section above. General principle
mirrors item 92 but for the speaker's/in-group's own actions: もうす/まいる for はじめまして・ヤンともう
します・アメリカからまいりました ("nice to meet you, my name is Yang, I came from America" — a
canonical self-introduction formula using two special humble verbs together).

#### 96. お／ご〜する
Productive humble-formation pattern: お + V(Group I/II) ます-form(stem) + する, or ご + V(Group III)
dictionary form. Same one-mora-stem restriction as item 93 (×お見します, ×お来します). Notes that a
few Group III verbs irregularly take お rather than ご (お電話する, "to telephone [humbly]" — こんばん
先生にお電話するつもりです). Politeness can be further raised by substituting いたします for します: か
いぎの時間がきまったら、ごれんらくいたします ("once the meeting time is set, I will humbly contact
you").

### (4) 丁寧語 (Polite expressions) section

#### 97. ございます
"<polite form of あります>." お手洗いはあちらにございます ("the restroom is over there"). Can be used
to politely negate availability while redirecting: りんごジュースはございませんが、オレンジジュースなら
ございます ("we don't have apple juice, but we do have orange juice").

#### 98. 〜でございます
"<polite form of です>." お手洗いはあちらでございます; 田中でございます。どうぞよろしくおねがいいたします
("I am [Mr./Ms.] Tanaka — pleased to meet you" — a formulaic polite self-introduction).

#### 99. お〜ございます
"<polite form of い-adjectives>," used predicatively in service/formal registers. A systematic sound
shift applies based on the adjective's final stem vowel before い: 〜ai→〜oo (たかい→おたこうございます),
〜oi→〜oo (ひろい→おひろうございます), 〜ii→〜uu (いそがしい→おいそがしゅうございます; irregular
おおきい→おおきゅうございます), 〜ui→〜oo (あつい→おあつうございます). Two exception classes: (1)
adjectives already beginning with お don't take an additional お prefix (おいしい→おいしゅうございます,
not ×おおいしゅうございます); (2) adjectives describing the speaker's own state don't take お (さびしい
→さびしゅうございます when the speaker describes themselves, not ×おさびしゅうございます — but おさびし
ゅうございますね is correct when sympathizing with someone else's loneliness).

#### 100. お／ご〜ください
"Please do 〜. <polite form of 〜てください>." お + V(Group I/II) ます-form(stem), or ご + V(Group
III) ます-form(stem), + ください. Same one-mora-stem restriction as items 93/96 (×お見ください,
×お来ください). Irregular pairings noted: おいでになる→おいでください (not ×おいでになってください),
ごらんになる→ごらんください. どうぞお入りください ("please come in"); この薬はねる前にお飲みください
("please take this medicine before going to bed").
