# Japanese — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Nihongo Notes 9 & 10 (Situational Japanese 4 & 5)

**Source:** Osamu Mizutani & Nobuko Mizutani, *Nihongo Notes 9: Situational Japanese 4* (The Japan
Times, 1984) and *Nihongo Notes 10: Situational Japanese 5* (The Japan Times, ~1985). Full volumes
(vol. 9: printed pp. 8–154, 74 short situational-grammar essays; vol. 10: printed pp. TBD, essays
to follow). This is the final pair in the 10-volume *Nihongo Notes* series.

**Coverage note:** No text layer in either PDF (`pdftotext` returns empty output); both volumes were
vision-read from rendered page images. Each PDF page image is a two-printed-page spread, not one
page per image (confirmed empirically: image index *i* ≈ book pages `2i-2`/`2i-1` once front matter
is accounted for) — the same gotcha flagged in the extraction spec from the Serbian/Croatian/Bosnian
run. Every grammar essay and every distinct illustrative vocabulary/phrase item is captured; the
series' format (one tightly-scoped situational-usage point per 2-page spread, mini-dialogue +
example sentences + paraphrased explanation) is already close to the project's target information
density, so coverage is close to exhaustive rather than selective. Direct example sentences are
reproduced only as short illustrative quotes (1-2 lines), per copyright discipline — the surrounding
narrative framing and explanatory prose is paraphrased throughout.

**Vision-reading guard note:** Vol. 9's title page (image 1 / recto) carries a handwritten library
date stamp ("NOV 20 1984") in the left margin — clearly a stamp/handwritten addition, not typeset
source content. Excluded entirely, flagged here per the spec's marginalia-guard requirement. Nothing
else handwritten was observed; a stray faint "99" print artifact in one corner of the title page
appears to be pre-print bleed-through/scan noise, not reader marginalia, and was likewise not
extracted as content.
