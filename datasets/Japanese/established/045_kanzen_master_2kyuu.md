## Grammar points

*(Points 1-58, numbered per the source's own numbering. Each entry paraphrases the English
companion's explanation, cross-checked against the Japanese-medium book's example sentences and any
register notes it marks. Example sentences below are drawn/paraphrased from both sources; only 1-3
per point are kept, representative of the book's fuller 2-6-sentence sets.)*

### 1. 〜として(は)／〜としても／〜としての — "as" (capacity/role/function)

Marks a noun as the capacity, role, or function in which something acts. 彼は国費留学生として日本へ
来た "He came to Japan as a government-sponsored student." A second, distinct sense recorded under
the same heading — 〜としても／疑問詞+ひとつ+としても — means "even, granting that," built on a
question-word + counter pattern (e.g. 誰一人として「not even a single person」) and is close in
meaning to 〜と言えども but less formal and requires the question-word pattern. 火事のあと、誰一人と
して生き残っていなかった "After the fire, not even one person survived." A related compound,
としても／にしても ("granting that; even if"), marks that the preceding action was ineffective or
useless: 勉強するとしても、合格できません "Even if I study, I'll still fail."

### 2. 〜とともに — "together with; as; while"

Three sub-senses: (A) two things/people share a property — お正月は家族とともに過ごしたい "I want to
spend New Year's together with family"; (B) two things happen simultaneously — 学生を指導するととも
に、研究にも力を入れている "while guiding students, also putting effort into research"; (C) two
things change in parallel over time — 年をとるとともに、体力が衰える "as one ages, physical strength
declines."

### 3. 〜において(は)／〜においても／〜における — "at; in; during"

Marks the place or time of an action/event; the book flags this as a **very formal** register
marker. 会議は第一会議室において行われる "The meeting is held in Conference Room 1."

### 4. 〜に応じて／〜に応じ／〜に応じた — "in proportion to; according to"

The 〜に応じた form is used only when modifying a following noun. 保険金は被害状況に応じて、払われる
"Insurance payouts are made according to the extent of damage."

### 5. 〜にかわって／〜にかわり — "in place of; on behalf of"

(A) one thing/agent replacing another — 人間にかわってロボットが作業をしている "robots are doing the
work in place of humans"; (B) acting on someone's behalf — 父にかわって、私が結婚式に出席しました "I
attended the wedding on my father's behalf."

### 6. 〜に比べて／〜比べ — "compared to"

兄に比べて、弟はよく勉強する "Compared to his older brother, the younger brother studies a lot."

### 7. 〜にしたがって／〜にしたがい — "as; in proportion to" (gradual co-occurring change)

Describes two things that change spontaneously and gradually in accordance with each other. The
にしたがい variant is flagged as generally used in writing (a stylistic difference only); the
pattern cannot take momentary/punctual verbs. 高く登るにしたがって、見晴らしがよくなった "As one
climbed higher, the view improved."

### 8. 〜につれて／〜につれ — "as; in proportion to; with"

Both halves of the sentence must represent an ongoing change (not static states). 品質がよくなるに
つれて、値段が高くなる "As quality improves, the price rises."

### 9. 〜に対して(は)／〜に対し／〜に対しても／〜に対する — "towards; for; against"

Marks the (usually human) target toward which an action is directed. お客様に対して失礼なことを言っ
てはいけません "You mustn't say rude things toward customers."

### 10. 〜について(は)／〜につき／〜についても／〜についての — "about; concerning"

日本の経済について研究しています "I'm researching Japan's economy."

### 11. 〜にとって(は)／〜にとっても／〜にとっての — "for; from the standpoint of"

この写真は私にとって、何よりも大切なものです "This photo is, for me, more precious than anything
else."

### 12. 〜に伴って／〜に伴い／〜に伴う — "as; with"

(A) accompanying/following a change — 人口が増えるに伴って、いろいろな問題が起こってきた "as the
population grows, various problems have arisen"; (B) happening at the same time — 地震に伴って、
火災が発生することが多い "fires often occur along with earthquakes."

### 13. 〜によって／〜により／〜による／〜によっては — "due to; by; depending on"

Five distinct sub-senses covered: (A) marking the agent of a passive verb — アメリカ大陸はコロンブ
スによって発見された "The Americas were discovered by Columbus"; (B) marking a cause — 不注意に
よって大事故が起こることもある "carelessness can cause major accidents"; (C) marking a means/method
— 問題は話し合いによって解決したほうがいい "problems are better solved through discussion"; (D)
showing variation between things — 習慣は国によって違う "customs vary by country"; (E) marking a
conditional "depends on" — この薬は人によっては副作用が出ることがあります "this medicine may cause
side effects depending on the person."

### 14. 〜によると／〜によれば — "according to" (reported information)

Marks the source of information being relayed secondhand. 天気予報によると、あしたは雨が降るそうで
す "According to the weather forecast, it will rain tomorrow."

### 15. 〜を中心に(して)／〜を中心として — "centered on; focused on"

駅を中心にたくさんの商店が集まっている "Many shops are clustered around (centered on) the station."

### 16. 〜を問わず／〜は問わず — "regardless of"

留学生ならどなたでも国籍、年齢、性別を問わず申し込めます "Any international student may apply
regardless of nationality, age, or gender."

### 17. 〜をはじめ／〜をはじめとする — "~ among other things; not only ~ but also"

Picks out the first/most prominent item in a list, with emphasis on that item. 上野動物園にはパン
ダをはじめ、子供たちに人気がある動物がたくさんいます "Ueno Zoo has many animals popular with
children, pandas among them."

### 18. 〜をもとに／〜をもとにして — "based on"

〜をもとに pairs with a following noun, 〜をもとにして with a following verb. ノンフィクションという
のは事実をもとにして書かれたものです "Non-fiction is written based on facts."

### 19. 〜うえ(に) — "in addition; not only ~ but also"

Introduces an additional, emphatic statement. 彼女は頭がいいうえに、性格もよい "Not only is she
smart, she also has a good personality."

### 20. 〜うちに／〜ないうちに — "while; during; before"

Marks a period of time within which an action occurs; distinguished from 〜の間 (which covers the
whole duration throughout which something happens). 日本にいるうちに、一度京都を訪ねたいと思ってい
ます "While I'm in Japan, I want to visit Kyoto at least once." Also covers "while (background
state continues)": 寒かったが、走っているうちに体が暖かくなった "It was cold, but while running my
body warmed up."

### 21. 〜おかげで／〜おかげだ — "thanks to"

科学技術が発達したおかげで、我々の生活は便利になった "Thanks to technological development, our
lives have become convenient." The set phrase おかげさまで ("thanks to you/fortunately") is marked
as an idiomatic usage (慣用).

### 22. 〜かわりに — "instead of; in place of; to make up for"

Three sub-senses: (A) substituting one thing for another — 私立大学を1つ受けるかわりに、国立大学を
3つ受けたい "instead of applying to one private university, I want to apply to three national
ones"; (B) acting in place of someone — 病気の父のかわりに、わたしが参りました "I came in place of
my sick father"; (C) compensating/trading off — わたしが料理するかわりに、あなたは掃除してください
"I'll cook, so in exchange please clean."

### 23. 〜くらい／〜ぐらい／〜くらいだ／〜ぐらいだ — "to the extent that" (degree)

棚から物が落ちるくらい大きい地震があった "There was an earthquake big enough that things fell off
shelves."

### 24. 〜最中に／〜最中だ — "in the middle of; during"

考えている最中に、話しかけられて困った "I was troubled by being spoken to right in the middle of
thinking something through."

### 25. 〜次第 — "as soon as" (prerequisite-then-action)

Shows that completing one action is the prerequisite for another. 新しい住所が決まり次第、連絡しま
す "I'll contact you as soon as the new address is decided." (A different 次第 sense, "depends on,"
is covered separately as point 162.)

### 26. 〜せいだ／〜せいで／〜せいか — "because of; (someone's/something's) fault"

Expresses the cause of an undesirable result. 〜せいで marks an undesirable result outside the
speaker's control; 〜せいか can mark either a desirable or undesirable result. 電車が遅れたせいで、
遅刻した "Because the train was late, I was late (to something)."

### 27. 〜たととん(に) — "the moment; just as"

The instant one thing happened, something else immediately followed. 犯人は警官の姿を見たとたん、
逃げ出した "The moment the culprit saw the police officer, he ran off."

### 28. 〜たび(に) — "each time; whenever"

わたしは旅行のたびに、絵葉書を買います "Every time I travel, I buy a postcard."

### 29. 〜て以来 — "ever since ~ (and still ongoing)"

日本に来て以来、日本に対する考え方が少しずつ変わってきた "Ever since coming to Japan, my way of
thinking about it has gradually changed."

### 30. 〜とおり(に)／〜どおり(に) — "in the way that; as"

説明書に書いてあるとおりにやってみてください "Please try it the way it's written in the
instructions." Also covers set-expectation usage: 予想どおり、Aチームが優勝した "As expected, Team A
won."

### 31. 〜ところに／〜ところへ／〜ところを — "just when; right as"

When someone did something, a consequence immediately followed as a result. Particle choice
matters: を makes it the verb's grammatical object, へ pairs with 来る for movement toward a place,
and に parallels 〜の時に. こっそりたばこを吸っているところを、妹に見られた "I was caught by my
younger sister right as I was secretly smoking."

### 32. 〜ほど／〜ほどだ／〜ほどの — "to the extent that" / superlative-by-negation

(A) degree/extent — 会場にはあふれるほど、ギターを持った若者が集まっていた "Guitar-carrying young
people gathered to the point of overflowing the venue"; (B) "nothing is as ~ as ~" — 彼女ほど頭の
いい人には会ったことがない "I've never met anyone as smart as her."

### 33. 〜ばかりに — "because of (regrettably)"

Marks regret over the cause of a bad outcome. うそをついたばかりに恋人に嫌われてしまった "Just
because I told a lie, my partner ended up hating me."

### 34. 〜ものだから — "because; so" (unavoidable, excuse-giving reason)

Gives a reason for something negative that was unavoidable, often used to make an excuse. 事故で電
車が遅れたものですから、遅くなってすみません "Sorry for being late — the train was delayed because
of an accident."

### 35. 〜ように／〜ような — "like (simile)" / "so that (purpose)"

(A) simile — ケーキのような甘いものはあまり好きではありません "I don't much like sweet things like
cake"; (B) purpose/goal — 風邪を引かないように気をつけてください "Please be careful so that you
don't catch a cold." Also extends to showing an example, a guess/conjecture (彼は部屋にいないよう
です "it seems he's not in the room"), an indirect order, and change-of-state (自転車に乗れるように
なりました "I've become able to ride a bicycle").

### 36. 〜一方だ — "only keeps (increasing/worsening)"

Shows an increasing tendency; requires a verb of change. 最近、パソコン通信の利用者は増える一方だ
"Lately, the number of internet users just keeps increasing." (A distinct 一方(で) contrastive sense
recurs as point 76.)

### 37. 〜おそれがある — "there is a risk/concern that"

Used for statements about potential undesirable outcomes. 早く手術しないと、手遅れになるおそれがあ
る "If surgery isn't done soon, there's a risk it will be too late."

### 38. 〜ことになっている — "it has been decided/arranged that"

An event occurs irrespective of the speaker's own volition; often expresses permission or
prohibition, similar to 〜してもいい. この寮では、玄関は12時に閉まることになっている "At this
dormitory, the entrance is set to close at 12."

### 39. 〜ことはない — "no need to"

時間は十分あるから、急ぐことはない "There's plenty of time, so no need to hurry."

### 40. 〜しかない — "have no choice but to"

事故で電車が動かないから、歩いて行くしかない "The train isn't running because of an accident, so I
have no choice but to walk."

### 41. 〜ということだ — "I heard that / that means that"

(A) quoting secondhand information — 新聞によると、また地下鉄の運賃が値上げされるということだ "The
paper says subway fares are going up again" (becomes 〜とのこと in letters); (B) "that means that…"
— ご意見がないということは賛成ということですね "No objection means agreement, right?"

### 42. 〜まい／〜まいか — "probably won't / won't" (negative volition/conjecture)

〜まい expresses near-certain negative conjecture or firm negative intention; 〜まいか softens this,
akin to 〜まいかもしれない. こんなまずいレストランへは二度と来るまい "I won't ever come back to such
a bad restaurant."

### 43. 〜わけがない／〜わけはない — "there's no reason why ~ / it's impossible that ~"

Following a negative verb: "there's no reason ~ can't." Following a positive verb: "it's almost
impossible that ~." あんな下手な絵が売れるわけがない "There's no way such a badly-made painting
would sell."

### 44. 〜わけにはいかない／〜わけにもいかない — "mustn't / must" (external-circumstance obligation)

Following a negative verb: can't do something due to external circumstance ("mustn't"). Following a
positive verb: "must." 絶対にほかの人に言わないと約束したので、話すわけにはいかない "I promised
never to tell anyone else, so I can't (mustn't) talk."

### 45. 〜から〜にかけて — "from ~ to ~" (spanning time/place)

昨夜から今朝にかけて雨が降りました "It rained from last night through this morning."

### 46. たとえ〜ても／たとえ〜でも — "even if ~"

たとえ難しくてもやりがいのある仕事をしたい "Even if it's hard, I want work that's worth doing."

### 47. 〜さえ〜ば — "as long as; if only" (conditional)

Used only with conditional clauses (contrast point 60's non-conditional 〜さえ). 住所さえわかれば、
地図で探して行きます "As long as I know the address, I'll find it on a map and go."

### 48. 〜も〜ば〜も／〜も〜なら〜も — "both ~ and ~; neither ~ nor ~"

父はお酒も飲めばタバコも吸うので、健康が心配だ "My father both drinks and smokes, so I worry about
his health."

### 49. 〜やら〜やら — "~ and ~ among other things"

帰国前は飛行機の予約をするやらおみやげを買うやらで忙しい "Before returning home, I'm busy with
things like booking the flight and buying souvenirs."

### 50. 〜だらけ — "littered/covered with (negative)"

このレポートは字が間違いだらけで読みにくい "This report is littered with typos and hard to read."

### 51. 〜っぽい — "-ish; apt to"

(A) visual/felt resemblance — あの黒っぽい服を着た人はだれですか "Who is that person wearing the
blackish clothes?"; (B) behavioral tendency — 彼は怒りっぽいけれど、本当は優しい人です "He's
quick-tempered, but actually a kind person."

### 52. 〜がたい — "difficult/impossible to do"

Flagged as used only in written Japanese, and only with transitive verbs. 彼女がそんなことをすると
は、信じがたい "It's hard to believe she'd do such a thing."

### 53. 〜がちだ／〜がちの — "tend to; liable to" (undesirable tendency over time)

Refers to a general pattern over time (not just the present moment); there's a high probability of
occurrence with an identifiable cause. 母は病気がちなので、あまり働けない "My mother is often sick,
so she can't work much."

### 54. 〜気味 — "a touch of; slight tendency toward" (present state)

Contrasted with がちだ (point 53): 気味 is based on present sensory feeling/perception and describes
only a slight degree, not a general pattern. 仕事が忙しくて、最近少し疲れ気味だ "I've been a little
tired lately from being busy with work."

### 55. 〜げ — "looking like; seeming" (emotion-based impression)

Used with emotions, becomes a な-adjective, works similarly to 〜そうな／〜そうに, and describes an
opinion based on observation. 彼女は悲しげな様子で電話した "She made the call looking sad."

### 56. 〜かけだ／〜かけの／〜かける — "unfinished; half-done"

テーブルの上に飲みかけのコーヒーが置いてある "There's a half-drunk coffee sitting on the table."

### 57. 〜きる／〜きれる／〜きれない — "do completely / be (un)able to finish doing"

42.195キロを走りきるのは大変なことだ "Running the full 42.195 km (a marathon) to the end is no
small feat."

### 58. 〜ぬく — "do thoroughly to the very end"

(A) perseverance from start to finish — 戦争の苦しい時代を生きぬいてきた人たちは、精神的にも強い
"People who lived through the hardships of wartime to the very end are mentally strong too"; (B)
intensifier, "very/とっても" — これは考えぬいて、出した結論です "This is a conclusion reached only
after very thorough thought."
