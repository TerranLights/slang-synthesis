"Serbian–English vocabulary," printed pages 306–309 (PDF pages 163–164 of the 177-page scan).

**Coverage note:** This is the book's back-of-book alphabetical Serbian→English dictionary, extracted
exhaustively (every headword and sub-entry, not a sample). The companion "English–Serbian vocabulary"
(printed pages 318 onward, PDF page 169+) is the reverse of the same word list and is deliberately
**not** extracted, per this project's established precedent for two-way glossaries (redundant content).

**Vision-reading guard:** This source has a garbled/unusable OCR text layer and was vision-read from
150dpi page-image renders of PDF pages 158–175 (buffer around the estimated glossary range). The scan
is two-printed-pages-per-image spreads; offset was verified empirically (PDF page 163 = printed pages
306–307, confirming glossary start; PDF page 168 = printed pages 316–317, confirming glossary end
just before printed page 318 where "English–Serbian vocabulary" begins). **No handwritten marginalia
was found on any page read for this extraction** — all content on pages 306–317 is clean typeset
print, consistent font throughout, no pencil/pen marks, underlines, or margin notes observed.

**Content character note:** This is a plain pedagogical back-of-book glossary (basic core vocabulary
from the course's lessons), not a slang/register-annotated dictionary. It carries **no explicit
dialectal/regional annotation** (no B/C/S-style tags) and **no register labels** — every entry is
`core` usage tier by default; the extraction spec's rule against inferring frequency from lesson
placement or region from publisher/title applies throughout. No taboo/slang/colloquial-tier entries
were found in this glossary (that content, if present in this book at all, would live in the running
