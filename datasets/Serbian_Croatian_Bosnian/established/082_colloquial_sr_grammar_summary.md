# Serbian/Croatian/Bosnian — Established Grammar: Colloquial Serbian, "Grammar summary"

**Source:** Celia Hawkesworth with Jelena Ćalić, *Colloquial Serbian: The Complete Course for
Beginners* (Routledge, 2006), "Grammar summary" section, printed pp. 298–312 (PDF pages 154–161 of
`Colloquial Serbian.pdf`; this PDF is scanned as two-page spreads, one image per PDF page — confirmed
by direct inspection: PDF page 154 shows printed pp. 298–299, PDF page 161 shows printed p. 312 on
its left half and the "Key to exercises" title page (printed p. 313) on its right half). "Key to
exercises" begins at printed p. 313 and is correctly excluded (drill answers, no content value per
the coverage rule).

**Vision-reading guard.** This PDF has no usable text layer for this range — vision-read in full via
`pdftoppm -png -r 150` page-spread images (PDF pages 154–161, each containing two printed pages).
Every element on every page in this range is consistent, cleanly typeset print matching the
surrounding page — headers, running titles, page numbers, tables, and body text all in the same
typeface throughout. **No handwritten marginalia, pencil annotation, underlining, or any other
reader-added mark was found anywhere in the assigned range** — this copy's scan is clean for this
chunk (contrast the sister-book PDF's `established/072`, which did carry a book-internal
photo-facsimile of handwritten Cyrillic, itself correctly identified as printed source content, not
marginalia; no equivalent facsimile occurs in this chunk).

**Non-redundant supplement — scope decision: near-total redundancy confirmed.** This project has
already extracted `established/072`, the "Grammar summary" chapter of Hawkesworth's own earlier
sister volume *Colloquial Croatian and Serbian* (Routledge, 1998) — same author, same "Grammar
summary" structure and section numbering (1 Noun declensions, 2 Adjective declensions, 3 Personal
pronoun declensions, 4 Verb types, 5 Mobile a, 6 Consonant changes, 7 Formation of questions, 8 Other
tenses, 9 Collective numerals), plus `established/023`, `037`–`045`, `046`–`058` (three other
independent reference grammars covering the same core-grammar territory). Checked this chunk
section-by-section against those files, and against a corpus-wide grep for the chunk's most
distinctive terms (`telad`/`teleta`, `momak`/`legendi` mobile-a examples, `dvojica`/`obojica`,
`dvoji`/`petori` distributive paradigms, `aorist`) before writing this file:

- **Skipped as fully redundant — §1 Noun declensions, §2 Adjective declensions, §3 Personal pronoun
  declensions**: full paradigm tables for masculine/neuter/feminine nouns, adjective definite/
  indefinite/soft-stem declension, and personal pronoun declension are already tabulated in
  comparable or greater detail in `established/023` and `038`/`052`. The genitive-plural `-i`
  alternative for feminine nouns in two consonants (`torba` → `torbi`) cross-references this same
  chunk's own §5 and is covered there instead (see below).
- **Skipped as fully redundant — §4 Verb types**: the three-type present-tense-ending system, the
  `-ivati`/`-ovati` → `-uj-` pattern, the `-avati` → `-j-` pattern (with the `pokušavati` retained-stem
  exception), and the `-ći`-infinitive irregular-stem set (`reći`→`reknem`, `pomoći`→`pomognem`,
  `ići`→`idem`, `doći`→`dođem`) are **word-for-word identical in wording and example set** to
  `established/072`'s treatment of the same section from the sister book — confirming this is the
  same author reusing the same "Grammar summary" text nearly verbatim across both books, not
  independent coverage. `established/024`'s Appendix 9 additionally gives a fuller ~500-verb
  classification of the same three-type system. The handful of present-tense model pairs unique to
  this chunk's phrasing (`čitati`→`čitam`; `trčati`→`trčim`, `držati`→`držim`, `stajati`→`stojim`,
  `bojati se`→`bojim se`; `počinjati`→`počinjem`, `ostajati`→`ostajem`; `pisati`→`pišem`,
  `kazati`→`kažem`; `govoriti`→`govorim`; `krenuti`→`krenem`; `uzeti`→`uzmem`; `jesti`→`jedem`;
  `iscrpsti`→`iscrpim` vs. `grepsti`→`grebem`) are ordinary high-frequency verbs whose present-tense
  forms are pedagogically basic and already recoverable from the lesson-chapter vocabulary tables
  (`established/001`–`022`) and the verb-type appendix (`established/024`); they add no new rule, so
  they are not separately re-tabulated here.
- **Skipped as fully redundant — §5 Mobile a**: **this is the single clearest case of duplication
  found in this chunk.** Every example in this chunk's masculine (`momak`/`momka`/`momaka`,
  `pas`/`psa`/`pasa`, `borac`/`borca`/`boraca`), l→o masculine (`ugao`/`uglova`, `posao`/`poslova`),
  neuter (`pismo`/`pisama`, `društvo`/`društava`), feminine (`sestra`/`sestara`,
  `devojka`/`devojaka` — Serbian ekavian spelling of `established/072`'s `djevojka`), and adjective
  (`dobri`/`dobar`, `kratki`/`kratak`, `radosni`/`radostan`, `topli`/`topao`, plus `sav`/`kakav`/
  `takav`/`nikakav`) subsections is the same example set `established/072` already extracted in full
  from the sister book under the same "Mobile a" heading — including the **identical** feminine
  genitive-plural-in-`-i` example (`legenda` → `legendi`), which `established/072` had specifically
  flagged as new/unique-in-corpus at the time it was written. `established/072`'s note that this
  section was "not found anywhere else in this project's corpus" is now superseded: it *is* found
  here too, confirming the two "Grammar summary" chapters are substantially the same text reused
  across editions rather than independent content. Nothing new to add.
- **Skipped as fully redundant — §6 Consonant changes**: the `k/g/h + i → c/z/s`, `k/g/h + e → č/ž/š`,
  palatal-`j` mutation (`p→plj`, `b→blj`, `v→vlj`, `m→mlj`, `t→ć`, `d→đ`, `s→š`, `z→ž`, plus the
  additional `st+j→šć`, `zd+j→žđ` pairs given here), and voiced/unvoiced assimilation (`sladak`→
  `slatko`; `s`/`z` → `š`/`ž` before palatals, `misliti`→`mišljenje`) tables duplicate
  `established/024`, `045`, `047`, `053`, `009` exactly as already noted in `established/072`'s own
  skip list for this identical section.
- **Skipped as fully redundant — §7 Formation of questions** (`zar ne?`, `je li?`/`je l' da?`, `zar` +
  affirmative for surprise): already covered in `established/007`, `048`, `049`, as already noted in
  `established/072`'s skip list for this identical section.
- **Skipped as fully redundant — §8 Other tenses** (aorist, imperfect, pluperfect, future perfect,
  historic present): already covered in `established/041` (fuller formal treatment across verb types)
  and `established/064` (pluperfect/future-perfect drilling), as already noted in `established/072`'s
  skip list. This chunk's aorist example verb (`reći`: `rekoh, reče, reče, rekosmo, rekoste, rekoše`)
  and its `biti`-imperfect forms (`bejah/beh` etc.) are the same forms already available via
  `established/023` and `040`.
- **Skipped as fully redundant — §9 Collective numerals** (neuter collectives `dvoje`/`troje`/etc.,
  the `Desetero putnika je došlo` genitive-taking construction, masculine collective numerals in
  `-ica` — `dvojica`/`trojica`/`obojica` — with the mixed-gender-plural-verb-agreement rule,
  `oboje`-vs.-`obojica` "both," the obsolete oblique-case collective declension, and the distributive
  numeral paradigms `dvoji`/`petori` for pluralia tantum and paired-item nouns): **this is the exact
  content `established/072` itself flagged as its own chunk's single genuinely-new find** ("the
  collective/distributive numeral declension paradigms... go beyond `established/019`/`056`'s
  coverage"). This chunk gives the identical paradigm tables, the identical worked examples
  (`Nama dvoma je govorio`; `Poznajem njena dva brata, obojica su zgodna`; the `dvoji`/`petori`
  five-case table; `Čitali smo istu vest u trojim novinama` vs. `established/072`'s ijekavian
  `Čitali smo istu vijest u trojim novinama` — a one-word ekavian/ijekavian spelling difference, not
  new content), confirming `established/072` already fully captured this material from the sister
  book. Nothing new to add.
- **Neuter noun `-et-`/`-en-` infix footnote** (§1, note 2: "tele... gen. sing. teleta... n. pl.
  telad"): checked and already covered in full, with a fuller systematic treatment (the `-en-`/`-et-`
  contrast, the `drvo` "tree" vs. "wood" lexical split, `dete`/`kći` exceptions), in
  `established/051` §7.4.4.2.

**Net result: no new vocabulary items and no new grammar points were found in this chunk.** Every
section of this "Grammar summary" chapter either duplicates material already captured from four other
reference grammars, or — for §5 (Mobile a) and §9 (Collective numerals) specifically — duplicates
`established/072`'s extraction of the near-identical chapter in this author's own earlier sister
book, word-for-word or with only ekavian/ijekavian spelling variation. This is a legitimate outcome
of the "non-redundant supplement" pattern, not a failure to look: the coverage-rule bar
(genuinely new/deeper material) was checked against explicitly, per section, and not met anywhere in
this chunk.
