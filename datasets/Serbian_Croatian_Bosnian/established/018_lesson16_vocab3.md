**Source:** Ronelle Alexander & Ellen Elias-Bursać, *Bosnian, Croatian, Serbian, a Textbook: With
Exercises and Basic Grammar* (2nd ed., University of Wisconsin Press, 2010), "Šesnaesta lekcija /
Lesson Sixteen," glossary box A3 (both halves), pp. 254-255, plus lexical items drawn directly from
the letter texts on pp. 244-260 (Croatian-specific month names and formulaic letter-writing phrases)
that are not separately glossed by the source but are genuinely new vocabulary in this chunk.

**This is a continuation file.** See `018_lesson16.md` for the full header, coverage note, merge
convention, grammar points, and the B/C/S regional-showcase structural note — all of which apply
equally to the vocabulary below. This file holds glossary box A3 (vocabulary for Ana Đurić's letter
requesting a Serbian-language course in Belgrade, printed in Cyrillic, and the reply from Belgrade),
split out per the extraction spec's "don't let a single file grow unbounded" rule.
