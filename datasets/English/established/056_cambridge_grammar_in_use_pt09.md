# English — Established Vocabulary/Grammar: Cambridge *English Grammar in Use* (Murphy), Part 9 — "~ing and the Infinitive" (Units 52–56)

**Source:** Raymond Murphy, *English Grammar in Use* (2nd edition, Cambridge University Press), UK
edition. Converted via `soffice --headless --convert-to txt`; clean text layer throughout. Covers
Units 52–56: Verb + ~ing (*enjoy doing/stop doing*); Verb + *to...* (*decide to do/forget to do*);
Verb + (object) + *to...* (*I want (you) to do*); Verb + ~ing or *to...* (1) (*remember/regret* etc.);
Verb + ~ing or *to...* (2) (*try/need/help*) — printed pp. 104–113 of the unit text.

**Non-redundant-supplement note.** CMOS Ch. 5 (checked `001`–`011`) classifies gerunds/infinitives
structurally (as noun-equivalents, §5.10) but has no verb-complementation inventory at all — no list
of which matrix verbs select a bare gerund vs. a *to*-infinitive vs. an object-plus-infinitive, and
critically no coverage of the systematic *meaning* differences the same two complement types can
carry after a single verb (*remember doing* vs. *remember to do*, etc.). This is the single densest
concentration of true minimal-pair semantic contrasts in the whole book and all five units are kept
in full. Drill exercises skipped per the coverage rule.

## Grammar points

### Unit 52 — Verb + ~ing (*enjoy doing*/*stop doing* etc.)

The closed gerund-only verb class (see Vocabulary), its negative form (*not ~ing*), the
verb+somebody+~ing extension with its passive counterpart, the optional perfect gerund
(*having done*), and the *that*-clause alternative available after *admit/deny/suggest* specifically.

### Unit 53 — Verb + *to...* (*decide to do*/*forget to do* etc.)

The closed infinitive-only verb class (two subgroups, with the second additionally licensing
continuous/perfect infinitives), the parallel noun+*to...* construction (*a decision to do*), the
*dare* idiosyncrasy, and the question-word+*to...* construction.

### Unit 54 — Verb + (object) + *to...* (*I want (you) to do* etc.)

Three distinct complementation classes built around whether an object is required, optional, or
alternates with a bare gerund (see Vocabulary for all three), plus the *make/let* + bare-infinitive
causative pair and its active/passive *to*-asymmetry.

### Unit 55 — Verb + ~ing or *to...* (1): *remember*/*regret* etc.

States the general heuristic (gerund for simultaneous/prior action, infinitive for a following
action) then works through three genuine minimal-pair verbs where gerund vs. infinitive choice
changes the meaning outright: *remember*, *regret*, *go on* (see Vocabulary) — plus a near-free-
variation class (*begin/start/intend/continue/bother*) with one surface restriction.

### Unit 56 — Verb + ~ing or *to...* (2): *try/need/help*

Three more minimal-pair or flexible-complementation verbs: *try* (attempt vs. experimental test),
*need* (active necessity vs. the passive-force *needs doing* construction — a genuine
form/meaning mismatch worth flagging), and *help* (optional *to*-drop, plus the unrelated
*can't help ~ing* idiom).
