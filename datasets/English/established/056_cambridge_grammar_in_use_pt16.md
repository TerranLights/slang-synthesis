## Grammar points

### Unit 91 — Relative clauses (1): clauses with *who/that/which*

The identifying function of relative clauses, the person/thing relativizer split (*who/that* vs.
*that/which*), and the categorical ban on *what* as an in-clause relativizer (contrasted with its
correct fused-relative function).

### Unit 92 — Relative clauses (2): clauses with or without *who/that/which*

The subject/object-position asymmetry governing relative-pronoun omission (obligatory retention in
subject position, free omission in object position), the resumptive-pronoun ban, and
preposition-stranding at the clause's end with simultaneous pronoun omission.

### Unit 93 — Relative clauses (3): *whose/whom/where*

The possessive relativizer *whose*, the formal/uncommon-in-speech object relativizer *whom* (plus
its fronted-preposition use), the locative relativizer *where*, and two further fixed-head-noun
relativization idioms (*the day/year/time (that)...*, *the reason (that/why)...*) each with their
own omission or connector-choice flexibility.
