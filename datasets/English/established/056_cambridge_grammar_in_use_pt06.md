## Grammar points

### Unit 41 — Passive (1): *is done*/*was done*

Core active/passive contrast (what the subject does vs. what happens to it), the pragmatics of
agent-omission, and the present-simple/past-simple passive paradigm (see Vocabulary).

### Unit 42 — Passive (2): *be/been/being done*

Extends the paradigm across every remaining form: infinitive, perfect infinitive, present/past
perfect, present/past continuous — each given its own active/passive minimal pair.

### Unit 43 — Passive (3)

Four independent extensions: the fixed *was born* idiom, the double-object-verb passive, the gerund
passive (*being done*), and the informal *get*-passive with its "genuine happening/change" semantic
restriction plus its lookalike non-passive idioms (see Vocabulary for all).

### Unit 44 — *It is said that...*/*He is said to...*; *(be) supposed to...*

The impersonal hearsay-attribution construction and its personal-subject paraphrase, plus the
three-way polysemy of *(be) supposed to* — reportive, plan/expectation, and prohibition senses (see
Vocabulary) — a genuine semantic-ambiguity point worth flagging for later conlang slang-mechanics
work (one surface form, three distinct discourse functions).

### Unit 45 — *Have something done*

The causative construction (arranging for someone else's action on your behalf) and its separate,
unrelated adversative sense (something unwelcome simply happens to you), sharing one identical
surface form (see Vocabulary) — plus the informal *get*-variant.
