## Grammar points

### Units 131–135 — Verb + preposition (1)–(5)

Five units' worth of fixed verb-plus-preposition collocations organized around *at/to* (131),
*about/for/of/after* (132), *about/of* (133), *of/for/from/on* (134), and *in/into/with/to/on*
(135). The recurring, genuinely teachable pattern across all five units is the SAME-VERB,
DIFFERENT-PREPOSITION minimal pair carrying a real meaning difference — *shout at/to*, *throw...
at/to*, *look for/after*, *dream about/of*, *hear about/of/from*, *think about/of* (the one
partially-neutralizing case, explicitly flagged as such by the source), *remind...about/of*,
*complain...about/of*, and *warn...of or about/about-only* — contrasted with collocations that are
simply fixed (one verb, one obligatory preposition, no alternative) and a handful of complementation
contrasts where a near-synonym verb takes no preposition at all (*discuss* vs. *talk about*; *pay
[a bill]* vs. *pay for [a purchase]*; *ask [a question]* vs. *ask for [a thing]*).
