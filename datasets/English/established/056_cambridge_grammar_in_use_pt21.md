## Grammar points

### Unit 112 — *Although/though/even though*; *In spite of/despite*

The core concessive-meaning pair expressed through two different complementation classes
(clause-taking *although* vs. noun/gerund-taking *in spite of/despite*), the parallel-structure
contrast with causal *because*/*because of*, and *though*'s distinct additional function as a
clause-final discourse particle alongside its ordinary clause-initial subordinator use.

### Unit 113 — *In case*

The precautionary-preparation sense of *in case* (present or past), its *just in case* hedge, the
central *in case*-vs-*if* minimal-pair distinction (unconditional precaution vs. genuine
conditionality), and the unrelated fixed notice-register idiom *in case of* (a false friend of plain
*in case*).

### Unit 114 — *Unless*; *As long as* and *provided/providing*

The negative-conditional subordinator *unless* and the four-way *as long as/so long as/provided
(that)/providing (that)* conditional-synonym set, both sharing the same present-tense-for-future
restriction already established for other future-subordinate-clause contexts (Unit 25).

### Unit 115 — *As* (reason and time)

The causal sense of *as* ("because"), the simultaneity sense of *as* ("at the same time as," with
intensified *just as*), and the three-way *as*/*when*/*while* contrast — including the genuine
polysemy trap where using *as* with a static situation instead of a dynamic action silently reverts
its meaning to the causal sense.
