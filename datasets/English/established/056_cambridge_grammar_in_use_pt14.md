## Grammar points

### Unit 81 — *A friend of mine*; *my own house*; *on my own*/*by myself*

Three independent constructions: the possessive-pronoun-after-*of* idiom for indefinite possessor
reference, the exclusive-possession *my own* determiner (including its self-performed-action
extension), and the two interchangeable "alone" idioms.

### Unit 82 — *Myself/yourself/themselves* etc.

The coreference-marking (obligatory where subject=object) and emphatic (unrelated, optional-emphasis)
functions of the identical reflexive-pronoun set, the lexically-idiosyncratic class of verbs that
block reflexives outright, and the *-selves*/*each other* reciprocal-vs-reflexive minimal pair.

### Unit 83 — *There...* and *it...*

The existential/anaphoric division of labor between *there* (introduces new referents) and *it*
(refers back to established ones), the unrelated locative-adverb sense of *there*, the obligatory
dummy-subject uses of *it* (extraposition; weather/time/distance), and the worked minimal-pair test
distinguishing all of the above.

### Unit 84 — *Some* and *any*

The core polarity default (positive→*some*, negative→*any*, extended to semantically-negative
contexts), the question-specific refinement (default *any*, with *some* for expected-"yes"
questions and offers/requests), the *if*-licensing extension of *any*, the unrelated free-choice
sense of *any*, and the singular-antecedent/plural-pronoun agreement pattern for the compound
indefinite pronouns.
