## Grammar points

### Unit 124 — *In/at/on* (place) (3)

Four further sets of fixed locational idioms layered on top of the Units 122–123 core system:
institutional-condition *in* vs. role-location *at*, the *at sea*/*in the sea* minimal pair, the
event-occupant *at*-vs-building-itself *in* distinction for named venues, the settlement *in*-
vs-journey-waypoint *at* distinction, and the vehicle-class-conditioned *on*/*in* transport split.

### Unit 125 — *To/at/in/into*

The directional *to* (contrasted with static *in/at*), the *been to* experiential idiom, the
verb-specific *get to*/*arrive in*/*arrive at* exception set, the zero-preposition motion/location
split unique to *home*, and the *into*/*in* entry preposition (with *enter*'s own
redundancy-blocking exception) paired against its opposite *out of*, plus the vehicle-class-specific
boarding idiom *get on/off*.

### Unit 126 — *On/in/at* (other uses)

Five further closed sets of fixed, non-spatial/non-temporal idioms built on *on* (activity-purpose,
broadcast-medium, state, and two discourse-adverbial idioms) and *in* (weather-exposure,
writing-mode, abstract-state/stance), plus the productive *at* + rate/measure-phrase pattern.

### Unit 127 — *By*

Five distinct functions of *by*, several sharing no semantic connection: method/manner (bare-noun
collocations, including the unintentional-happening idiom set contrasted with *on purpose*),
mode-of-transport (with its determiner-triggered switch to *in*/*on*), passive-voice agent-marking
(contrasted with instrument-marking *with*), authorship/creation, simple spatial proximity
("next to"), and margin-of-difference quantification.
