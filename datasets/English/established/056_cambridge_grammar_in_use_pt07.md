## Grammar points

### Unit 46 — Reported speech (1): *He said that...*

Direct vs. reported speech as two ways of relaying an utterance; the backshift-of-tense rule (see
Vocabulary) triggered by the past-tense reporting verb; optional omission of *that*; the past-simple
→ optionally-past-perfect shift within an already-past-anchored report.

### Unit 47 — Reported speech (2)

The still-true exception to backshift (and the reverse case — backshift becomes *obligatory* once
the reported content is known to be no longer true); the *say*/*tell* complementation contrast; the
infinitival construction for reporting imperatives/requests after *tell*/*ask* (and the more
restricted *said to do* variant).
