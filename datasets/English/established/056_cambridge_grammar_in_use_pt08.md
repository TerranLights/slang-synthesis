## Grammar points

### Unit 48 — Questions (1)

Core inversion rule and *do/does/did*-insertion (see Vocabulary), the who/what/which-as-subject
exception that blocks dummy-auxiliary insertion, preposition-stranding at the end of wh-questions,
and negative questions' surprise/agreement-seeking pragmatic function (with its potentially
confusing yes/no answer-polarity rule, and the *why don't we...?* word-order note).

### Unit 49 — Questions (2): *Do you know where...?*; *She asked me where...*

Embedded-question word order (statement order, no dummy *do*), the *if/whether* connector
requirement for embedded yes/no questions, and the same word-order shift applied to reported
questions (combined with Unit 46's tense backshift).

### Unit 50 — Auxiliary verbs (*have/do/can* etc.); *I think so*/*I hope so* etc.

Auxiliary-verb ellipsis to avoid repetition; the *have you?*/*isn't she?* echo-tag of polite interest
or surprise; *so*/*neither* agreement constructions requiring inversion; and the *I think so* family
of stance-plus-*so* substitution constructions with their irregular, verb-specific negative forms
(see Vocabulary).

### Unit 51 — Question tags

Tag formation (auxiliary mirroring the main clause, default polarity-reversal), the
intonation-dependent dual function of the same surface tag (rhetorical agreement-seeking vs. genuine
information request/polite favor-asking), and three fixed exceptions to the general pattern:
*let's...shall we?*, imperative...*will you?*, and the suppletive first-person *aren't I?*.
