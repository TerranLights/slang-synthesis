## Grammar points

### Unit 73 — *The* (2): *School*/*the school*

The role-vs-location article split for institution nouns (see Vocabulary — a systematic semantic
conditioning, not an arbitrary memorized list), plus the unrelated fixed zero-article activity idioms
*bed/work/home* and the *sea*-as-voyage sense.

### Unit 74 — *The* (3): *Children*/*the children*

The default zero-article rule for generic reference (including bare *most* + noun), the minimal-pair
contrast between generic and specific-subset reference on the same noun, and the source's own
acknowledgment that a restrictively-modified noun phrase can still count as generic — an explicitly
gradient, not sharply binary, category boundary.

### Unit 75 — *The* (4): *The giraffe*/*the telephone*/*the piano* etc.; *the* + adjective

Three distinct nominalizing/type-denoting uses of *the*: type/class reference for a singular
countable noun (including the *man* exception), the closed adjective-only nominalized-group class
(*the rich, the homeless*, etc. — always plural, never pluralized, never singular), and the
nationality-adjective collective-reference pattern with its three morphological subtypes.

### Units 76–77 — Names with and without *the* (1) and (2)

A comprehensive proper-noun article system: the zero-article default for most place/person names
(with the official-state-name exception), the obligatory-*the* counter-rule for bodies of water,
plural-form place/people names, and compass regions, and the finer building/institution-naming
conventions (streets/parks zero-article; most other named institutions *the*-requiring;
possessive-apostrophe-named businesses and companies/airlines zero-article) — with the source's own
explicit caveat that this is a general tendency, not an exceptionless rule.

### Unit 78 — Singular and plural

Four distinct form/agreement mismatches: pluralia-tantum paired-part nouns (plural form, plural
agreement, singulative *a pair of*), *-ics* nouns (plural-looking form, singular agreement),
collective nouns (singular form, optional-to-default plural agreement in British English, with
*police* as an obligatory-plural exception), and measure phrases (plural form, obligatory singular
agreement because the amount is conceived as one unit) — plus the suppletive plural *people*.

### Unit 79 — Noun + noun (*a tennis ball*/*a headache* etc.)

The productive noun-compounding construction (modifying noun functions adjectivally, kept singular
regardless of notional plurality), its inconsistent orthography, the container/content minimal-pair
pattern it enables, and the parallel singular-measure-modifier rule for number+unit compounds.

### Unit 80 — *-'s* (*the girl's name*) and *of...* (*the name of the book*)

The default *-'s* (animate possessor) vs. *of*-phrase (inanimate possessor) split, the obligatory-
*of* spatial-part-noun exception, the free-variation zone for organizations/places, the full
*-'s*/*-s'*/irregular-plural apostrophe-placement morphology, and the extension of *-'s* to temporal
nouns and duration-as-possessed-quantity phrases.
