## Grammar points

### §151 — The pronoun/adjective *care*
*Care* "which, which one, who" (interrogative adjective, interrogative pronoun, and relative pronoun)
indicates persons or things previously referred to. Full declension given for masculine and feminine,
singular and plural: N. *care* (pl. *cari* or *care*), G. *al/a/ai/ale cărui(a)* (m.)/*cărei(a)* (f.)/
*căror(a)* (pl., both genders), D. *cărui(a)*/*cărei(a)*/*căror(a)*, A. *pe care* (sg.)/*pe cari* or
*pe care* (pl.). Footnote: although prescriptively *ce* should be used for things and *care* for
persons, this distinction is not consistently observed in actual Romanian usage — a genuinely
important descriptive-vs-prescriptive note, directly relevant to later register/mechanics work.
Footnote 2: the *-a* suffix is added to the G.-D. forms specifically when *căruia* etc. functions as a
substantivized pronoun (standing alone) rather than as an adjective before a noun.

### §152 — Use of *care*
As an interrogative adjective: (a) used for both persons and things. (b) the possessive article
combines with the genitive in singular and plural (*Ale cărui băiat sunt cărţile astea?* "These books
belong to which boy?"). (c) both *care* and *cari* are valid plurals, though *cari* is explicitly
flagged as not accepted in present-day (1953) approved orthography despite still being in live use —
a genuine then-current prescriptive/descriptive split, directly useful for understanding which forms
were already archaizing by the mid-20th century. As interrogative/relative pronoun: (a) the
accusative can drop its preposition *pe* when the object is not a personal one (*Banii cari i-am
primit* "The money which I received" instead of *pe cari i-am primit*) — a further data point for the
differential object marking system already seen with *pe* elsewhere in the book. (b) the possessive
article may likewise combine with the genitive in both numbers. *Care* is accented, in every form, on
the first syllable — a specific prosodic fact worth flagging for any future phonological/stress-
pattern mechanics work.
