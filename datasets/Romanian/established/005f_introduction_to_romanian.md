## Grammar points

### §99 — Phonetic changes in verbs (conjugational morphophonology)
A systematic listing of stress-and-flexion-triggered alternations, functionally overlapping with
§60–61's noun/adjective alternations but tabulated specifically for verb paradigms — directly
relevant to the project's typology focus:
**Vowels**: (a) stressed a→ă unstressed (tac/tăcem). (b) ă→e before a syllable containing e or i
(văd/vezi; cumpăr/să cumpere). (c) â→i before a following i (vând/vinzi). (d) stressed e→ea before a
syllable containing ă (leg/leagă). (e) i→e sometimes when unstressed (vine/venim). (f) stressed o→oa
before a syllable containing ă or e (joc/joacă; pot/poate). (g) stressed o→u unstressed (joc/jucăm).
**Consonants**: (a) d→z before i (cad/cazi; văd/vezi). (b) n or r sometimes fall before i (pun/pui;
sar/sai). (c) s→ş before i (cos/coşi). (d) sc→şt before i (cresc/creşti). (e) şc→şt before i (musc/
muşti). (f) t→ţ before i (port/porţi). This is a fuller, verb-specific restatement of the same
underlying palatalization-before-i process already seen for nouns/adjectives in §60, confirming it's
a general synchronic rule of the language, not an isolated nominal phenomenon.

### §100 — Adverb formation
Romanian has no dedicated adverbial morphology/suffix. (a) Most adjectives can function as adverbs
unchanged when modifying a verb instead of a noun (Vorbeşte frumos "He speaks beautifully"). (b)
Adjectives derived from nouns with the suffix -esc (bărbătesc "manly" from bărbat "man") form
adverbs by changing -esc to -eşte (Vorbeşte bărbăteşte "He speaks in a manly way"; Citi rar şi
bătrâneşte "He read slowly and like an old man"). (c) Many nouns can themselves function as adverbs
modifying a verb (Facu ţara pustie "He made the country [like] a desert" — the book's gloss suggests
the noun here functions almost predicatively/adverbially). (d) Adverb comparison parallels adjective
comparison; adverbial superlatives can be formed with foarte "very" or prea "too" (foarte bine "very
well," prea repede "too quickly").

### §101 — Present indicative of irregular verbs a bea "to drink" and a usca "to dry"
a bea: beau, bei, bea, bem, beţi, beau. a usca: usuc, usuci, usucă, uscăm, uscaţi, usucă.
