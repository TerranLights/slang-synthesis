## Grammar points

### Revision: the four verb conjugations, consolidated — Unit 10, pp. 118–122

The unit walks back through all four conjugations established piecemeal across Units 1, 3, 7, and
9, using a single train-journey dialogue as a running example, and adds two previously-unseen
4th-conjugation verbs that illustrate yet another conjugation subtype beyond the `-esc` type
(`a citi`) and non-`-esc` type (`a dormi`) from Unit 7: **`a hotărî`** ("to decide") takes an
`-ăsc`-pattern present (`hotărăsc, hotărăști, hotărăște, hotărâm, hotărâți, hotărăsc`), and
**`a cobori`** ("to descend") follows yet another pattern (`cobor, cobori, coboară, coborâm,
coborâți, coboară`) — meaning the "4th conjugation" the book has been building up since Unit 7 in
fact has at least three distinct present-tense subtypes, not two. Worth flagging for the mechanics-
analysis phase: Romanian's traditional "four conjugations" undersell the real number of distinct
present-tense inflection patterns once the `-i`-infinitive class is examined closely.
