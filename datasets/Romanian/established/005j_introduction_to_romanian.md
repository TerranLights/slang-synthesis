## Grammar points

### §122 — Cardinal numbers 1–19
Full list captured above as vocabulary. Numbers 11–19 are compound (base + *-sprezece* "on ten,"
cognate with the Slavic-calqued teen-number pattern found across the Balkans) with shortened,
idiomatic "-spce" forms used in ordinary conversation — a genuine register split (formal/written vs.
spoken-colloquial numeral forms) worth flagging for later mechanics analysis.

### §123 — *Un, una* as pronouns
When used pronominally (not as the numeral/article "a/an"), *un/una* decline: masc. N.-A. *unul*
"the one" / *unii* "some"; G.-D. *unuia* / *unora*. Fem. N.-A. *una* / *unele*; G.-D. *uneia* /
*unora*.

### §124 — Declension of *zece* "ten"
*Zece* itself declines when substantivized: N.-A. sg. *zecea* "the ten," pl. *zecile* "the tens";
G.-D. sg. *zecii*, pl. *zecilor*.

### §125 — Use of cardinal numbers
(a) Usually precede the noun (*doi prieteni*, *două case*). (b) Numbers are not declined themselves,
but take *a* before a genitive and *la* before a dative, e.g. *Are o inteligenţă a zece* "He has the
intelligence of ten," *A făcut la zeci de alţii cum mi-a făcut mie* "He has treated tens of others
just as he treated me." (c) *Unu/una* take the article: in compound numbers (*douăzeci şi unul* "21"
m., *douăzeci şi una* f.), and as subject/predicate (*Unul dintre ei a sosit* "One of them arrived").
(d) *Una* is used in arithmetic (*Una cu una fac două* "One and one are two").

### §126 — *Amândoi* and *ambii*, both meaning "both"
Both decline (tables given: *amândoi/amândouă*, G.-D. *amânduror* for both genders; *ambii/ambele*,
G.-D. *ambilor/ambelor*). With *amândoi*, both it and its noun are fully declined (*amânduror
fraţilor* "of/to both brothers"); when substantivized, the G.-D. takes suffix *-a* (*amândurora*).
*Ambii* always precedes its noun, which then stays undeclined (*ambilor fraţi* "of/to both brothers").
A genuine stylistic/register doublet, not a meaning distinction, worth flagging for mechanics
analysis.

### §127 — Comparison of adjectives
Comparative formed analytically with *mai*: (a) equality — *tot atât de*/*tot aşa de ... ca şi*
"just as ... as," e.g. *E tot atât de bogat ca şi dânsa* "He is just as rich as she is." (b)
inferiority — *mai puţin ... decât* "less ... than," or *nu aşa de ... ca*. (c) superiority — *mai
... decât* "more ... than." The superlative is formed by placing the demonstrative article *cel*
(full declension table given: *cel/cei* m., *cea/cele* f., G.-D. *celui/celor*, *celei/celor*)
before the comparative of superiority or inferiority, e.g. *cel mai inteligent* "the most
intelligent," *cel mai puţin inteligent* "the least intelligent." Footnote: an intensive/superlative
sense can also be expressed by reduplicating an adjective or adverb (*Rău, rău baiat* "a very bad
boy," *E tare, tare rău* "he is extremely bad") or prefixing *aşa de* "so" — an expressive-register
alternative to the analytic superlative, worth flagging for later mechanics work as a likely source
of colloquial intensification patterns. Footnote 2: in a series of superlatives, *cel* is used only
once, at the start.

### §128 — Further uses of *cel*
(a) Before a substantivized adjective, e.g. *Cel bogat ajută pe cel sărac* "The rich man helps the
poor man." (b) For emphasis introducing an adjective, e.g. *Alexandru cel Mare* "Alexander the
Great," *Omul cel bun* "The good man." (c) Before the ordinal *dintâi* "first," e.g. *cei dintâi
oameni* "the first men." (d) Before a material noun introduced by a preposition, e.g. *Tava cea de
aramă* "The copper tray." (e) With a number before a noun, for emphasis, e.g. *Cei trei soldaţi s'au
luptat cu viteji-e* "The(se) three soldiers fought bravely." (f) Before an indefinite adjective or
pronoun, where *cei* takes on demonstrative force, e.g. *Cele câteva femei pe care le-am văzut* "The
few women whom I saw," *Cei pe cari i-am văzut* "The ones (those) whom I saw."

### §129 — Present indicative of irregular verbs *a mânca*, *a scrie*, *a sta*, *a rămânea*
Full paradigms given: *a mânca* → mănânc, mănânci, mănâncă, mâncăm, mâncaţi, mănâncă. *a scrie* →
scriu, scrii, scri-e, scri-em, scri-eţi, scriu. *a sta* → stau, stai, stă, stăm, staţi, stau. *a
rămânea* → rămân, rămâi, rămâne, rămânem, rămâneţi, rămân.
