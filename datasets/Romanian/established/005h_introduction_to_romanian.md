## Grammar points

### §109 — Possessive adjectives
Full paradigm tables for masculine and feminine, singular and plural possessor: *meu/mei* "my,"
*tău/tăi* "your," *său/săi* "his/her," *lui* "his" (invariable); *nostru/noştri* "our," *vostru/
voştri* "your (pl.)," *lor* "their" (invariable). Feminine forms: *mea/mele*, *ta/tale*, *sa/sale*,
*noastră/noastre*, *voastră/voastre*, *lor/lor*.

### §110 — Position and use of the possessive adjective
(a) Always placed after the noun it modifies and agrees in gender/number with the thing possessed,
e.g. *fratele meu* "my brother," *casele mele* "my houses." (b) If followed by another adjective,
that adjective takes the demonstrative article *cel*, e.g. *fratele meu cel mare* "my big brother."
(c) With two or more nouns under one possessive, the possessive is placed only after the last noun
and agrees only with it, e.g. *fratele şi sora mea* "my brother and my sister." (d) The possessive
adjective is often replaced by unstressed dative pronoun clitics, e.g. *Mi-a furat cartea* "He stole
my book" (lit. "He stole to-me the book"). (e) *lui* (m.) and *ei* (f.) can replace *său/sa/săi/sale*
to disambiguate possessor gender — *cartea sa* is ambiguous between "his" and "her," but *cartea ei*
= only "her book," *cartea lui* = only "his book." Footnote: *său, săi, sa, sale* are used when the
possessor is the subject of the main clause (reflexive-possessive sense); *lui, ei* when the
possessor is not the subject.

### §111 — Possessive pronoun
Formed by prefixing the possessive-article forms *al/ai* (m.) and *a/ale* (f.) to the possessive
adjective: *al meu* "mine," *ai mei* "mine (pl.)," *a mea* "mine (f.)," *ale mele* "mine (f.pl.),"
and parallel for all persons — *al tău/al său/al lui/al nostru/al vostru/al lor*, etc. Note *a sa/
ale sale* vs. *a ei/ale ei* for "hers," mirroring the §110(e) disambiguation.

### §112 — Uses of the possessive article *al*
Beyond pronoun formation: (a) with ordinal numbers, e.g. *al doilea* "the second," *al treilea* "the
third" (full ordinals deferred to Lesson XXIV). (b) With a noun in the genitive case to indicate
possession, e.g. *Frumoasele cărţi ale profesorului* "The professor's beautiful books." (c) When two
possessors appear, the first already marked by a possessive adjective and the second in the genitive,
*al* precedes the last possessor (usually also preceded by the demonstrative article), e.g. *Cartea
mea şi cea a profesorului* "My book and that of the professor's." (d) With a noun/pronoun in the
genitive after the verb *a fi* "to be," e.g. *Cărţile sunt ale profesorului* "The books are the
professor's," *Cartea aceasta e a cuiva* "This book is someone's." (e) When the possessive adjective
is not immediately preceded by noun+definite article, *al* precedes it, e.g. *un prieten al meu*
"a friend of mine" — though present-day usage, especially with feminine singular nouns, increasingly
allows the article directly after noun+definite article instead, e.g. *Logodnicul al domnişoarei Pop*
"Miss Pop's fiancé."

### §113 — Omission of the possessive article
Omitted immediately after a noun bearing the definite article and immediately followed by a genitive
noun, e.g. *Cartea profesorului* "The professor's book" — but retained if the noun is modified, e.g.
*Cartea interesantă a profesorului* "The professor's interesting book." Footnote: in everyday speech
the form *a* is used regardless of gender or number (a colloquial-register simplification worth
flagging for later mechanics analysis).

### §114 — Agreement of the possessive article
Agrees with the thing possessed (declined or undeclined), not the possessor, e.g. *O carte a
profesorului din clasa a doua* "A book (belonging) to the second-grade professor," *Delegaţi ai
guvernului* "The government's delegates." Note: present-day usage trends toward the article simply
agreeing with the following noun, without regard to the formal rule — another live simplification.

### §115 — Present indicative of irregular verbs *a lua* "to take" and *a şti* "to know"
Full paradigms: *a lua* → iau, iei, ia, luăm, luaţi, iau. *a şti* → ştiu, ştii, ştie, ştim, ştiţi,
ştiu.
