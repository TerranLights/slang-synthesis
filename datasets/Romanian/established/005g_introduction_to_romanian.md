## Grammar points

### §102 — Four conjugations
Romanian has four verb conjugations, identified by the infinitive ending: 1st = -a (a schimba "to
change"); 2nd = -ea (a putea "to be able"); 3rd = -e (a face "to make/do"); 4th = -i (a simţi "to
feel").

### §103 — Present indicative, all four conjugations (regular pattern)
Full paradigms given: a schimba → schimb, schimbi, schimbă, schimbăm, schimbaţi, schimbă. a putea →
pot, poţi/poci, poate, putem, puteţi, poate (footnote: putea has two stems, pot- only under stress —
3 persons sg. + 3rd pl. of pres. indic./subj. and 2nd sg. imperative — and put- elsewhere). a face →
fac, faci, face, facem, faceţi, fac. a simţi → simt, simţi, simte, simţim, simţiţi, simt. a scoborî
(a 4th-conj. verb with an -r- stem) → scobor, scobori, scoboară, scoborîm, scoborîţi, scoboară
(footnote: a small class of 4th-conj. verbs with infinitive stems in -r change stem-final i to î when
stressed). A further footnote clarifies English's three present-tense forms (I change/I am changing/
I do change) all collapse to the single Romanian form; the simple form also serves for questions.
Another footnote: 1st-conj. verbs with a stem in -i, -l, or -r (a apropia, a afla, a intra) add -u in
the 1st sg. present indic./subjunctive (aflu, apropiu, intru).

### §104 — The "-ez" augment in 1st-conjugation verbs
Many 1st-conjugation verbs insert -ez- before the regular ending in the 3 singular persons and 3rd
plural of present indicative, present subjunctive, and the 2nd-sg. imperative. Full paradigm for a
lucra "to work": indic. lucrez, lucrezi, lucrează, lucrăm, lucraţi, lucrează; subj. să lucrez, să
lucrezi, să lucreze, să lucrăm, să lucraţi, să lucreze; imperative lucrează.

### §105 — The "-esc" augment in 4th-conjugation verbs
The exact parallel process for 4th-conjugation verbs, inserting -esc- in the same slots. Paradigm for
a vorbi "to speak": indic. vorbesc, vorbeşti, vorbeşte, vorbim, vorbiţi, vorbesc; subj. să vorbesc, să
vorbeşti, să vorbească, să vorbim, să vorbiţi, să vorbească; imperative vorbeşte. (Verbs taking this
augment are marked "-esc" in the book's own vocabularies going forward — now reflected in this file's
vocabulary table's Notes column.)

### §106 — The "-ăsc" variant for r-stem 4th-conjugation verbs
A small subclass of 4th-conj. verbs with an infinitive stem in -r takes -ăsc- (rather than -esc-) in
the same augmented slots, with stem-i→î under stress. Paradigm for a hotărî "to decide": indic.
hotărăsc, hotărăşti, hotărăşte, hotărîm, hotărîţi, hotărăsc; subj. să hotărăsc, să hotărăşti, să
hotărască, să hotărîm, să hotărîţi, să hotărască; imperative hotărăşte.

### §107 — Conjugation drift and conjugation doublets
A living tendency for 2nd-conjugation verbs to shift toward the 3rd conjugation (a ţinea/a ţine "to
hold," both attested). Many 1st-conjugation verbs have a 4th-conjugation doublet (a prefera/a
preferi "to prefer"); occasionally the two forms diverge semantically (a datora "to owe [money]" vs.
a datori "to have a moral obligation to") but in both examples given, either form may also be used
without a meaning distinction — a genuinely live, not fully lexicalized split.

### §108 — Further uses of the present indicative
Beyond marking an ongoing action or general truth: (a) **historical present** for a vivid narration
of a past event (La 4 iulie 1776 America declară independenţa ei). (b) After a past-tense verb in a
că-clause, to denote an action simultaneous with the main-clause past (Mi-a spus că astăzi nu
lucrăm). (c) For an action/state begun in the past and still ongoing — Romanian present where English
needs a perfect progressive (Sunt în America de douăzeci şi cinci de ani "I've been in this country
for 25 years"; Lucrez aici de trei ani "I've been working here for 3 years") — a durative-aspect
pattern worth flagging for later mechanics analysis. (d) In place of the future: (1) in questions
(Unde ne ducem mâine?); (2) with a promise (Vin la voi mâine); (3) to indicate a decision (Îi scriu
odată ce sunt gata).
