# Russian Slang Corpus — *Dermo!*'s Thematic Chapters (I–X)

**Source:** Edward Topol, *Dermo! The Real Russian Tolstoi Never Used* (Russian Information
Services, 2011). Same physical PDF as `01_dermo_alphabetical_glossary.md` — see that file's header
for full source/vision-reading/offset details (no text layer; printed page = PDF page − 18;
scanned one printed page per image; no handwritten marginalia found anywhere in the book).

**This is research only — nothing here is canon** until worked into actual setting material by
explicit decision.

## Coverage decision for this file (explicit, per this project's auditability convention)

The book's printed pp. 1–123 comprise ten numbered thematic chapters, written as running essayistic
prose interleaved with bolded term/idiom lists and cartoon illustrations — a fundamentally
different format from the closing alphabetical glossaries captured in `01`. Given the volume (over
120 pages of this format) and this project's "comprehensive but not exhaustive" coverage discipline
for prose-embedded vocabulary, coverage across this file is **uneven by design**:

- **Chapters IX ("Body Parts... and What Russians Call Them" / the жопа idiom cluster) and X ("A
  Tour Through the Three Tiers of Russian Profanity")** were read and captured in full, entry by
  entry — these are the book's most systematic, glossary-like thematic chapters (dense idiom lists
  under a single root, minimal narrative padding) and the source of this book's flagship finding:
  the systematic хуй/пизда/ебать/жопа root-radiation system, including the author's own explicit
  "triple-decker profanity" combinatorial demonstration.
- **Chapters I–VIII** (mat/oath structure and folklore; general cursing/insults; drinking slang;
  computer/tech slang; love-making vocabulary; business slang; youth slang/insults) were vision-read
  in full across every page, but are captured here as a **representative selection** rather than an
  exhaustive term-by-term transcription — these chapters are much more narrative/anecdotal in
  format (jokes, cartoons, extended cultural commentary framing each term), so a full transcription
  would mostly capture prose padding rather than new vocabulary density. Each chapter's genuinely
  distinct vocabulary items and its most mechanically/culturally significant idioms are captured
  below; a future non-redundant-supplement pass could deepen any of these eight chapters if a
  specific downstream analysis need arises.

## Cross-cutting patterns worth flagging for Phase 3/mechanics analysis

- **Prefix-driven aspectual/semantic fan-out from one root**: ебать alone yields distinct senses via
  prefixation for "deceive" (наебать/объебать), "strike" (ёбнуть/наебнуть), "annoy/pester"
  (заебать/приебаться/подъебнуть), "provoke conflict" (приёбываться), and pure astonishment-exclamation
  (ебитская сила) — a single root feeding a genuinely large, prefix-organized semantic map, not just
  a handful of synonyms.
- **A closed set of non-taboo "neutral verb repurposed as sex euphemism" items** (иметь, употреблять,
  сделать, жарить, драть) sits alongside the taboo-root system as a separate, milder euphemism
  register for the identical semantic content — useful as a register-substitution model.
- **The author explicitly demonstrates (not just documents) productive multi-root combination** —
  the "triple-decker" p. 123 passage is a rare case in this corpus of a primary source spelling out
  its own generative combination mechanism as pedagogy, rather than this project inferring
  productivity from a list of independently attested forms.
- **The жопа cluster's proverb-shaped idioms cross-pollinate with the хуй root** (На каждую хитрую
  жопу есть хуй с винтом) and register-graded synonym triads (жопа/задница/попа) mirror the
  таboo/mild/neutral synonym-ladder pattern also seen for "thingamajig" (хуёвина/херовина/хреновина)
  in `01` — the same three-tier register-ladder structure recurs across at least two independent
  semantic domains (body-part vocabulary and generic-placeholder nouns), suggesting a general
  productive register-stratification mechanism in this language's slang system worth modeling
  explicitly in the eventual conlang mechanics.
